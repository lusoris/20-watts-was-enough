# 0026 — Test recruited and phase-qualified machinery as one factorial family

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-27

## Context

The clinical-biotechnology audit found four source-domain operations that are
not represented with the same boundary conditions in the current test suite:
induced proximity, sparse cross-correction, transient splice redirection, and
phase-selective chaperoning. Each operation reuses machinery or capability that
already exists, but they act at different boundaries and must not be collapsed
into a generic claim about reuse.

The biological evidence establishes scoped molecular, cellular, animal, or
clinical observations. It does not establish that a digital translation works,
saves energy, or beats conventional systems mechanisms. Conversely, a
synthetic software fixture cannot retest or strengthen the biological claims.

## Decision

1. Retain four source-domain claims and four separate speculative transfer
   claims. Keep their evidence authorities explicit.
2. Route only the transfer claims through specification-only
   [Fixture F-029](../experiments/fixtures/029-clinical-biotechnology-endogenous-machinery.md):
   relation-triggered maintenance, sparse typed correction, reversible latent
   path selection, and phase-qualified preservation and release.
3. Keep the four tracks in one fixture while they share an accounting and
   promotion contract. Split them only when executable runners require
   materially different environments or statistical designs.
4. Require mature systems nulls in every track: explicit queues and garbage
   collection, content-addressed distribution and gossip, routers and
   parameter-efficient updates, and packaging, retry, replication, reload, or
   recompilation.
5. Do not add a principle or architecture candidate. A biological mechanism
   name is not a systems primitive, and a successful implementation must still
   demonstrate a residual advantage at matched complete cost.
6. Keep F-029 at `NO_RESULT` and non-executable until a runner, frozen manifest,
   validation path, and result authority are added separately.

## Consequences

- The project records reusable endogenous machinery as a falsifiable design
  question without borrowing clinical efficacy as AI evidence.
- Compatibility, transport, mediator lifetime, release, off-target effects,
  queueing, recovery, and complete lifecycle cost remain visible.
- A conventional baseline can kill any individual track without invalidating
  the source-domain observation or the other three tracks.
- No energy or performance gain can be claimed until an eligible execution
  reports task-native service, protected failures, resource use, and uncertainty.

## Supersession

Supersede this record if execution shows that the tracks require different
authorities, if deduplication collapses one transfer completely into an existing
fixture, or if a supported residual mechanism merits a candidate-level design.
Adding sources or implementing a smoke slice without changing those boundaries
does not require a new decision.
