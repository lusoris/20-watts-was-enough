# 0025 — Route parasitology through adversarial lifecycle tests

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-27

## Context

The open-world field search identified parasitology as a cross-taxonomy depth
gap. Its literature contains recurring problem classes that resemble existing
project principles—identity masking, conditional commitment, control capture,
population interaction and delayed response—but resemblance alone does not
justify inventing another principle or architecture candidate.

The source biology and the proposed AI translation also carry different
authorities. A biological experiment can support a source-domain observation;
it cannot by itself establish that an engineered translation works, saves
energy, or generalizes beyond its declared system boundary.

## Decision

1. Retain four separate, bounded claim records rather than one broad
   "parasite-inspired" mechanism:
   [C-1569](../research/claims.md#c-1569) for observable-surface versus latent
   identity, [C-1570](../research/claims.md#c-1570) for costed lifecycle
   commitment, [C-1571](../research/claims.md#c-1571) for host/control-plane
   capture, and [C-1572](../research/claims.md#c-1572) for
   intervention-induced competitive release.
2. Route all four records through specification-only
   [Fixture F-028](../experiments/fixtures/028-parasitology-adversarial-lifecycles.md),
   whose complete conventional nulls and failure criteria remain distinct for
   surface rotation, hierarchical commitment, independent control authority,
   and population interaction.
3. Do not add a principle or architecture candidate. Deduplicate each
   translation against existing identity, routing, memory, control, security,
   ecological and robust-control mechanisms before any future promotion.
4. Keep biological evidence, engineering rationale, executable test authority,
   result authority and energy authority separate. F-028 remains `NO_RESULT`,
   non-executable and non-promotional until a later implementation satisfies
   its declared gates.
5. Treat the new EuroSciVoc, DFG and ANZSRC routes as qualified coverage of a
   bounded first pass, not as completion of parasitology or its clinical,
   veterinary, vector, immune and taxonomic breadth.

## Consequences

- Parasitology contributes falsifiable tests without inflating the catalogue
  with synonym principles.
- A future system must beat explicit conventional robustness, hierarchical
  control, security and ecological-control nulls at matched total cost.
- Claims about working systems, energy savings or generality cannot cite the
  source-domain papers as substitutes for an executed AI experiment.
- The unresolved virology and broader parasitology gaps remain visible in the
  field-coverage inventory.

## Supersession

Supersede this record if an executed F-028 implementation supports promotion,
if new evidence collapses or separates one of the four problem classes, or if
the field-routing authority changes. Adding sources or refining a protocol
without changing those boundaries does not require a new decision.
