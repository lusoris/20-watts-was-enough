# 0002 — Evidence precedes major assertions

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-05

## Context

The imported prose mixed biological observations, published engineering
results, and proposed mechanisms. It also promoted illustrative percentages and
an energy ratio to facts without compatible measurements.

## Decision

Every major assertion receives a stable claim ID and one of four evidence
statuses before it enters canonical prose. Quantitative results require explicit
assumptions and boundaries.

## Consequences

- Uncertainty remains visible.
- A source supports only the scope it actually tested.
- Attractive analogies cannot become architecture requirements without a
  falsifiable engineering argument.
