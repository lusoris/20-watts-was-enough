# Code of Conduct

## Expected conduct

Participation in this project requires respectful, evidence-focused
collaboration. Contributors and maintainers should:

- criticize claims, methods, code, and evidence without attacking people;
- state uncertainty, conflicts, corrections, and limits plainly;
- give credit, preserve provenance, and respect licences and privacy;
- make disagreement specific enough to examine or test;
- welcome good-faith questions across disciplines and levels of experience;
  and
- stop or narrow a discussion when it exposes personal, confidential, or
  security-sensitive information.

Scientific disagreement, a negative result, or rejection of a proposal is not
misconduct when expressed in good faith and supported with reasons.

## Unacceptable conduct

The following are not accepted in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, stalking, intimidation, discriminatory abuse, or
  unwanted sexual attention;
- personal insults, sustained disruption, or deliberate humiliation;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- knowingly falsifying evidence, provenance, authorship, results, or conflicts
  of interest;
- pressuring someone to conceal a correction, failed experiment, security
  issue, or licensing problem; or
- using project access, reports, or private material to retaliate against a
  participant.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
published project spaces, and other interactions where someone represents the
project.

For non-confidential concerns, flag the specific interaction to
[`@lusoris`](https://github.com/lusoris). Do not post sensitive evidence merely
to prove a conduct concern. For confidential abuse occurring on GitHub, or a
concern involving the maintainer, use GitHub's platform reporting and blocking
tools. Security vulnerabilities follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), not the
public conduct route.

The maintainer will consider context, severity, pattern, safety, and the need to
preserve evidence. Proportionate actions may include a clarification request,
content edit or removal, warning, temporary participation limit, rejection of
a contribution, or ban from project spaces. Confidential reports and personal
data are shared only as needed to assess and act on the concern.
