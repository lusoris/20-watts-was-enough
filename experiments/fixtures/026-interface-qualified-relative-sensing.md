## Question and hypotheses

The fixture asks one common question: when the same task-relevant history is
presented at different positive multiplicative scales, does a causal relative
representation improve transfer or reduce complete work without hiding an
absolute variable needed for risk or control?

The registered hypotheses are deliberately directional and killable:

1. **H1 — trajectory qualification.** Equal final values or equal peaks do not
   predict low complete-trajectory discrepancy.
2. **H2 — mechanism nonuniqueness.** Several causal loops can realize similar
   relative responses; matched step fit cannot identify the mechanism.
3. **H3 — finite support.** Relative arms help mainly under multiplicative
   shifts inside their positive, unsaturated reference envelope.
4. **H4 — interface dependence.** The sufficient statistic can differ between
   external-input, internal-state, downstream-output and behavior interfaces.
5. **H5 — dual-channel necessity.** A ratio-only representation fails when a
   registered target depends on absolute magnitude.
6. **H6 — lifecycle qualification.** Reference updates, resets, selector
   errors, fallback and communication can erase the apparent savings.

Failure of an absolute-only arm is not evidence for the proposed translation.
The conditional dual-channel arm must beat the strongest applicable ordinary
null under the same information, state, tuning and action authority.

## Systems, scenarios, and tasks

All ten tracks are synthetic, positive-domain causal systems executed in
binary64. Each track has public-development, private-confirmation and hostile-
transfer partitions, but only the public partition may be specified before a
future sealed-seed procedure exists.

Common scenario axes are:

1. background magnitude and held-out multiplicative scale;
2. step, pulse, ramp, stochastic and closed-loop input history;
3. additive offset, noise, quantization, clipping and saturation;
4. reference age, contamination, missingness, reset and cross-channel change;
5. external, internal, downstream and behavioral observation interfaces;
6. relative-defined and absolute-critical task targets; and
7. CPU operations, state bytes, observation bytes, messages, writes, wall time,
   artifact bytes and later measured joules.

No physical concentration, light level, transcript count, receptor, cell,
plant or organism is simulated as a claim about the corresponding biology.
Source-shaped names identify the falsification boundary; every DGP is an
artificial mathematical object.

## Arms, baselines, and strongest nulls

Every track instantiates the applicable subset of this closed arm registry:

1. `A-RAW`: causal model receiving raw absolute observations only.
2. `B-STATIC-DIV`: static divisive normalization with no learned reference.
3. `B-STREAM`: future-free streaming mean/variance normalization.
4. `B-LOG-RATIO`: explicit $\log u_t-\log r_t$ with domain and stale-state
   checks.
5. `B-DIFFERENCE`: additive difference and derivative features.
6. `B-STATE-SPACE`: linear/nonlinear state-space reference estimator.
7. `B-RECURRENT`: compact GRU-style state with the same state and tuning cap.
8. `C-IFFL`: explicit incoherent feed-forward diagnostic mechanism.
9. `C-RECEPTOR-MEMORY`: channel-specific resource-bearing reference state.
10. `C-DUAL`: conditional absolute, ratio, difference, derivative and support
    channels with an abstention/fallback action.
11. `O-STATISTIC`: evaluator-only oracle that receives the true DGP statistic;
    it is a diagnostic ceiling and cannot act or qualify for promotion.

All feature engineering performed outside an arm is counted as that arm's
construction and runtime work. Batch statistics using future samples are
prohibited. If a mature null implements the same effective transition more
simply or cheaply, the proposed mechanism fails.

## Matched budgets and equal information

1. A/B/C arms receive byte-identical initial causal observations, timestamps,
   masks and acquisition menus. The oracle receives hidden state only after
   actions have been frozen for scoring.
2. Maximum observed-history bytes, recurrent/reference-state bytes, parameter

   are matched within a track.
3. Each arm can spend the same acquisition budget. Observations acquired by one
   arm are not leaked to another arm.
4. Reference pre-adaptation time, reset samples, calibration histories and

5. Abstention is an action with task-native cost. Fallback work, including a

6. Selector, gate, logging, provenance, state writes, messages and artifact

   every arm may be reported separately.
7. The confirmation analysis cannot tune margins, supports, thresholds,
   transforms, architectures or stopping rules.
8. Synthetic operation counts remain operations. They are never converted to
   joules. Joules require a later calibrated workstation measurement.

The future machine-readable registry must freeze every cap in native units and

## Measurements and units

### Task and invariance measurements

1. task-native loss, with its DGP-specific unit;
2. complete-trajectory discrepancy, dimensionless after a frozen output scale;
3. peak, latency, duration, adaptation-tail and calibration errors reported
   separately;
4. classification or selector error, abstention rate and abstention loss;
5. success probability, path length and action count for active-search tracks;
6. recoverability error for protected absolute variables; and
7. risk-gate violations, never averaged into ordinary task loss.

For output component $j$ and scale factor $p$, the default trajectory score is

$$
D_{j,p}
=
\sqrt{
\frac{1}{T}
\int_0^T
\left(
\frac{y_{j,p}(t)-y_{j,1}(t)}{s_{y,j}}
\right)^2 dt
},
$$

where $T$ is seconds and the nonzero scale $s_{y,j}$ is frozen from public
development data. Peak equality cannot substitute for $D_{j,p}$.

### Resource measurements

1. CPU time in seconds and process wall time in seconds;
2. peak resident memory and retained model/state in bytes;
3. scalar arithmetic, transcendental evaluations and random draws as separate
   counts;
4. observed, transmitted and logged bytes;
5. reference, cache and parameter writes as counts and bytes;
6. acquisition, fallback and model invocation counts;
7. generated artifact bytes and file count; and
8. later workstation energy in joules with boundary and uncertainty, currently
   unavailable.

No composite score may mix these dimensions without preregistered weights and
a sensitivity analysis.

Every applicable track performs these interventions:

1. remove the absolute channel;
2. remove the relative channel;
3. freeze the reference state;
4. reset the reference at the wrong time;
5. contaminate the reference with another channel;
6. remove the support/validity flag;
7. replace the mechanism with explicit log ratio;
8. replace it with a matched state-space or recurrent null;
9. hide or reveal one calibrated absolute observation;
10. switch a multiplicative shift to an additive one;
11. move inputs toward zero, clipping or saturation; and
12. charge versus omit maintenance only as an audit contrast, never as the
    primary result.

Ablations share the same held-out worlds as their parent arm. Their extra
records are identified separately; they cannot be pooled as independent seeds.

## Statistical analysis plan

1. Freeze a public-development generator, seed grammar, configuration grid,
   primary loss, margins, multiplicity family and analysis code before any
   confirmation seed is revealed.
2. Use paired worlds across arms and analyze seed-level aggregates. World-level
   observations within a seed are not treated as independent confirmation
   replicates.
3. Report paired effect estimates with simultaneous confidence intervals for
   task loss, risk, trajectory discrepancy and each primary resource. Do not
   replace intervals with thresholded $p$-values.
4. Require task/risk noninferiority before testing resource superiority.
5. Apply a closed Holm procedure across the ten primary track comparisons, or
   freeze a hierarchical gate with the same family-wise error control.
6. Report all protected transfer strata, including failures and abstentions.
   No post-hoc removal of near-zero, saturated, stale-reference or
   absolute-critical worlds is allowed.
7. Calibration, tail risk and worst-stratum effects accompany means.
8. Seeds, generator identity, raw append-only events and analysis artifacts
   remain sufficient for deterministic recomputation.
9. A future powered confirmation count must be derived from public pilot
   variance and a frozen minimum relevant effect; the count is not invented in
   this prose contract.

## Promotion, rejection, and kill rules

A track can support its bounded artificial translation only if all of the
following hold:

1. task and protected-risk noninferiority pass simultaneously;
2. at least one complete resource endpoint improves with its confidence
   interval beyond the frozen relevance margin;
3. the proposed arm beats every applicable mature null, not only `A-RAW`;
4. the improvement survives its registered hostile-transfer strata or the
   support gate abstains before violation;
5. reference maintenance, selection, fallback and artifacts are charged;
6. no privileged hidden state, future statistic or evaluator label reaches the
   policy; and
7. raw records, code, configuration and environment deterministically replay.

The claim is rejected or retired if any of these kill conditions occurs:

1. a static, log-ratio, streaming, state-space or recurrent null matches or
   dominates the proposed arm;
2. trajectory collapse exists only for steps or peaks;
3. an additive shift, near-zero input, saturation or stale reference causes an
   unflagged protected failure;
4. the ratio-only representation loses an absolute-critical target;
5. maintenance or fallback removes the resource advantage;
6. the selector needs confirmation labels or future observations;
7. the effect depends on one seed, one scale interpolation range or one
   unregistered exclusion; or
8. measured workstation energy later fails to support any energy claim.

Passing synthetic tests would establish only the frozen DGP result. It would
not validate the biological source, a deployed system, safety, conformity or a
general efficiency law.

## RSD-T01 — Full-trajectory symmetry versus weaker lookalikes

**Claim:** [C-1540](../../research/claims.md#c-1540).

**Question.** Can an arm distinguish exact/approximate full-trajectory scale
symmetry from finite-horizon endpoint return, Weber-like peak equality and
static normalization?

**DGP.** Generate positive initialized nonlinear state-space systems in five
balanced generator families chosen to expose different response properties:

1. exact scale-equivariant systems;
2. approximate systems with a frozen nonzero trajectory error;
3. finite-horizon endpoint-return systems without full-trajectory symmetry;
4. equal-peak/shifted-latency systems; and
5. static ratio maps with no causal adaptation.

These families are not mutually exclusive scientific properties. A static
ratio map is itself exactly scale-symmetric, endpoint return and equal peak can
coexist, and exact trajectory symmetry does not identify a dynamic mechanism.
The evaluator therefore assigns separately evaluated cross-cutting targets;
they are not statistically or logically independent:

1. system-level trajectory symmetry across the complete frozen history × scale
   grid: `exact`, `approximate`, or `absent`, classified from the maximum
   registered cell discrepancy $D_{j,\max}=\max_{h,p}D_{j,h,p}$ and frozen exact
   and approximate tolerances;
2. per-cell finite-horizon endpoint return $A_{\mathrm{end},j,h,p}$ relative to
   each trajectory's own prestimulus value, kept distinct from asymptotic exact
   adaptation;
3. per-cell equality $E_{\mathrm{peak},j,h,p}$ of peak absolute departure from
   prestimulus, with an earliest-tie rule;
4. causal-memory truth only when declared equations, state access, or an
   identifying intervention supports it; and
5. generator-side support membership by input domain, transformation family,
   instrument range, initialization contract, causal observation contract, and
   evaluation window. Arm-side support detection and abstention are scored
   separately because detectability depends on the observation interface.

Endpoint and peak matrices remain primary. A compact reducer is frozen as
`all` when every registered cell is true, `none` when every cell is false and
`partial` otherwise. Missing or rejected cells retain abstentions; they are not
dropped from $D_{j,\max}$ or averaged away.

Generator-family identification is retained only as a secondary synthetic
diagnostic. Each initialized system receives same-ratio/different-background
steps, pulses, ramps and seed-dependent band-limited stochastic profiles.
The isolated foundation fixes dimensionless scale factors $p\in\{2,4,8\}$
relative to the $p=1$ reference: $p\in\{2,4\}$ are
`observed-development`, and $p=8$ is `withheld-prospective`. That public role
freezes a later information-cut candidate; it is not a confirmation secret and
is not yet consumed by the runner.

Before comparator expansion, the scientific grid must freeze multiple scale
cells for every initialization, their development or prospective roles, and
valid hostile worlds separately from malformed-record sentinels. This
foundation does not by itself implement the DGP, a predictive endpoint, or a
comparison.

**Arms.** The actionable registry is exactly `A-RAW`, `B-STATIC-DIV`,
`B-STREAM`, `B-LOG-RATIO`, `B-DIFFERENCE`, `B-STATE-SPACE`, `B-RECURRENT`, and
`C-DUAL`. All eight actionable arms receive one byte-identical raw causal
packet. Division, logarithms, references, derivatives, support features and
summaries are constructed inside the arm and charged there. `O-STATISTIC` is
evaluator-only and is excluded from information parity, tuning, promotion
comparisons and resource rankings.

**Primary endpoint.** Conformance and cost for the observable trace
coordinates, with abstentions retained in the primary denominator, subject to
a simultaneous upper bound on complete-trajectory discrepancy estimation
error. Five-way generator-family macro recall is secondary. Because the
registered raw packet exposes both complete output trajectories, paired
trajectory match, endpoint return, peak equality and discrepancy are directly
computable; their errors test implementation conformance and cost, not whether
a representation learned the property. Causal memory is not scored from a
finite trace unless an identifying intervention or equation-access contract is
registered. A predictive claim requires a separate amendment that withholds a
suffix, scale cell, or intervention and freezes the corresponding evaluator
contract before comparator outputs are inspected.

**Hostiles.** Valid scientific hostile worlds include equal peak with shifted
latency, slow adaptation tail, additive offset, hidden reset, near-zero
reference, clipping, and future-normalization leakage. They retain axis-specific
inside/outside support across input domain, transformation, instrument,
initialization, causal observation, and evaluation window, plus their
denominator. Malformed ordering, checksum, unit, or future-derived packet
sentinels instead test fail-closed contract rejection and are never substituted
for those worlds.

**Kill rule.** Under a future registered predictive cut, kill the
relative-symmetry diagnostic if step and peak summaries perform equivalently on
the protected non-step families, or if `C-DUAL` does not beat the
state-space/recurrent null after full state cost. The currently exposed
complete-trajectory endpoint cannot trigger that comparative rule.

**Required artifacts.** Per-trajectory inputs/outputs, initialization identity,
scale group, interface, generator family, separately evaluated trace facts,
structural-truth provenance, axis-qualified support vector, arm-side support
decision, identical actionable-arm packet hash, operation/state ledger and
paired seed-level analysis.

## RSD-T02 — Mechanism discrimination under matched step behavior

**Claims:** primary [C-1541](../../research/claims.md#c-1541), with the
adaptation, intervention, equivalence and floor boundaries in
[C-1560](../../research/claims.md#c-1560),
[C-1562](../../research/claims.md#c-1562),
[C-1563](../../research/claims.md#c-1563), and
[C-1564](../../research/claims.md#c-1564). C-1561 supplies evidence context
for a future pulse-signature extension and is not routed as complete here.

**Question.** Which causal properties remain identifiable after several
synthetic systems are forced to produce the exact same canonical step trace,
and can an estimator recover a separately registered singular-limit
supremum-error floor without deleting the fast boundary layer?

**Two strata.** `T02-MECH` handles intervention-qualified property inference.
`T02-FLOOR` handles the source-shaped fast/slow norm question. Their outputs and
statistics remain separate.

**Matched-step DGP.** The five equation-defined recipes are a reduced I1-FFL,
nonlinear reported-output feedback, two-channel local reference memory, static
affine normalization plus an ordinary high-pass state, and explicit log
difference plus the same readout order. With $F_*=2$,
$b\in\{0.5,2,8\}\,\mathrm U$ and
$\tau_*\in\{0.5,1,2\}\,\mathrm s$, every recipe is initialized at normalized
background and produces

$$
y_*(t)=e^{-t/\tau_*}
$$

for the canonical step. The exact equations and derivations are frozen in the
[T02 mathematical contract](../../math/interventional-mechanism-equivalence.md#exact-matched-step-construction).
Initialization residual must be at most $10^{-12}$ and every pairwise
canonical-step supremum difference at most $10^{-10}$. These are synthetic
construction tolerances, not biological margins.

**Primary truth.** `generator_recipe` is evaluator-only provenance. The
scoreable vector contains drive transform, reported-output feedback,
channel-local state and causal-memory status. Nonlinear update form remains
equation provenance because it co-varies with output feedback in this five-world
bank and cannot be credited as a separately identified coordinate. The reduced
I1-FFL and affine high-pass recipes intentionally share the same operational
property vector and full-panel equivalence class. The evaluator retains a
pairwise state of `separated`, `equivalent`, or `unresolved`; it never copies a
recipe name into property truth.

**Intervention panel.** The fixed full panel has exactly 26 episodes:

1. three canonical steps;
2. six pulse-width × period cells with
   $w\in\{1/8,1/2,2\}\,\mathrm s$,
   $\Pi\in\{1/2,2,8\}\,\mathrm s$ and $w<\Pi$;
3. eight linear/exponential, up/down, $0.5/4\,\mathrm s$ ramps;
4. two opaque-state resets and two opaque-state freezes;
5. one reported-output clamp;
6. two interrupted-ramp input holds; and
7. same-channel and cross-channel restimulation.

All episodes last $24,\mathrm s$. Apart from the three background-specific
steps, they use $b=2\,\mathrm U$ and $\tau_*=1\,\mathrm s$. Pulses switch
$v$ from one to two from $j\Pi$ through $j\Pi+w$. Up-ramps move from one to
two and down-ramps from one to $0.5$, linearly in fold or log fold. Reset occurs
at $0.75\,\mathrm s$; freeze and output-clamp windows are
$[0.5,1)\,\mathrm s$; interrupted ramps pause after one second of active
progress; and restimulation uses channel A on $[0,1)\,\mathrm s$, a one-second
gap, then A or B on $[2,3)\,\mathrm s$. The closed machine descriptors retain
the numeric commands rather than only episode names.

Every recipe exposes two opaque state handles; inert padding and a hidden
seed-derived permutation prevent state count or name from leaking provenance.
Refractory stability and period skipping are one-sided signatures: presence
may separate a certified pair, while absence retains uncertainty. The current
five-world construction does not plant the Rahi-style signature—the registered
feedback nonlinearity vanishes at the square-pulse levels—so C-1561 is evidence
input for a future pulse-signature world, not a result or completed construction
test in this tranche.

**Observation regimes.** `O0-MATCHED-STEP` exposes three episodes and at most
4,611 rows and requires abstention for every varying structural coordinate.
`O1-FULL-PANEL` exposes 26 episodes and at most 39,962 rows and is the future
fixed-panel primary regime. `O2-SELECT6` begins with `O0`, permits six further
queries with no more than two privileged internal interventions, and is
secondary. Active selection receives no primary authority until its own
prospective response-freeze contract exists.

**Pairwise separation.** For recipes $m,n$ and intervention $i$, use

$$
D_{\infty}^{m,n,i}=\max_k|y_{m,i,k}-y_{n,i,k}|.
$$

For estimate $\widehat D$ and refinement-error bound $\eta$, a
public-development numerical separation requires
$\widehat D-\eta\ge10^{-3}$ and $\eta\le10^{-8}$. Bounded numerical
equivalence requires $\widehat D+\eta\le10^{-10}$; analytic equivalence must
not conflict with that check. All other pairs are unresolved. The RMS
difference is diagnostic only.

The v1 construction certificates use $b=2\,\mathrm U$,
$\tau_*=1\,\mathrm s$, a $24\,\mathrm s$ horizon and binary64 RK4 with
$1/1024\,\mathrm s$ versus $1/2048\,\mathrm s$ refinement. Half-open event
windows use the left command at the final RK4 stage. Conservative lower bounds
are $0.23$ for clamp and cross-channel separations and $0.07$ for the
affine-versus-log ramp; observed refinement differences must not exceed
$10^{-12}$. The I1-FFL/affine-high-pass equivalence is analytic under the
registered positive affine interface.

**Actionable arms.** The closed registry is `A-RAW`, `B-STATIC-DIV`,
`B-STREAM`, `B-LOG-RATIO`, `B-DIFFERENCE`, `B-STATE-SPACE`, `B-RECURRENT`,
`C-MECHANISM-BANK` and `C-DUAL`. `O-GRAPH` is evaluator-only and excluded from
parity, tuning, promotion and resource ranking. None of the actionable arms is
implemented or currently eligible.

**Primary mechanism endpoint.** Per-property calibration in nats and
dimensionless decision loss are reported separately. A correct decision on an
identifiable singleton has loss zero; abstention on that singleton costs
$0.25$; abstention on a certified non-singleton set has loss zero; a wrong
decision or forced declaration inside a non-singleton set costs one. Report
coverage, selective risk and reliability, never a hidden composite.

**Floor DGP and endpoint.** Cross a source-shaped singular input-degradation
model and exact-zero/regular-perturbation controls over
$\epsilon\in\{10^{-1},\ldots,10^{-7}\}$ and
$p\in\{0.5,2,4,8,20\}$. The 105 cells use both an
$\epsilon$-scaled fast grid and an eight-second slow grid. The primary endpoint
is

$$
D_{\infty,\epsilon,p}
=
\sup_{0\le t\le8\,\mathrm s}
|y_{\epsilon,p}(t)-y_{\epsilon,1}(t)|.
$$

Integrated RMS remains diagnostic. A finite sweep can validate the registered
analytic construction and bound; it cannot prove an asymptotic theorem.

**Information firewall.** Before response freeze, actionable arms receive only
causal inputs, reported outputs, masks, opaque commands, timestamps and units.
Recipe, equation, semantic state, parameter, property, future sample,
equivalence certificate and continuous evaluator truth remain hidden. Public
code creates no confirmation secrecy.

**Resource vector.** Retain episodes, sample rows, serialized bytes, input
commands, resets, freezes, output clamps, channel switches, state writes,
scalar operations, transcendental evaluations, retained-state bytes,
parameter bytes, tuning trials, wall seconds and later measured joules as

**Hostiles.** Additive offset, near-zero input, clipping, hidden reset,

Parser, checksum, unit and order failures remain malformed sentinels outside

**Current authority.** The isolated machine registry is
`contract-foundation-only`, `public-development`,
`registered-projection-no-secret-custody`, comparison false and `NO_RESULT`.
It is not consumed by the v2 T01 runner and adds no execution claim.

## RSD-T03 — Multiplicatively rescaled active chemical search

**Claim:** [C-1542](../../research/claims.md#c-1542).

**Question.** Does relative sensing preserve active-search behavior across
multiplicative source strengths without overclaiming additive or saturated
transfer?

**DGP.** A two-dimensional positive scalar field has source location, diffusion
width, transport drift and amplitude. Agents receive causal local samples and
choose heading/speed actions. Development and confirmation vary amplitude
multiplicatively; protected transfers add a constant background, change width,
clip the sensor or move the signal toward zero.

**Arms.** raw gradient controller, streaming normalization, log-gradient
controller, state-space plume estimator, recurrent controller, and gated
relative/absolute controller.

**Primary endpoint.** Success probability within a frozen horizon, gated by
collision/risk noninferiority. Secondary endpoints are path length in metres,
time in seconds, samples, actions, operations, bytes and state writes.

**Interventions.** Same spatial profile at held-out amplitudes; same local
difference but different ratio; same ratio but different absolute load;
additive offset; saturation; and sensor dropout.

**Kill rule.** Kill if benefit disappears against the state-space/recurrent
null, if path success hides more sensing or compute, or if the support gate
does not reject additive/saturated out-of-regime cases.

**Required artifacts.** Field specification, trajectory, samples, actions,
support flags, terminal outcome, path/resource ledger and per-regime paired
analysis.

## RSD-T04 — Internal gain robustness and absolute counter-task

**Claim:** [C-1543](../../research/claims.md#c-1543).

**Question.** Does an internal fold statistic transfer across component gain
and initial-state variation, and what information is lost for an absolute
target?

**DGP.** A positive upstream signal passes through a variable-gain internal
state to two downstream targets: one depends on post/pre fold change and one on
post-stimulus absolute state. Gains, starting states and upstream amplitudes
vary independently.

**Arms.** absolute decoder, fixed normalization, log ratio, gain-augmented
regression, state-space/recurrent decoder and `C-DUAL`.

**Primary endpoint.** Joint noninferiority on the ratio-defined and
absolute-defined targets; report each separately and prohibit compensation of
absolute failure by relative success.

**Hostiles.** gain outside support, extreme initial state, partial reset,
absolute/relative target switch after representation construction, and
measurement noise coupled to magnitude.

**Kill rule.** Kill a ratio-only translation on any protected absolute-target
failure. Kill `C-DUAL` if ordinary gain augmentation or state-space estimation
matches its joint frontier more cheaply.

**Required artifacts.** Gain/state intervention, both target definitions,

decision.

## RSD-T05 — Heterogeneous baseline to downstream count

**Claim:** [C-1544](../../research/claims.md#c-1544).

**Question.** Which causal statistic best predicts a downstream count when
baseline abundance varies across units?

**DGP.** Generate positive input trajectories and overdispersed counts from one
of five hidden causal statistics: absolute level, additive change, fold change,
time integral, or a short recurrent functional. Baseline, noise, dispersion and
missingness vary independently.

**Arms.** single-statistic generalized linear models, a preregistered combined
model, matched compact recurrent predictor, `C-DUAL` selector, and oracle
statistic ceiling.

**Primary endpoint.** Held-out count deviance and calibration for each DGP
class. Report variance explained only as a secondary descriptive quantity.

**Interventions.** Same fold/different change, same change/different fold,
baseline shift, causal-window truncation, missing baseline and future-maximum
leakage sentinel.

**Kill rule.** Kill a universal fold-statistic claim if another statistic wins
its own DGP or if a compact recurrent null dominates across classes. Reject any
arm that computes a maximum using observations after the decision deadline.

**Required artifacts.** causal windows, target-generating statistic hidden
from arms, predictions, count likelihood, calibration bins, leakage audit and
complete feature/compute ledger.

## RSD-T06 — Statistic selection across interfaces and protocols

**Claim:** [C-1545](../../research/claims.md#c-1545).

**Question.** Can a selector distinguish absolute level, rectified additive
difference, temporal derivative and multiplicative ratio without assuming the
pathway name determines the statistic?

**DGP.** Four balanced generator families receive steps, linear ramps,
exponential ramps and random piecewise-smooth inputs. Parameters create both
identifiable and deliberately observationally unresolved worlds.

**Arms.** fixed absolute/difference/derivative/ratio policies, state-space and
recurrent selectors, `C-DUAL` with abstention, and oracle DGP label.

**Primary endpoint.** Class-balanced selector loss including a frozen
abstention cost, gated by downstream task loss. Identifiable and unresolved
worlds are reported separately.

**Interventions.** same ratio versus same difference pairs, linear versus
exponential ramp, response delay, rectification, negative changes,
channel/interface swap and unobserved baseline.

**Kill rule.** Kill if the selector makes confident assignments on the
protected unresolved set, uses future trajectory, or cannot beat deploying the
best fixed statistic plus a conventional change detector.

**Required artifacts.** DGP/interface ID retained only for evaluation,
observation history, candidate features, selector posterior, abstention,
downstream loss, calibration and per-protocol confusion matrices.

## RSD-T07 — Density-qualified secrete-and-sense relay

**Claim:** [C-1546](../../research/claims.md#c-1546).

**Question.** Does a relative local relay preserve collective dynamics across

**DGP.** Agents occupy a graph or spatial grid. They sense a positive external
field, update internal state, secrete messages and adapt. Density, volume,
transport delay, degradation, topology, message loss and refractory time vary
factorially.

**Arms.** absolute relay, density-normalized controller, ordinary consensus or
coupled oscillator, recurrent local controller, relative relay, and gated
relative/absolute relay.

**Primary endpoint.** Target oscillation/coordination quality with frequency,
phase coherence and failure probability reported separately. Messages, bytes,
state writes, operations and wall time are co-primary resources.

**Interventions.** density scaling at fixed transport; transport/degradation
change at fixed density; topology split; delay; loss; stale reference; and
absolute field hazard.

**Kill rule.** Kill generic density-robustness claims if invariance disappears
when transport is made explicit. Kill the relative relay if mature coupled-
oscillator/consensus control is noninferior and cheaper.

**Required artifacts.** graph/grid, density and transport parameters, every
message event, state trace, frequency/coherence estimates, failures and full
communication ledger.

## RSD-T08 — Relative benign response with absolute hazard

**Claim:** [C-1547](../../research/claims.md#c-1547).

**Question.** Can a controller retain approximate relative sensitivity in a
benign range while preserving an engineered absolute stop outside it?

**DGP.** A positive input has a registered low/medium regime with
ratio-dependent pulse target and a separate synthetic high-input damage
function. The absolute damage counter-task is an engineering stressor; it is
not attributed to a specific biological sensing channel.

**Arms.** proportional controller, adaptation-only controller, log-ratio
controller, fixed absolute threshold, state-space/recurrent controller and
gated dual-channel controller.

**Primary endpoint.** Benign-regime trajectory loss subject to zero-tolerance
protected damage violations. Resource comparison occurs only after the damage
gate passes.

**Interventions.** equal-fold pulses across benign backgrounds, equal peak with
different decline shape, slow ramp through the damage boundary, clipping,
sensor saturation, stale reference and missing absolute channel.

**Kill rule.** Kill any ratio-only/adaptation-only arm on an absolute damage
violation. Kill the dual-channel translation if a conventional threshold plus
state-space null matches it with lower complete cost.

**Required artifacts.** regime, input, reference, response trajectory,
absolute damage state, stop action, support decision, all violations and
resource ledger.

## RSD-T09 — Channel-specific reference-memory lifecycle

**Claim:** [C-1548](../../research/claims.md#c-1548).

**Question.** When does a resource-bearing channel-specific reference improve
relative response after construction, turnover, reset and staleness are
charged?

**DGP.** Multiple positive ligand-like channels have independent backgrounds,
shared downstream capacity and reference resources. Background exposure
reduces an interface state; production, recovery and turnover replenish it.
The generator includes both receptor-memory-consistent and alternative-memory
worlds so the mechanism is never assumed unique.

**Arms.** EMA reference, state-space estimator, compact recurrent state,
channel-specific interface memory, shared-memory ablation and `C-DUAL`.

**Primary endpoint.** Relative-target loss and cross-channel specificity,
gated by stale-memory and absolute-target risk. Maintenance writes, production,
recovery time, state bytes and compute are primary resources.

**Interventions.** background shift, observation gap, channel switch,
cross-channel stimulation, forced reset, accelerated turnover, stopped
production, saturation and unseen background range.

**Kill rule.** Kill uniqueness language if alternative memory fits. Kill the
translation if EMA/state-space references match accuracy and specificity more
cheaply or if lifecycle cost removes the frontier advantage.

**Required artifacts.** channel histories, interface/reference state, age and
support, reset/turnover events, predictions, cross-channel error, maintenance
and complete compute ledger.

## RSD-T10 — Robustness, recoverability, and compute frontier

**Claim:** [C-1549](../../research/claims.md#c-1549).

**Question.** What becomes unrecoverable from one invariant observation
interface, and how does a calibrated absolute side channel change robustness,
recoverability and resource cost?

**DGP.** Parameterized initialized systems produce an invariant output under a
declared scale transformation. Alternative worlds vary observation maps,
known/unknown parameter sets, initial-state access and one additional absolute
measurement.

**Arms.** invariant-only representation, raw absolute representation,
state-space/recurrent estimator, invariant plus noisy absolute side channel,
and evaluator-only full-state oracle.

**Primary endpoints.** nuisance-scale robustness and protected-parameter
recoverability are separate axes. Compute, bytes, observations and
calibration burden form the resource vector; no scalar winner is declared
without a frozen operating preference.

**Interventions.** remove/add an output, reveal/fix a parameter, change
initialization, change noise, change side-channel calibration, and switch the
target from invariant to absolute.

**Kill rule.** Kill universal “information loss” language if measurement
design restores recoverability. Kill an invariant-only design on the absolute
counter-task. Kill the side-channel design if robustness is lost or added cost
dominates a raw/state-space null.

**Required artifacts.** model/observation definition, known-parameter set,
initialization, transformation, representation, estimates, identifiability
diagnostic, calibration, paired frontier coordinates and full resource ledger.

diagnostic arms, omits the eight-actionable-arm comparator stack, full valid
