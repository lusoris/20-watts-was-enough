# Source provenance records

This directory preserves traceable origins for material that started the
project. Google/Gemini/Docs entries are link-only provenance records; their
bodies are not reproduced in the public current tree. Everything here is a
historical input, not a canonical claim and not scientific evidence.

This index is project-authored and licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. That notice
does not extend to the imported files it describes.

## Licensing boundary

This directory is excluded wholesale from the repository's EUPL and CC BY-SA
grants unless an individual file carries an explicit project licence notice.
Linked discussions, documents, source leads, official taxonomy snapshots, and
every other third-party work retain their original copyright, database rights,
licences, notices, and statutory conditions. A provenance record, citation,
link, bibliography entry, or lawful quotation does not relicense the underlying
work. See
[`../LICENSING.md`](../LICENSING.md).

Rules:

- Do not silently alter origin URLs, titles, dates, or publication boundaries.
- Record access and transcription limitations in the provenance record.
- Move supported ideas into the canonical concept only through the evidence
  ledger in [`../research/claims.md`](../research/claims.md).
- Treat every quantitative claim encountered at an origin as unaudited until a
  claim entry says otherwise.
- Preserve copyright, licence, attribution, provenance, and use-basis notices;
  do not infer an open licence from academic publication or public access.

### Automated publication boundary

`npm run validate:sources` treats this public source tree as a byte-exact,
closed allowlist. It verifies the recorded byte size and SHA-256 digest of this
index, all ten provenance records, and the exact six files in the dated
taxonomy snapshot. Adding a file, replacing an allowed path, appending a
Google/Gemini body, or changing even one byte therefore fails closed until the
change receives an explicit review and the integrity manifest is deliberately
updated. This prevents a familiar link-only record or taxonomy filename from
becoming a publication path for unreviewed content.

## Official taxonomy snapshots

The dated [`taxonomies/2026-08-25/`](taxonomies/2026-08-25/README.md)
directory preserves the EU EuroSciVoc 1.6 scheme and hierarchy, the official
DFG 2024--2028 and corrected ANZSRC 2020 classifications as link-only source
publications, and classification-only CSV extracts used to expand the
whole-science routing inventory. The EuroSciVoc snapshots and the two minimal
extracts define names and hierarchy only; they do not support scientific claims
or imply that a field has been researched. Each upstream work retains the
rights, attribution, and exclusions recorded in the dated provenance index.
