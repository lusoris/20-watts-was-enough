# Research-output disclosures

This directory records contributor responsibility, support, competing
interests, and material external-tool use for tagged research snapshots and
other claim-eligible outputs. A disclosure describes how an output was made; it
does not promote a claim, prove independent review, or replace the evidence and
experiment ledgers.

Each record states:

- output identity and claimed authority;
- contributors, concrete roles, accountable approver, and approval state;
- funding, donated infrastructure, data or equipment access, and other material
  support;
- declared financial and non-financial competing interests, including an
  explicit unresolved state when no declaration exists;
- material AI, automation, and external services, with purpose and dated
  identity where available; and
- human and automated verification actually completed, including what was not
  independently reviewed.

The controlling requirements are in the
[`research integrity baseline`](../research-integrity-baseline.md). Preserve
historical release disclosures. Correct or supersede them visibly rather than
rewriting an old snapshot's process.
