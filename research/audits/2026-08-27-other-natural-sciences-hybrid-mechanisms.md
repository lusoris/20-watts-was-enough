# Other natural sciences: hybrid mechanisms across residual field boundaries

- **Audit date:** 2026-08-27
- **Breadth target:** OECD FORD 1.7, *Other natural sciences*, treated as a residual-class omission detector rather than a coherent discipline.
- **Scope:** five bounded mechanisms from underrepresented hybrid or interface fields: long-distance microbial electron transport, stress-triggered reversible biostasis material, herbivore-qualified third-party recruitment, root-mediated hydraulic redistribution, and amorphous-precursor biomineralization.
- **Evidence rule:** only official classification sources and primary empirical papers support source-domain propositions. Reviews, analogies, and taxonomy labels do not establish a mechanism.
- **Normative context:** none. The OECD and EU sources below classify fields; they do not supply scientific evidence or legal requirements.
- **Promotion state:** integrated source audit. Fine-grained taxonomy routes now point to this audit, but no central `C-` claim, `P-` principle, architecture candidate, fixture, result, or energy claim is added.
- **Repository decision:** all five mechanisms deduplicate into existing principle families or mature engineering nulls. Their durable value is a set of new boundary conditions and negative controls, not five new biologically named components.

## Classification boundary

The [OECD Frascati Manual](https://doi.org/10.1787/9789264239012-en) lists `1.7 Other natural sciences` beside mathematics, computing, physical, chemical, Earth/environmental, and biological sciences. It does not define one shared mechanism for the residual. The [EU Publications Office description of EuroSciVoc](https://interoperable-europe.ec.europa.eu/collection/eu-semantic-interoperability-catalogue/solution/euroscivoc) says that its first two levels align with FORD while lower levels add established and emerging fields discovered in CORDIS material.

The correct audit operation is therefore:

1. use FORD 1.7 as a reminder that named parent fields are not exhaustive;
2. search classification disagreements and intersections for mechanisms hidden by parent ownership;
3. route a retained result to the most precise child fields after review; and
4. never infer evidence from the residual label itself.

This audit sampled five intersections that were unassigned before this review
and are now recorded in the [fine-grained inventory](../taxonomies/field-depth.md):

## Executive finding

Five source mechanisms survived the evidence screen, but none survives deduplication as a new project principle:

1. **Cable bacteria spatially separate compatible half-work while preserving a conserved carrier flow.** This is a physically specific instance of compartmentalized processing and structural transport, not evidence for a new communication or intelligence primitive.
2. **Tardigrade CAHS proteins provide a scoped example of stress-triggered reversible material reorganization.** Desiccation tolerance and reversible gel or filament formation are experimentally supported; a single universal protective mechanism, especially vitrification alone, is not.
3. **Herbivore-induced plant volatiles can recruit a third trophic level through a typed environmental cue.** This is a source-qualified content-routed alarm with an external responder, not generic cooperation or proof that every volatile is honest.
4. **Hydraulic redistribution follows water-potential gradients through an existing root network and can reverse direction.** This is gradient-driven resource prepositioning, not foresight, altruism, or a universal plant allocation policy.
5. **Sea-urchin biomineralization can pass through transient amorphous calcium-carbonate phases before durable crystallization.** This is staged physical commitment, not evidence that an amorphous software state will discover a better architecture.

The mechanisms are genuinely distinct at source level: conserved charge transport, reversible stress materialization, tri-party cue-mediated recruitment, bidirectional hydraulic flux, and precursor-to-crystal conversion have different state transformations and failure boundaries. At project level they strengthen existing `P-` bundles and conventional nulls.

## 1. Long-distance electron transport across separated redox zones

### Source observation

Nielsen et al. perturbed oxygen above defaunated marine sediment and observed a response in sulphide concentration more than 12 mm below the oxic zone in less than one hour. Their mass balance attributed more than 40% of total oxygen consumption in that studied sediment to electrons conducted from the anoxic zone; ordinary molecular diffusion could not explain the response time ([doi:10.1038/nature08790](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature08790)).

Pfeffer et al. then found long filamentous Desulfobulbaceae in current-producing sediments. Filament cutting, pore-size barriers, and interposed layers disrupted the geochemical coupling, while microscopy identified longitudinal structures consistent with electron transport ([doi:10.1038/nature11586](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature11586)). Meysman et al. later placed intact filaments and isolated fibre sheaths across electrodes and measured sustained currents over distances up to 10.1 mm, supporting electronic rather than ionic conduction in the prepared samples ([doi:10.1038/s41467-019-12115-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-12115-7)).

The normalized operation is:

```text
spatially separated donor and acceptor
-> locally execute each compatible half-reaction
-> transport the conserved carrier through a dedicated continuous structure
-> fail when the carrier path or either boundary resource is lost
```

It is not “the cells communicate over long distances.” The demonstrated quantity is charge transfer coupled to redox chemistry.

### Proposed AI translation

Use a narrow carrier to connect processing stages that must reside in different resource zones instead of moving the full substrate, duplicating scarce boundary resources, or co-locating all work. Candidate examples include a low-state sufficient statistic transported between data-local and verification-local compute, or a hardware pipeline split across thermal or energy zones.

This translation is useful only if the carrier preserves everything required by the downstream half-work. Semantic compression or information discarded by the carrier is a separate claim.

### Deduplication

- **Nearest principles:** [P-008 compartmentalized interaction](../principle-registry.md#p-008--compartmentalized-interaction), [P-010 structural offloading](../principle-registry.md#p-010--structural-offloading-and-co-design), and [P-013 externalized shared state](../principle-registry.md#p-013--externalized-shared-state).
- **Existing domain owners:** the [microbial ecology audit](2026-08-05-microbial-ecology-biofilms.md) already separates transported metabolites from cooperation; the [electrochemistry audit](2026-08-25-electrochemistry-interface-memory-degradation.md) owns charge, interface, transport, and conversion boundaries; the [process-engineering audit](2026-08-05-process-engineering.md) owns staged material and energy flows.
- **Why it remains source-distinct:** cable bacteria couple spatially separated electrochemical half-reactions through a continuous multicellular conductor. That is not ordinary metabolite cross-feeding or a diffusing quorum field.
- **Why it does not earn a new `P-`:** after removing substrate chemistry, the artificial operation is disaggregated pipeline placement plus conserved-flow transport. Network flow, co-location, remote procedure, pipeline, and move-compute-versus-move-data designs are mature nulls.

### Falsifiable measurable prediction

At matched quality and boundary resources, a carrier-split arm should improve accepted work per joule and per transported byte only when all three conditions hold:

1. the donor-side and acceptor-side resources are genuinely separated;
2. the transported sufficient state is materially smaller or cheaper than the substrate or resource duplication; and
3. path loss, validation, and recovery do not erase that saving.

The advantage must disappear when resources are co-located, when the sufficient state approaches substrate size, or when the carrier is cut. Report accepted tasks, task loss, transported bytes and byte-metres, p50/p95 latency in seconds, carrier failures, recovery time in seconds, and measured boundary energy in joules.

## 2. Stress-triggered reversible biostasis material

### Source observation and live dispute

Boothby et al. reported that tardigrade-specific intrinsically disordered proteins were upregulated during drying, that depletion impaired desiccation survival in the tested tardigrades, and that heterologous expression improved desiccation tolerance in yeast and bacteria. Purified proteins formed glassy material in their assays, motivating a vitrification hypothesis ([doi:10.1016/j.molcel.2017.02.018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.molcel.2017.02.018)).

Subsequent work resolved additional material behaviour. Yagi-Utsumi et al. observed concentration-dependent CAHS1 fibrils and reversible sol–gel transitions, including reversible condensates after osmotic shock in heterologous cells ([doi:10.1038/s41598-021-00724-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-00724-6)). Tanaka et al. found stress-dependent reversible filament formation, gel transition, increased cell stiffness, reduced shrinkage, and improved hyperosmotic-stress survival; helix-disrupting mutations impaired filament and gel formation ([doi:10.1371/journal.pbio.3001780](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.3001780)).

Crilly et al. found that tested desiccation-tolerance proteins and control proteins retained similar water and had similar glass-transition temperatures, while residue-level protection differed. Their results argue against enhanced water retention or vitrification as the sole explanation in those assays and point toward electrostatic interactions, charge patterning, and expanded conformations ([doi:10.1002/pro.4231](https://doi.org/10.1002/pro.4231)).

The retained mechanism is therefore not “turn into glass.” It is the narrower causal and material record:

```text
stress and concentration path
-> reversible protein reorganization
-> altered mobility, mechanics, metabolism, and cargo protection
-> recovery after stress removal under a qualified rehydration path
```

Which material property causes protection remains protein-, cargo-, stress-, and assay-dependent.

### Proposed AI translation

Before a long inactive or hostile interval, transform a bounded module and its required state into a low-activity protected representation; after the interval, restore and validate service. The artificial mechanism must specify what is arrested, what remains active, how the transition is triggered, which state is retained, and how restoration failure is detected.

This is more naturally a storage, runtime, or physical-substrate test than a training-algorithm label. “Biostasis mode” without a state transform and restore contract is only renaming sleep or suspend.

### Deduplication

- **Nearest principles:** [P-009 maintenance plane](../principle-registry.md#p-009--maintenance-plane) and [P-012 lifetime-matched memory](../principle-registry.md#p-012--memory-matched-to-information-lifetime), with [P-003 temporary trace](../principle-registry.md#p-003--temporary-trace-before-commitment) only when a reversible pre-state precedes slower protection.
- **Nearest candidates:** [Candidate 018 artifact tiering](../../experiments/candidates/018-value-reconstructability-aware-tiering.md) and [Candidate 005 severity-ordered containment](../../experiments/candidates/005-severity-ordered-containment.md).
- **Strongest nulls:** process suspend/hibernate, exact checkpoint plus power-down, replica failover, cold storage, application quiescence, thermal or voltage throttling, conventional physical excipients, and full restart from an immutable image.
- **Why no new `P-`:** the invariant is state preservation across an inactive interval plus validated restoration. The source adds a stress-responsive material implementation and sharp cargo/path dependence, not a new information-flow class.

### Falsifiable measurable prediction

A reversible-arrest arm should improve retained function per lifecycle joule only for a predeclared region of stress duration and severity where continuous operation, hot standby, or repeated refresh is expensive and exact restart is insufficient or slower. It must lose when transition overhead dominates, state changes during arrest are required, restore error exceeds the task tolerance, or ordinary checkpoint/power-down matches the frontier.

Report protected-state bytes, transition and restore seconds, lost or stale updates, post-restore task loss, silent restoration errors, repeated-cycle degradation, transition energy, idle energy, restore energy, and hardware or material replacement. Separate protein-source evidence from the speculative artificial translation.

## 3. Herbivore-qualified third-party recruitment

### Source observation

Turlings, Tumlinson, and Lewis found that caterpillar-fed maize seedlings released terpenoid volatiles, while artificial damage alone did not produce significant amounts of the same response unless caterpillar oral secretions were applied at the damaged sites. Female *Cotesia marginiventris* wasps learned to use plant-produced volatile cues associated with hosts or host by-products ([doi:10.1126/science.250.4985.1251](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.250.4985.1251)).

Alborn et al. isolated the fatty-acid–amino-acid conjugate volicitin from beet-armyworm oral secretions. Applied to damaged maize leaves, it induced a volatile blend that attracted parasitoid wasps; mechanical damage alone did not reproduce that blend in the reported experiment ([doi:10.1126/science.276.5314.945](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.276.5314.945)). Paré, Alborn, and Tumlinson used isotope-labelled plants to show that the fatty-acid portion of volicitin was plant-derived while the caterpillar performed hydroxylation and glutamine conjugation ([doi:10.1073/pnas.95.23.13971](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.95.23.13971)).

The source-level operation is tri-party and conditional:

```text
local tissue damage AND attacker-associated chemical context
-> induced transported volatile blend
-> experienced third-party receiver localizes the source
-> receiver action may suppress the attacker
```

The plant does not transmit a signed identity claim, the volatile is not a command, and parasitoid arrival is not guaranteed benefit.

### Proposed AI translation

Emit a typed, locally grounded incident cue that recruits a compatible external responder rather than sending every event to a central controller. The responder must independently decide whether and how to act, and the system must retain cue provenance, transport conditions, receiver availability, and resulting harm.

The testable residue over an ordinary alarm is the conjunction of local damage evidence with attacker-associated context and content-based third-party routing. It is not a justification for unconstrained autonomous escalation.

### Deduplication

- **Nearest principles:** [P-002 local autonomy and escalation](../principle-registry.md#p-002--local-autonomy-with-exception-escalation), [P-011 transient coalitions](../principle-registry.md#p-011--transient-communication-coalitions), and [P-013 externalized shared state](../principle-registry.md#p-013--externalized-shared-state).
- **Existing source owners:** the [plant distributed-control audit](2026-08-05-plant-distributed-control.md) already owns wound alarms and pub/sub nulls; the [plant plasticity audit](2026-08-25-plant-plasticity-memory-signalling.md) owns common alarm plus typed context; the [chemical-sensing audit](2026-08-05-olfaction-chemical-sensing-plume-tracking.md) owns transport, intermittency, mixtures, active localization, drift, and poisoning.
- **Source-level distinction:** this result crosses three trophic actors and recruits an external receiver from a cue generated by damage plus herbivore material. It is not identical to within-plant wound propagation.
- **Why no new `P-`:** after abstraction it is content-based event routing with an independently acting subscriber. Event buses, SOAR systems, typed incident dispatch, and decentralized task allocation are mature nulls.

### Falsifiable measurable prediction

A typed local-recruitment arm should reduce harm and central communication only when damage evidence and attacker-associated context jointly improve responder selection. It must tie or lose under generic faults, absent responders, unreliable transport, novel attacker cues, cue suppression, deceptive cues, or a central dispatcher with the same evidence and budget.

Use a full `damage × attacker-context × responder-availability × transport × deception` factorial. Report false and missed recruitment per eligible event, responder travel or activation latency in seconds, residual harm, collateral action, messages and bytes, responder utilization, service loss, and lifecycle joules. Include a damage-only alarm, context-only classifier, centralized dispatcher, content-based pub/sub, and oracle responder as separate arms.

## 4. Bidirectional hydraulic redistribution through an existing network

### Source observation

Caldwell and Richards supplied deuterated water to deep roots of *Artemisia tridentata* and detected it in neighbouring grass stem water. The experiment supported nocturnal movement from moist deep soil through roots into drier upper soil, followed by later uptake, and also exposed capture by neighbouring plants ([doi:10.1007/BF00378231](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00378231)).

Burgess et al. used modified heat-pulse sap-flow measurements in *Grevillea robusta* and *Eucalyptus camaldulensis*. They observed redistribution from deep wet layers toward dry surface horizons, then reverse movement from rewetted surface soil toward deeper horizons after seasonal rain ([doi:10.1007/s004420050521](https://doi.org/10.1007/s004420050521)).

For locations $i$ and $j$, a deliberately minimal artificial analogue is

$$
Q_{ij}(t)=G_{ij}(t)\left[\Psi_i(t)-\Psi_j(t)\right],
$$

where $Q_{ij}$ is resource flow in resource units per second, $G_{ij}$ is effective path conductance in resource units per potential-unit per second, and $\Psi_i-\Psi_j$ is the signed resource-potential difference. This equation is an artificial transport null, not a fitted law for either source study. Its purpose is to preserve direction reversal: changing the gradient changes the flow sign.

### Proposed AI translation

During low foreground demand, allow existing bidirectional paths to move a bounded resource from high-potential to low-potential regions, subject to reserve and leakage constraints. Examples include cache warming, replica prepositioning, memory-page migration, thermal preconditioning, or energy-buffer balancing.

The source does not justify predictive prefetch by itself. A gradient equalizer has no knowledge of future requests; any claimed anticipation must come from a separately measured forecast.

### Deduplication

- **Nearest principles:** [P-003 temporary reserve](../principle-registry.md#p-003--temporary-trace-before-commitment), [P-006 negative feedback](../principle-registry.md#p-006--homeostatic-negative-feedback), [P-010 structural offloading](../principle-registry.md#p-010--structural-offloading-and-co-design), and [P-013 externalized state](../principle-registry.md#p-013--externalized-shared-state).
- **Nearest candidate:** [Candidate 013 deficit–capability routing](../../experiments/candidates/013-deficit-capability-routing.md), but the hydraulic source lacks its deficit-up/context-down signaling contract.
- **Existing source owner:** the [plant distributed-control audit](2026-08-05-plant-distributed-control.md) covers source–sink flow, drought response, and routing, but not this direction-reversing ecohydrological result.
- **Strongest nulls:** passive potential equalization, backpressure, cache warming, static safety stock, receding-horizon prepositioning, learned prefetch, and no-movement local reserve.
- **Why no new `P-`:** after abstraction this is bounded resource flow over an existing conductive graph. Gradient flow and prepositioning are mature mechanisms.

### Falsifiable measurable prediction

Gradient-only redistribution should improve shortage loss only when conductance, future local demand, and retention align. It should reverse direction after the potential gradient reverses, and it should become harmful when a neighbour captures the resource, leakage is high, movement consumes scarce path capacity, or future demand is anti-correlated with the gradient.

Hold total resource and path capacity fixed. Report shortage events, served demand, stranded or appropriated resource, movement units and unit-metres, foreground interference, reversal delay in seconds, leakage, prediction input bytes if any, and joules. An arm that uses future-demand information must be labelled and compared separately from the gradient-only arm.

## 5. Amorphous precursor before durable mineral commitment

### Source observation

Politi et al. used water etching, spectroscopy, diffraction, and microscopy during sea-urchin spine regeneration and found initial deposition of amorphous calcium carbonate before crystalline calcite ([doi:10.1126/science.1102289](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1102289)). In larval spicules, nanoscale spectromicroscopy resolved an initial hydrated amorphous phase, an intermediate amorphous phase, and final calcite, with juxtaposed phases and a tortuous transformation path through pre-existing amorphous units ([doi:10.1073/pnas.0806604105](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0806604105)).

The retained source mechanism is staged material transformation:

```text
deposit shapeable metastable precursor
-> arrange within a constrained growth geometry
-> transform locally into a lower-mobility durable phase
-> inherit conversion defects, inclusions, and path dependence
```

The papers establish this route in the sampled sea-urchin structures. They do not establish that every biomineral uses it or that the amorphous state globally optimizes final form.

### Proposed AI translation

Keep a structural proposal in a cheap-to-edit precursor representation while essential constraints are still arriving, then convert it once into a validated lower-maintenance structure. Candidate examples are a mutable intermediate graph followed by compiled sparse placement, or a rich trace followed by contract-preserving consolidation.

The artificial translation must define what becomes harder to change after conversion. Without a change in edit cost, runtime cost, physical placement, or recoverability, “crystallization” is only decorative terminology.

### Deduplication

- **Nearest principles:** [P-003 temporary trace before commitment](../principle-registry.md#p-003--temporary-trace-before-commitment) and [P-010 structural offloading](../principle-registry.md#p-010--structural-offloading-and-co-design).
- **Nearest work:** [structural consolidation](../../concept/50-grokking-and-pruning.md#structural-consolidation-before-deletion), [Candidate 001 adaptive topology](../../experiments/candidates/001-adaptive-topology.md), and [Candidate 017 semantic compaction](../../experiments/candidates/017-contract-preserving-semantic-compaction.md).
- **Strongest nulls:** ordinary mutable intermediate representation plus compiler, transactions, checkpoint and rollback, staged rollout, simulated annealing, differentiable structure search followed by pruning, and periodic global rebuild.
- **Why no new `P-`:** the normalized operation is already temporary state before expensive structural commitment. Biomineralization adds a physical manifestation and conversion-failure test.

### Falsifiable measurable prediction

A precursor-then-convert arm should beat direct early commitment only when important constraints arrive before conversion, precursor edits are cheaper than durable edits, and conversion preserves protected function. It must lose when constraints arrive after conversion, the precursor consumes excessive memory or energy, conversion creates defects, or a conventional mutable IR and compiler matches it.

Report edits before and after commitment, invalid intermediate states, final task quality, conversion seconds, conversion failures, rollback success, retained bytes, runtime work saved after conversion, and lifecycle joules. Include a never-commit arm to expose whether the durable phase adds any value.

## Compact fixture proposal: ONS-X01 hybrid mechanism discrimination

This is a proposal, not a fixture file or result. It is deliberately one fixture with five isolated tracks and a shared measurement shell; there is no kitchen-sink arm that combines all mechanisms.

### Common contract

For each track:

1. generate development, confirmation, and transfer worlds from disjoint seeds and mechanism parameters;
2. freeze generators, arm implementations, budgets, metrics, exclusions, and decision rules before confirmation reveal;
3. pair every arm on the same world, arrival sequence, faults, resource boundaries, and measurement provider;
4. equalize task-quality targets, CPU/GPU access, memory, persistent bytes, communication, tuning opportunities, and wall-time ceiling;
5. charge transition, carrier, movement, validation, restore, failed work, fallback, maintenance, and retained-state costs to the invoking arm;
6. report energy in joules only with a calibrated external measurement boundary; otherwise report provider unavailable plus CPU/GPU time and bytes; and
7. retain a mechanism only if it improves a held-out Pareto frontier over the strongest eligible ordinary null and fails in the predicted negative-control regime.

### Shared endpoints

Every track reports a native outcome vector, never one “bio-inspired efficiency” score:

- accepted task utility and protected task loss per eligible task;
- false, missed, unsafe, or invalid actions per eligible event;
- p50 and p95 service, transition, restore, and recovery time in seconds;
- bytes retained, moved, and transmitted, plus messages and topology edits;
- failed work, retries, fallback steps, and unavailable service seconds;
- measured energy in joules by component and boundary, or explicit unavailability;
- peak memory bytes, persistent byte-seconds, and hardware/device wear where applicable; and
- operator person-minutes for configuration, incident handling, repair, and validation.

### Analysis and rejection

Use paired world-level contrasts with uncertainty that keeps repeated events within a world clustered. Select the strongest eligible null on development worlds only. Confirmation tests the candidate against that frozen null; transfer changes the source-defining boundary rather than merely resampling noise.

Reject a mechanism-specific artificial claim if any of the following occurs:

1. the candidate wins only when its source-defining negative control is absent from evaluation;
2. an ordinary composed null reaches the same quality–risk–resource frontier;
3. the apparent saving excludes transition, transport, storage, restore, fallback, or human work;
