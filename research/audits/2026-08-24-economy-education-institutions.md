## Evidence-typing firewall

Every substantive row or generated result in this audit carries exactly one
primary type and may cite other types only as constraints.

1. **`E` -- empirical finding:** an observed association, experiment, or
   estimated effect for named units, period, setting, measures, and design.
2. **`I` -- identification statement:** assumptions under which a contrast is
   causal, comparable, transportable, or not identified.
3. **`D` -- institutional/statistical definition:** a category, role, account,
   competence, qualification, curriculum layer, or process stage created by a
   declared institution or measurement system.
4. **`N` -- normative requirement:** a legal duty, right, prohibition,
   recommendation, or policy objective. It says what must or should happen, not
   what empirically works.
5. **`P` -- project proposal:** a record schema, synthetic generator, metric,
   architecture mapping, or hypothesis introduced by this project.

Forbidden promotions include `N -> E` (a right proves effectiveness), `D -> E`
(an official category is a natural kind), `E -> N` (an average effect decides a
right), `E -> I` (fit proves identification), and `P -> E` (a clean simulation
is scientific confirmation).

## Executive finding

These fields add useful operators, not one new metaphor:

1. **fiscal observation has multiple ledgers:** legal liability, cash receipt,
   accrual, national-account classification, financing, and economic incidence
   can point to different actors, dates, and quantities;
2. **organizations implement bundles through people and time:** a formal
   practice can be offered, adopted, enacted, combined, abandoned, or diffused,
   and its effect can depend on complementary practices and scarce managerial
   attention;
3. **labour markets are transition systems:** employment, unemployment, and
   non-participation are stocks under a reference definition, while hires,
   separations, entries, exits, occupation changes, task changes, hours, pay,
   and job quality are distinct flows or attributes;
4. **aggregate policy effects require an identified shock and horizon:** a
   budget identity or time-series innovation is not automatically an
   intervention, and anticipation, automatic responses, regime, prices,
   aggregation, and general-equilibrium feedback can change an estimated
   response;
5. **education systems have layers:** intended curriculum, implementation,
   opportunity to learn, performed assessment, score, interpretation, use,
   attained learning, delayed retention, transfer, progression, and later
   outcomes are not interchangeable;
6. **inclusion is a protected vector:** access, presence, participation,
   belonging, support, learning, progression, qualification, transition,
   voice, and remedy can move differently; and
7. **vocational competence is distributed:** workplace tasks, vocational
   school, training regulation, trainer and venue capability, assessment,
   competent bodies, employer commitment, and technology change jointly bound
   what a certificate can support.

The AI translation is a set of **typed transition and evidence contracts**.
They may reduce false inference by preserving who defined a state, what event
changed it, which implementation occurred, which outcome was measured, which
authority applied, and where transport fails. They earn no promotion unless
they beat complete public-finance, organizational, labour, causal, educational
measurement, inclusive-governance, and VET nulls at equal lifecycle cost.

- **Education:** dedicated entry added for system layers, curriculum,
  assessment, inclusion, comparative systems, and VET. Early childhood,
  higher education, adult education, teacher education, subject didactics,
  educational technology, language education, and most special-education
  practice remain incomplete.
- **Migration:** no new general migration claim. Stocks, flows, residence,
  event histories, selection, networks, and authority were already treated in

  institutional-transport problem.
- **Comparative law:** no legal-mechanism claim. Law supplies versioned
  competence, applicability, authority, right, review, and remedy fields; legal
  comparison itself requires qualified jurisdiction-specific work.

## Terminology firewall

1. A **statutory taxpayer** is legally required to remit a tax; the
   **economic incidence** concerns changes in real resources or welfare after
   behavioural and market adjustment.
2. **Tax revenue**, **tax liability**, **cash receipt**, **ESA accrual**, and
   **tax expenditure** use different operators and dates.
3. A government **budget balance**, an ESA **net lending/net borrowing** item,
   and a public-sector financing need can differ by perimeter and accounting.
4. A fiscal **identity** constrains accounts; it does not identify a causal
   effect, optimum, or sustainable path.
5. A management practice can be **prescribed**, **available**, **adopted**,
   **enacted**, **routinized**, **abandoned**, or **diffused**. These are not one
   Boolean installation state.
6. A bundle effect is not the sum of its components when practices are
   complements, substitutes, sequenced, capacity-constrained, or measured with
   common error.
7. A worker's **labour-force status** is a reference-period statistical
   classification; it is not their contract type, livelihood security, hours,
   income adequacy, job quality, capability, or preference.
8. An employment **stock** is counted at a time; a hire, separation, entry,
   exit, promotion, task change, or migration event is a flow over an interval.
9. A **job**, **occupation**, **task**, **skill**, **qualification**, and
   **worker** are different units. Technology can change tasks inside a stable
   occupational label.
10. **Registered unemployment** and EU-LFS/ILO **unemployment** are different
    administrative and statistical constructs.
11. A time-series **innovation**, a narratively identified policy action, an
    eligibility exposure, and a realized intervention are not synonyms for a
    policy shock.
12. A fiscal **multiplier** is an estimand with numerator, denominator, price
    basis, aggregation, horizon, discounting, regime, and identification
    method; it is not a constant attached to “spending.”
13. The **intended curriculum** is an authorized expectation; the
    **implemented curriculum** is what institutions and teachers make
    available; **opportunity to learn** is what a learner can actually engage;
    the **attained curriculum** is inferred from evidence of learning.
14. A learning **outcome statement**, an educational experience, a test item,
    a score, a qualification, and workplace competence are distinct artifacts.
15. **Reliability** concerns score consistency under a declared replication
    universe. **Validity** concerns support for a proposed interpretation and
    use. Neither is a permanent property of a test name.
16. A score comparison requires enough construct, item, administration,
    language, access, scale, and population comparability for that use.
17. **Assessment for learning**, certification, selection, accountability,
    system monitoring, and research are different uses with different error
    consequences.
18. **Inclusion** is not placement in a common building. Access, presence,
    participation, voice, support, belonging, learning, progression, and
    remedy remain measurable separately.
19. **Reasonable accommodation**, universal design, individualized support,
    modified content, and a changed target construct are different operations.
20. German **dual VET** distributes learning across at least company and
    vocational-school venues under distinct legal and governance routes;
    “dual” is not merely a blend of online and offline lessons.
21. An EQF or DQR **level** is a qualifications-reference relation, not proof
    that two curricula, occupations, holders, or legal entitlements are
    identical.
22. **Credential recognition**, qualification comparability, labour-market
    acceptance, and demonstrated task performance are separate decisions.
23. A **country** or “education system” label is not a treatment. Tracking,
    finance, curriculum, selection, teacher labour, language, welfare,
    qualification, and labour-market institutions form changing bundles.
24. An immigrant or migrant-background category is not an explanation. The
    relevant language, prior curriculum, legal status, resources, selection,
    timing, and institutional treatment must be measured directly where lawful
    and justified.

## Evidence synthesis

### 1. Public finance: the remitter, account, and bearer are different

**Empirical and theoretical evidence (`E/I`).** Harberger's general-equilibrium
analysis made corporate-tax incidence depend on sectoral production,
substitution, and factor mobility rather than the corporation named on the tax
form. Its historical model is not a current EU incidence estimate. Its durable
contribution is the non-identity between statutory remittance and equilibrium
burden ([doi:10.1086/258636](https://doi.org/10.1086/258636)).

**Institutional definition (`D`).** ESA 2010 establishes common sectors,
transactions, accrual rules, consolidation, and accounts for EU statistics.
Destatis separately explains that German quarterly cash statistics and ESA
net lending/net borrowing can differ because their perimeters and methods
differ. The 2025 German GNI inventory describes the sources and transformations
used for ESA compilation. A revision is therefore a versioned measurement
event, not evidence that the previous number was fraud or that the revised
number is causally sufficient.

**Normative requirement (`N`).** Regulation (EU) 2024/1263 and German Basic
Law Arts. 104a--109 allocate fiscal-governance and federal responsibilities.
They do not decide who economically bears a tax or whether a particular
spending change caused output.

**Proposed AI translation (`P`).** Maintain a fiscal event with separate
fields for legal authority, liable/remitting actor, tax base, rate/version,
cash date, accrual date, receiving government, ESA code, consolidation
perimeter, behavioural response model, incidence population, price basis,
horizon, distributional statistic, uncertainty, and revision lineage.

**Efficiency mechanism (`P`).** A typed event can prevent repeated conversion
and invalid joins between legal, cash, ESA, and incidence tables. It is useful
only if the avoided false claims and reconciliation work exceed schema,
classification, and review cost.

**Evidence status and measurable prediction.** The accounting distinctions and
model-dependence of incidence are established. Any claimed burden allocation is
model- and evidence-qualified. In `EEI-T01`, the typed record should reduce
wrong-bearer and wrong-period claims relative to a complete microsimulation and
account-reconciliation null, not merely relative to a cash-table baseline.

**Failure modes.** Statutory-incidence realism; cash/accrual mixing; perimeter
drift; treating an identity as a behavioural model; double-counting transfers;
undisclosed price deflator; ignoring open-economy responses; converting a legal
allocation into a welfare judgement; and hiding revisions.

### 2. Management and organization: adoption is a trajectory, not a switch

**Empirical evidence (`E`).** Bloom and colleagues randomized free management
consulting among a small sample of large Indian textile plants. The studied
package changed adoption and measured plant outcomes; it did not estimate a
universal “management quality” coefficient. Nine years later, about half of the
originally adopted practices had been dropped, while a treatment--control gap
remained; reported causes included managerial turnover and limited director
time ([doi:10.1093/qje/qjs044](https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjs044);
[doi:10.1257/app.20180369](https://doi.org/10.1257/app.20180369)). Ichniowski,
Shaw, and Prennushi's longitudinal steel-line study associates coherent work-
practice systems with productivity and reports little benefit from isolated
practices in that setting. It is strong process-specific evidence, not a
randomized universal complementarity theorem.

**Institutional and method boundary (`D/I`).** `C-1346` already separates
formal rules, accounts of routines, and performed routines. `C-1370` already
separates a policy rule from capacity, discretion, burden, take-up, and service.
The residue here is longitudinal: offered, adopted, enacted, combined,
maintained, abandoned, and diffused states need event time, role, dependency,
and resource records.

**Normative requirement (`N`).** German Works Constitution Act provisions can
require information, consultation, expertise, codetermination, or training in
specified workplace contexts. Directive (EU) 2024/2831 adds scoped platform-
work rules for algorithmic management. These are authority gates, not evidence
that a management bundle improves productivity.

**Proposed AI translation (`P`).** Represent each system practice as a
versioned routine with intended operation, prerequisites, complementary and
conflicting routines, responsible roles, training, actual invocations,
exceptions, capacity, outcome channel, adoption/abandonment events, turnover,
and diffusion path.

**Efficiency mechanism (`P`).** Dependency-aware rollout can avoid spending on
isolated practices whose complements or maintainers are absent. It may also
localize whether failure came from design, adoption, execution, persistence,
or outcome measurement.

**Evidence status and measurable prediction.** Scoped organizational effects
and persistence loss are established for the cited samples. Cross-domain
translation is plausible. `EEI-T02` requires better complement detection,
failure localization, and persistence forecasts than factorial, survival,
process-mining, and resource-aware workflow nulls.

**Failure modes.** Consultant-offer equals treatment; practice-count scalar;
common-method bias; bundle coefficient exported intact; productivity without
quality or worker outcomes; ceremonial adoption; turnover omitted; beneficial
workaround labelled noncompliance; rights treated as optimization costs; and
documentation volume used as success.

### 3. Labour and employment: count transitions and changing tasks

**Institutional definition (`D`).** Regulation (EU) 2019/1700 and the EU-LFS
methodology classify people using a reference population, week, age range, work
activity, job attachment, search, and availability. Destatis explicitly
distinguishes ILO unemployment from registered unemployment. Labour-market
flows link adjacent states; they are not recoverable from one net rate alone.

For three labour-force states -- employed $E$, unemployed $U$, and outside the
labour force $O$ -- a transition matrix can satisfy

$$
\mathbf{n}_{t+1}=\mathbf{P}_{t}^{\mathsf T}\mathbf{n}_{t}+\mathbf{b}_t,
$$

where $\mathbf{n}_t$ is a vector in **persons**, $P_{rs,t}$ is a probability of
moving from state $r$ to state $s$ over the declared interval, and
$\mathbf{b}_t$ contains population-entry, exit, and coverage changes in
persons. The same $\mathbf{n}_{t+1}-\mathbf{n}_t$ can arise from very different
gross transitions.

**Empirical evidence (`E`).** Autor, Levy, and Murnane operationalized tasks
inside occupations and found, in their historical US samples, computerization
associated with less routine-manual/routine-cognitive input and more
non-routine cognitive input. This is evidence that task composition can change
inside nominal categories, not a timeless task taxonomy or current EU causal
forecast ([doi:10.1162/003355303322552801](https://doi.org/10.1162/003355303322552801)).
Matching models likewise distinguish vacancy, search, job creation, job
destruction, and worker transitions under explicit assumptions.

**Normative requirement (`N`).** Equal treatment, workplace participation,
information, review, safety, and data-protection duties attach to actors and
decisions in scope. An employment statistic or synthetic utility function
cannot waive them.

**Proposed AI translation (`P`).** For adaptive systems, distinguish module or
worker existence, availability, assignment, active work, idle attachment,
search, transition, exit, task vector, capability evidence, workload, reward,
quality, and authority. Never infer individual capability from occupation or
employment state alone.

**Efficiency mechanism (`P`).** A state-flow-task ledger can distinguish real
capacity creation from relabeling, churn, hours intensification, or task
substitution, reducing mistaken scaling decisions.

**Evidence status and measurable prediction.** Official definitions are
established; task effects are sample- and period-bounded. `EEI-T03` should
reconstruct stocks, gross flows, task change, and uncertainty more accurately
than a complete multistate/task model before any project-specific record is
retained.

**Failure modes.** Net-rate realism; one-hour employment equals secure work;
registered/ILO conflation; person/job/contract confusion; stable occupation
equals stable task; vacancies equal feasible matches; wage equals welfare;
hours and unpaid work omitted; selective attrition; and real employment
decisions inferred from a synthetic test.

### 4. Development and macroeconomics: identify exposure before response

**Identification evidence (`I`).** Blanchard and Perotti used institutional
timing and automatic-response assumptions to identify fiscal innovations in a
structural VAR. Mertens and Ravn show analytically that anticipation can make a
standard information set non-fundamental and develop an estimator for their
stated fiscal model. The disagreement across approaches is not noise to delete;
it identifies shock timing and information as part of the estimand
([doi:10.1111/j.1468-0297.2010.02361.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1468-0297.2010.02361.x)).

A cumulative response multiplier may be written

$$
m_h=
\frac{\sum_{k=0}^{h}d_k\,\Delta Y_{t+k}}
     {\sum_{k=0}^{h}d_k\,\Delta G_{t+k}},
$$

where $\Delta Y$ and $\Delta G$ are changes in real euros per period under a
declared counterfactual, $d_k$ is a unitless discount or present-value weight,
and $h$ is a horizon in quarters or years. $m_h$ has units **euros of output per
euro of spending**. Price base, population scaling, shock, estimator, regime,
and denominator support are part of the value.

**Development evidence (`E/I`).** Duflo exploited variation in Indonesian
school-construction intensity across regions and exposure across cohorts. The
primary study estimates schooling and wage effects for its programme and
design, not a portable return to buildings, “education,” or infrastructure
([doi:10.1257/aer.91.4.795](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.91.4.795)). Concurrent
programmes, migration, spillovers, labour demand, cohort composition, and later
institutional conditions remain possible transport limits.

**Proposed AI translation (`P`).** A policy-effect record must bind shock or
assignment provenance, announcement and implementation dates, automatic rules,
agents' information set, exposure unit, treatment version, interference,
accounting and price basis, horizon, regime, estimator, identification
assumptions, support, outcomes, uncertainty, and revision.

**Efficiency mechanism (`P`).** Explicit shock lineage can prevent expensive
retraining or policy switching in response to predictable, endogenous, or
mis-timed variation. It also prevents a one-period metric from replacing a
dynamic response vector.

**Evidence status and measurable prediction.** The identification boundaries
are established; the project record is speculative. `EEI-T04` must reduce
impulse-response error, sign mistakes, and false cross-regime transport beyond
complete SVAR, narrative/event, local-projection, and sensitivity nulls.

**Failure modes.** Identity-as-causality; innovation-as-policy; fiscal
foresight omitted; automatic stabilizers called discretionary; nominal/real or
level/rate mixing; horizon shopping; one-country multiplier exported;
equilibrium feedback omitted; cohort exposure misclassified; and post-treatment
migration conditioned away.

### 5. Curriculum: standard, delivery, opportunity, and learning form a chain

**Institutional definition (`D`).** The IEA curriculum model distinguishes
intended, implemented, and attained curriculum. TIMSS frameworks separately
specify content/cognitive domains and collect contextual information. KMK
states that German education standards take effect only through Land
implementation and everyday school practice, and that appropriate tests
examine the extent to which competencies are reached. These statements define
layers and monitoring purposes; they do not prove a causal effect of a standard.

**Empirical evidence (`E`).** Schmidt, Wang, and McKnight operationalize
curriculum coherence across topic sequences in mathematics and science
standards. Their cross-system content analysis is evidence about represented
standards, not proof that one sequence causes learning for every learner.

For learner $i$, content element $c$, and interval $t$, let
$O_{ict}$ be **minutes of accessible opportunity**, $A_{ict}$ attempted tasks,
$F_{ict}$ feedback bits, and $R_{ic}(h)$ retained performance after horizon
$h$ days. None can be replaced by a binary “in curriculum” field. A simple
coverage statistic

$$
C_{it}=\frac{\sum_c w_c\,\mathbb{1}(O_{ict}>0)}{\sum_c w_c}
$$

is unitless and depends on declared nonnegative content weights $w_c$ with
$\sum_c w_c>0$; $\mathbb{1}(\cdot)$ is the unitless indicator function. It
does not measure depth, quality, retention, transfer, or inclusion.

**Proposed AI translation (`P`).** Store intended dependency graph, authorized
version, implemented sequence, accessible opportunities, help, attempts,
feedback, assessment sampling, acquired evidence, delayed retention, transfer,
cost, and who changed each layer.

**Efficiency mechanism (`P`).** The chain localizes whether a performance gap
comes from missing content, inaccessible opportunity, weak acquisition,
forgetting, assessment mismatch, or transfer failure, allowing targeted rather
than blanket retraining.

**Evidence status and measurable prediction.** Layer distinctions are
established; an optimal coherence or sequence is not. `EEI-T05` must beat
curriculum mapping plus knowledge tracing and learning-science schedulers on
stage attribution and held-out delayed transfer at equal opportunity and cost.

**Failure modes.** Policy text equals instruction; offered equals accessible;
coverage equals mastery; standards narrowed to tested samples; sequence without
learner state; teacher work omitted; inaccessible materials treated as
noncompliance by the learner; and increased test alignment mistaken for broad
transfer.

### 6. Assessment: validate the inference and the use

**Measurement principle (`I`).** Kane's argument-based approach makes the
proposed interpretation and use the object of validation. Scoring,
generalization, extrapolation, and decision claims require their own evidence;
validating one interpretation does not validate every use
([doi:10.1111/jedm.12000](https://doi.org/10.1111/jedm.12000)). The PISA 2022
technical report documents sampling, scaling, population modelling, item-
parameter invariance, linking, and uncertainty. Its work demonstrates how much
infrastructure comparability requires, not that every national ranking or
policy attribution is valid.

A two-parameter logistic item model illustrates the boundary:

$$
\Pr(X_{ij}=1\mid\theta_i)
=\left[1+\exp\{-a_j(\theta_i-b_j)\}\right]^{-1},
$$

where $X_{ij}$ is a scored response, $\theta_i$ is position on the declared
latent scale, $a_j$ is item discrimination per scale unit, and $b_j$ is item
difficulty in scale units. Fit does not establish that $\theta$ is the desired
construct, that parameters are invariant across language/access groups, or
that a threshold use is fair and beneficial.

**Normative requirement (`N`).** Education and employment uses can trigger
rights, equal-treatment, accessibility, data-protection, AI Act, Land school-
law, examination, and appeal requirements. Psychometric validity does not
grant legal authority; legal authority does not validate a score.

**Proposed AI translation (`P`).** Bind task sample, target construct,
administration/access mode, scorer, item/model version, response process,
reliability universe, group-comparability evidence, score, uncertainty,
interpretation, proposed use, decision rule, consequence monitoring, review,
appeal, and expiry.

**Efficiency mechanism (`P`).** One validated low-ambition signal can be reused
only for compatible inferences; invalid high-stakes reuse is rejected early.
Targeted recalibration can replace full reassessment when dependency tests pass.

**Evidence status and measurable prediction.** The validity boundary is
established. `EEI-T06` must reduce invalid-use approvals and decision regret
beyond IRT/DIF, generalizability, calibration, and human validity-argument
nulls while maintaining calibrated uncertainty.

**Failure modes.** Test-is-valid shorthand; reliability equals validity;
score equals trait; common scale without invariance; language/access effects
called ability; plausible values treated as individual diagnoses; cut-score
precision invented; system monitor used for individual selection; consequences
unmeasured; and appeal omitted.

### 7. Inclusion: optimize no scalar that can hide exclusion

**Normative requirement (`N`).** UN CRPD Article 24, concluded by the EU through
Decision 2010/48/EC, retains non-exclusion, equal access, reasonable
accommodation, support within general education, individualized support, and
academic and social development. The 2022 Council Recommendation on Pathways
to School Success calls for integrated prevention, intervention, and
compensation, combining universal and targeted measures. The recommendation is
non-binding; the Convention and applicable EU/German/Land law require separate
competence analysis.

**Empirical evidence (`E`).** Ruijs and Peetsma's review found heterogeneous
and sometimes ambiguous academic and socio-emotional evidence across inclusion
studies. It does not establish one placement effect and cannot decide rights.

Represent inclusion outcomes as

$$
\mathbf{I}_{it}=(A,P_r,P_a,B,S,L,G,Q,T,V,R),
$$

where $A$, $P_r$, $P_a$, $B$, $S$, $L$, $G$, $Q$, $T$, $V$, and $R$
respectively represent accessible offer, presence, participation, belonging,
support received, learning, progression, qualification, transition, voice, and
remedy. Components retain their own units -- proportions, days,
minutes, validated scale points, events, or resolution time. A scalar requires
declared weights and protected minimums; without them the sum is undefined.

**Proposed AI translation (`P`).** Apply the vector to every adaptive learning
or work system: access and help paths, actual participation, task opportunity,
performance, retention, transfer, burden, refusal, voice, review, and remedy
remain separately queryable.

**Efficiency mechanism (`P`).** Universal access paths can reduce repeated
special-case engineering, while typed individualized support targets residual
needs. Efficiency is accepted only after protected access and outcome floors.

**Evidence status and measurable prediction.** The rights and measurement
separation are established; comparative intervention effectiveness is mixed.
`EEI-T07` must detect hidden exclusion and prevent average-improving subgroup
harm beyond a mature multidimensional dashboard, missingness analysis,
accessibility audit, and rights review.

**Failure modes.** Placement equals inclusion; mean-score welfare; support
offer equals receipt; accommodation erased as unfair advantage; modified
construct compared as invariant; diagnostic label predicts need; voice token
without influence; missing excluded learners; universal design claimed to
remove individualized support; and efficiency overriding a protected floor.

### 8. Vocational education: competence spans venues, standards, and commitment

**Institutional definition (`D`).** BBiG sections 1--6 distinguish objectives,
venues, venue cooperation, recognized occupations, training regulations, and
experimentation. Further provisions govern contracts, trainer and venue
suitability, examinations, competent bodies, continuing training, and learner
representation. BIBB's 2025 account describes joint development of training
regulations involving the federal government, Länder, and social partners.
Vocational schools remain under Land law. The EU VET Recommendation and EQF
are coordination frameworks, not substitutes for these legal routes.

**Empirical/model evidence (`E/I`).** Dustmann and Schönberg model incomplete
contractibility of complex firm training and compare commitment settings. The
paper supports employer commitment as one mechanism for firm-based provision;
it does not prove that every German apprenticeship supplies the regulated
content or that its estimates transport unchanged
([doi:10.1257/app.4.2.36](https://doi.org/10.1257/app.4.2.36)).

For required task $k$, venue $v$, and period $t$, let $H_{kvt}$ be supervised
practice hours, $Q_{kvt}$ independently scored execution quality, and
$D_{kvt}$ days since verified practice. A certificate-level coverage claim
requires a declared aggregation over critical tasks; total hours alone allow
zero coverage of a critical task.

**Proposed AI translation (`P`).** Treat training of persistent specialists as
a contract across simulated work, structured instruction, supervised practice,
qualified evaluator, versioned capability standard, independent examination,
training-provider commitment, task evidence, transfer, and renewal under task
change.

**Efficiency mechanism (`P`).** Work-embedded practice can produce useful
output while training, and shared occupational standards can reduce bespoke
assessment. Both gains disappear if productive work crowds out critical
learning or the standard lags task change.

**Evidence status and measurable prediction.** The German institutional
structure is established; the commitment mechanism is model- and comparison-
qualified. `EEI-T08` must detect under-training, stale standards, and venue
gaps beyond conventional competency matrices, workplace logs, external exams,
and quality assurance.

**Failure modes.** Certificate equals current competence; productive hours
equal training; employer report as independent exam; school/workplace
duplication ignored; venue conflict hidden; trainer capability assumed;
technology change not versioned; learner rights treated as overhead; and
country label exported as architecture.

### 9. Comparative education and institutions: transport bundles, not labels

**Empirical evidence (`E`).** Hanushek and Wößmann compare changes between
primary and secondary international assessments across earlier- and later-
tracking systems and report greater inequality associated with early tracking
in their design. The unit is a country/system contrast assembled from multiple
assessments, not a randomized tracking age; cohort, measurement, composition,
other institutional differences, and later reforms bound the result
([doi:10.1111/j.1468-0297.2006.01076.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1468-0297.2006.01076.x)).

**Institutional definition (`D`).** EQF levels describe learning outcomes to
support transparency. They do not automatically recognize a regulated
profession, equate curricula, establish individual competence, or carry every
legal effect across jurisdictions.

**Identification boundary (`I`).** A transported estimate requires a defined
source and target, comparable measures, sufficient overlap, relevant effect
modifiers, treatment-version compatibility, and defensible interference and
selection assumptions. Migration background can enter these conditions but
must not serve as a causal placeholder for language, prior opportunity,
resources, legal position, or institutional treatment.

**Proposed AI translation (`P`).** Store system components -- governance,
funding, selection/tracking, curriculum, teacher/trainer labour, qualification,
language/access, welfare supports, labour demand, rights, and data operator --
rather than a country token. Transport only the components and relations with
measured support.

**Efficiency mechanism (`P`).** Component-level transport can reuse validated
subsystems without copying an entire institutional bundle or repeating a
failed comparison. The extra measurement may cost more than local evaluation.

**Evidence status and measurable prediction.** The cited association is
bounded; general transport is not established. `EEI-T09` must reduce target-
system effect error and unsupported equivalence claims beyond multilevel,
measurement-invariance, overlap, and transport-sensitivity nulls.

**Failure modes.** Country essentialism; level-code equivalence; PISA rank as
system quality; tracking age isolated from bundle; migrant category as
mechanism; language and accommodation omitted; source estimate exported across
labour demand; legal recognition inferred from learning outcomes; and no
target-support refusal.

## Mathematical and dimensional frame

### Fiscal accounts and incidence

For government perimeter $s$ and period $t$, use the accounting identity

$$
\Delta D_{s,t}=G_{s,t}+TR_{s,t}+i_{s,t}D_{s,t-1}-T_{s,t}-OR_{s,t}+A_{s,t},
$$

where $D_{s,t}$ is a debt stock in **euros** at the period boundary;
$\Delta D_{s,t}$ is its change in euros; $G$, $TR$, $T$, $OR$, and $A$ are,
respectively, purchases, transfers, tax receipts on the declared accounting
basis, other revenue, and stock--flow adjustments in **euros over period**
$t$; and $i_{s,t}$ is the unitless effective interest fraction over that
period, so $i_{s,t}D_{s,t-1}$ is interest in euros. This is a reconciliation
identity after perimeter and basis are fixed. It contains no behavioural
response.

For population group $g$, let

$$
I_g(h)=-\frac{EV_g^{\mathrm{reform}}(h)-EV_g^{\mathrm{base}}(h)}{N_g},
$$

where $EV$ is equivalent variation in real euros over horizon $h$ and $N_g$ is
persons. $I_g$ is **real euros per person over $h$**. It depends on preferences,
prices, incomes, market equilibrium, public-service treatment, and model. It is
not generally equal to tax remitted by group $g$.

### Organizational complementarity and persistence

For binary practice states $z_{kot}\in\{0,1\}$ in organization $o$ at time
$t$, a diagnostic factorial model is

$$
Y_{ot}=\alpha_o+\delta_t+\sum_k\beta_k z_{kot}
+\sum_{k<\ell}\gamma_{k\ell}z_{kot}z_{\ell ot}
+\mathbf{r}_{ot}^{\mathsf T}\boldsymbol\rho+\varepsilon_{ot}.
$$

$Y$ retains its native unit, such as conforming units per machine-hour;
$\alpha_o$ is an organization intercept in units of $Y$, $\delta_t$ is a period
effect in units of $Y$, and $\mathbf r$ records staffing, training minutes,
workload, and capital. Each coefficient $\rho_m$ has units of $Y$ per native
unit of resource component $r_m$, so
$\mathbf r^{\mathsf T}\boldsymbol\rho$ has units of $Y$. The residual
$\varepsilon$ also has units of $Y$. For binary $z$, $\beta$ and $\gamma$ have
units of $Y$. Interaction estimates are descriptive or causal only under the
design and model that generated them.

Let adoption state $S_{kot}\in\{\text{offered},\text{adopted},\text{enacted},\text{maintained},\text{abandoned}\}$.
Persistence at horizon $h$ is

$$
\pi_k(h)=\Pr(S_{ko,t+h}=\text{maintained}\mid S_{kot}=\text{enacted}),
$$

which is unitless; $h$ is a declared number of days, months, or periods and the
estimate requires censoring, turnover, and observation rules.

### Labour stocks, flows, and task content

Gross flow $F_{rs,t}$ is persons moving from state $r$ to $s$ during the
interval. A stock reconciliation for state $s$ is

$$
n_{s,t+1}-n_{s,t}=\sum_{r\ne s}F_{rs,t}-\sum_{r\ne s}F_{sr,t}+b_{s,t},
$$

where $n_{s,t}$ is the stock in state $s$ at boundary $t$, $F_{rs,t}$ is the
gross flow from state $r$ to $s$ during the interval, and $b_{s,t}$ is net
entry, exit, or coverage change not represented by an $r\rightarrow s$ flow,
all in **persons**. A rate divides by an explicitly named risk stock and is
unitless per interval.

For job $j$, normalized task share $q_{jk,t}$ satisfies $q_{jk,t}\ge0$ and
$\sum_k q_{jk,t}=1$. Task displacement is a vector
$\Delta\mathbf q_{j,t}$, not a count of jobs “automated.” Hours, pay, quality,
and persons must be reported separately.

### Education exposure, evidence, and inclusion

For learner $i$ and required content/task $c$, define a typed evidence vector

$$
\mathbf L_{ic}(h)=
(O_{ic},A_{ic},F_{ic},X_{ic},R_{ic}(h),T_{ic},U_{ic}),
$$

where $O$ is accessible opportunity in minutes, $A$ is an event count of
attempts, $F$ is feedback in recorded events or encoded bits, $X$ is immediate
performance in declared score units, $R(h)$ is retained performance in the
same or explicitly linked score units after $h$ days, $T$ is performance in
declared score units on a named transfer stratum, and $U$ is uncertainty in
the units of its associated estimate. These components cannot be summed
without a measurement and value model.

For protected inclusion component $k$, define a floor $l_k$ in the component's
native unit. A policy is admissible only if

$$
\Pr(I_{ik}<l_k\mid g)\le\epsilon_{kg}
$$

for every predeclared group/context $g$. Here $I_{ik}$ is component $k$ in its
native unit for person/trajectory $i$, $l_k$ is the protected floor in the
same unit, and $\epsilon_{kg}\in[0,1]$ is the maximum permitted violation
probability for component $k$ in context $g$. The denominator, missingness
process, and uncertainty must be specified. The floors are governance inputs,
not learned from average reward.

### Transport contract

If $S=1$ denotes a source system and $S=0$ a target, a conventional transported
mean under action $a$ may require

$$
\mu_T(a)=\int \mathbb E[Y\mid A=a,X=x,S=1],dF_T(x).
$$

Here $A$ is a versioned action, $X$ is a vector of measured effect modifiers,
$Y$ is an outcome in its native unit, $F_T$ is the unitless target-population
probability distribution over $X$, and $\mu_T(a)$ has the same unit as $Y$.
The expression is identified only with compatible outcome/action versions,
conditional exchangeability/transport assumptions, target overlap,
measurement comparability, and an interference model. Country or migrant-
background labels do not supply those conditions.

## CPU-only synthetic falsification programme

### Common execution contract

All nine protocols are executable descriptions for a later workstation harness.
No protocol is presently marked workstation-ready by this audit alone.

1. **Runtime:** CPython, R, or Node implementation; CPU only; deterministic
   pseudorandom generator recorded by name/version; at most 4 worker threads,
   8 GiB peak resident memory, no network, no accelerator, and no real data.
2. **Seed namespaces:** unit/smoke seeds `0`--`7`; discovery seeds
   `11000`--`11031`; tuning seeds `21000`--`21015`; sealed confirmation seeds
   `2026082400`--`2026082527` (128 paired worlds); independent replication
   seeds `2027082400`--`2027082463` (64 paired worlds) on a second generator
   family. Confirmation and replication seeds are never used for tuning.
3. **Arms:** `A` is a deliberately common but incomplete baseline; `B` is the
   complete mature domain null; `C` is the proposed typed record plus the same
   estimator budget available to `B`. Only `C-B` supports retention. Beating
   `A` alone has no promotion value.
4. **Equal budget:** identical generated observations and oracle truth access;
   identical training/tuning labels; wall-clock cap 20 minutes per seed;
   report CPU seconds, peak bytes, output bytes, and synthetic reviewer actions.
   `C` must remain at or below 1.25 times `B` CPU and peak memory and 1.50 times
   its stored bytes unless a preregistered Pareto analysis shows a protected
   error reduction that no cheaper point attains.
5. **Inference:** seed is the paired independent unit. Report mean/median
   paired contrasts, 95% studentized bootstrap confidence intervals with
   10,000 resamples, and the full distribution. Holm control at familywise
   $\alpha=0.05$ covers the nine primary `C-B` tests. No within-world row is
   treated as an independent replication.
6. **Common promotion threshold:** unless a protocol states a stronger gate,
   `C` must reduce its primary consequential false-claim rate by at least 20%
   relative and 2 percentage points absolute versus `B`; the Holm-adjusted 95%
   interval must exclude zero; interval coverage must lie in 93%--97% overall
   and at least 90% in every preregistered support stratum; protected-harm or
   false-authority rate may not rise by more than 1 percentage point (upper
   one-sided 95% bound); and the result must repeat directionally with at least
   10% relative improvement in the second generator family.
7. **Immediate rejection:** oracle leakage; real-person data; post-confirmation
   tuning; dropped seeds; unreported crashes; incompatible units silently
   coerced; rights converted to a reward weight; a recommendation or model
   output treated as legal authorization; or any real individual decision.
8. **Artifacts per protocol:** `contract.json`, `generator.json`,
   `seed-manifest.csv`, `environment.json`, `budget.json`, `worlds.parquet` or a
   documented deterministic generator hash, `arm-output.jsonl`,
   `metrics-per-seed.csv`, `aggregate.json`, `assumption-failures.jsonl`,
   `rejections.jsonl`, `plots/*.svg`, `report.md`, and `SHA256SUMS`. Every
   artifact includes protocol, arm, seed, schema version, and code commit.
9. **Promotion:** a surviving record refines Candidates 009, 011, 014, 019, or
   020 only after the common confirmation and replication gates. It creates no
   principle. Failure against `B`, a resource-gate breach, or loss of a
   protected floor retires the proposed composition for that domain.

## Cross-track failure modes

1. **Legal payer realism:** the remitting actor is labelled the economic bearer.
2. **Fiscal-ledger collapse:** cash, accrual, ESA, budget, and financing values
   are joined without perimeter, time, or revision.
3. **Identity causality:** an accounting equality is read as a behavioural
   response or policy optimum.
4. **Distribution-free efficiency:** revenue or output changes omit who gains,
   loses, waits, or loses access.
5. **Practice-count management:** unrelated and dependent practices become one
   “maturity” score.
6. **Offer/adoption/enactment collapse:** consultancy, installation, log entry,
   actual routine, and durable use share one treatment flag.
7. **Complement deletion:** the effect of a bundle is assigned to its most
   visible component.
8. **Turnover-free persistence:** routine survival ignores maintainers,
   training, attention, exit, and censoring.
9. **Net-flow blindness:** stable employment hides simultaneous hires,
   separations, entry, exit, and churn.
10. **One-hour welfare:** statistical employment is called secure, adequate, or
    desired work.
11. **Occupation essentialism:** a title fixes task content, capability, or
    automation exposure.
12. **Innovation equals shock:** a residual or forecast error is treated as an
    exogenous intervention.
13. **Foresight deletion:** announcement, expectation, implementation, and
    response share one date.
14. **Multiplier constant:** a response estimate loses its price basis,
    denominator, horizon, regime, and identification.
15. **Building equals education:** infrastructure exposure is transported as a
    generic learning treatment.
16. **Curriculum textualism:** authorized content is assumed taught,
    accessible, attempted, learned, retained, and transferred.
17. **Coverage mastery:** a topic mention or assessment sample becomes
    competence.
18. **Test validity shorthand:** a reliable or fitted test is “valid” for every
    interpretation and use.
19. **Comparable label fallacy:** identical score, subject, qualification, or
    level names imply invariant constructs.
20. **Plausible-value individualization:** population-analysis draws are used
    as individual diagnoses.
21. **Mean-score inclusion:** excluded or unsupported learners disappear while
    the average improves.
22. **Placement inclusion:** physical presence substitutes for participation,
    belonging, support, learning, and voice.
23. **Accommodation penalty:** access support is scored as evidence against
    capability without checking the target construct.
24. **Universal-design sufficiency:** a shared access path is claimed to remove
    all individual support needs.
25. **Productive-work training:** output hours are counted as supervised
    practice of every required task.
26. **Certificate realism:** a qualification proves current performance,
    transfer, legal entitlement, and technology-version compatibility.
27. **Provider-root corroboration:** employer log, trainer report, and internal
    assessment are called independent evidence.
28. **Country essentialism:** a national label acts as treatment or mechanism.
29. **Migrant placeholder:** a migration category replaces language, prior
    curriculum, selection, resources, legal context, and institutional action.
30. **Level-code recognition:** an EQF/DQR relation is treated as automatic
    professional recognition or task equivalence.
31. **Law-as-evidence:** a duty or prohibition is presented as proof that a
    method improves outcomes.
32. **Evidence-as-law:** an average empirical effect is used to remove a right,
    review, accommodation, participation, or remedy.
33. **Synthetic authority:** simulated success triggers a real tax, work, or
    education decision.
34. **Documentation victory:** more fields win without fewer consequential
    errors at equal lifecycle cost.
