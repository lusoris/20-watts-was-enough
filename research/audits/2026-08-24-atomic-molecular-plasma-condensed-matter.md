## Executive finding

The durable result is a set of **anti-collapse rules**, not nine new analogies.

1. Precision spectroscopy measures transitions through a timed interrogation
   and intervention design. Common-mode rejection can suppress local-oscillator
   noise while leaving a shared systematic shift untouched.
2. Molecular transition frequencies form a weighted difference graph.
   Connected components have an arbitrary energy origin, redundant cycles test
   consistency, and a new edge is valuable according to the covariance or
   connectivity it changes.
3. Quantum measurements are valuable relative to a registered observable
   family. Randomized measurements can reuse shots for many bounded
   observables; they are not compressed full-state tomography, and their
   efficiency is controlled by the measurement channel and shadow norm.
4. A finite device, trapped gas, or lattice simulation can show finite-system
   order without establishing a thermodynamic phase. Size, boundary,
   inhomogeneity, equilibration, correlation length, and competing
   first-order/crossover hypotheses remain in the claim.
5. Quenched disorder creates a distribution of samples. Near some random fixed
   points the relative sample-to-sample width does not vanish, so one large
   sample or a disorder mean with an IID standard error is not enough.
6. A bulk topological invariant, a finite-sample marker, and an accessible
   transport service are different objects. Disorder, mobility or spectral
   gap, contacts, temperature, interactions, and finite size connect them.
7. Plasma line radiation and probe signals are outputs of non-equilibrium
   kinetics, atomic data, geometry, opacity, and a detector. A scalar
   “temperature” is conditional on an electron-energy distribution and
   collisional--radiative model.
8. Fast plasma control depends on a physics-constrained, latency-qualified
   state estimate. Vessel currents and unavailable internal profiles can make a
   low residual or fast learned map physically stale.
9. Beam size is a projection of phase space. Emittance and coupling require
   multiple optics or direct phase-space sampling; many high-information
   monitors are interceptive and therefore consume or perturb the observed
   bunch.

The systems translation is correspondingly narrow: preserve the signed
intervention matrix, difference graph, observable family, size/disorder
ensemble, kinetic forward model, reconstruction constraint, and measurement
cost until the downstream decision is complete. Selective allocation,
active sensing, structural offloading, state estimation, robust control,
provenance, and tiering already exist in P-001/P-007/P-009/P-010/P-012/P-013
and Candidates 006/007/009/012/014/017/018. The physical sciences strengthen
their nulls and schemas; they do not justify a new registry entry.

## Shared mathematical boundary

### Timed spectroscopy is a signed intervention model

For interrogation \(i\), write the fractional indication as

\[
y_i=\frac{\hat{\nu}_i-\nu_{\rm ref}}{\nu_{\rm ref}}
   = a_i\theta+\mathbf{x}_i^\mathsf{T}\boldsymbol{\beta}
   +\int g_i(t)\,\delta y_{\rm LO}(t)\,dt+\epsilon_i .
\]

Here \(\theta\) is the target fractional frequency offset, \(a_i\) is its signed
sensitivity, \(\mathbf{x}_i\) records reversals or influence quantities,
\(\boldsymbol{\beta}\) are systematic-shift coefficients, \(g_i(t)\) is the
interrogation sensitivity function, and \(\delta y_{\rm LO}\) is local-oscillator
fractional noise. Synchronous differencing can cancel the final integral when
two clocks share it. It cannot identify a shift that is also shared and
collinear with \(a_i\). Rank, conditioning, covariance, duty cycle, servo
history, and zero-support interventions therefore precede precision digits.

### Molecular spectra are weighted difference graphs

For transition edge \(e=(l,u)\),

\[
\tilde{\nu}_e = \frac{E_u-E_l}{hc}+\epsilon_e,\qquad
\mathbf{s}=\mathbf{B}\mathbf{e}+\boldsymbol{\epsilon},
\]

where \(\mathbf{B}\) is an oriented incidence matrix, energies in
\(\mathbf{e}\) are in cm\(^{-1}\), and \(\mathbf{s}\) contains measured
wavenumbers in cm\(^{-1}\). One origin must be fixed per connected component.
For any signed cycle vector \(\mathbf{c}\) satisfying
\(\mathbf{c}^{\mathsf T}\mathbf{B}=0\),

\[
\mathbf{c}^{\mathsf T}\mathbf{s}=0
\]

within covariance if assignments and measurement models are mutually
consistent. Robust weighted least squares can localize tension; it cannot
decide the correct physical label without spectroscopic evidence.

### Quantum efficiency is observable-relative

For state \(\rho\), measurement channel \(\mathcal M\), and observable \(O_j\),
a randomized outcome \(b_i,U_i\) gives a snapshot

\[
\hat{\rho}_i=\mathcal M^{-1}\!\left(
 U_i^\dagger |b_i\rangle\langle b_i|U_i\right),\qquad
\hat{o}_j=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathrm{Tr}(O_j\hat{\rho}_i).
\]

The useful shot count depends on error \(\varepsilon\), failure probability
\(\delta\), observable count \(M\), the state/preparation process, readout
noise, and an observable/ensemble-specific shadow norm. The often-cited
logarithmic dependence on \(M\) does not remove a large shadow norm, correlated
shots, drift, basis cost, or exponential full-tomography boundary.

### Phase claims require a size and regime map

For reduced control parameter \(t=(\lambda-\lambda_c)/\lambda_c\), an observable
may obey

\[
X(L,t)=L^{-\kappa/\nu}
 f_X(tL^{1/\nu})\left[1+bL^{-\omega}+\cdots\right].
\]

The correlation length \(\xi\), boundary condition, aspect ratio, trap,
preparation time, equilibration, dynamic exponent where relevant, and
first-order/crossover alternatives must be declared. A Binder-like cumulant
\(U_L=1-\langle m^4\rangle/(3\langle m^2\rangle^2)\) is one diagnostic, not an
automatic phase oracle.

### Quenched disorder has two probability layers

Let \(\langle X\rangle_{\rm th|r}\) be the thermal/quantum expectation within
realization \(r\), and \([\cdot]_{\rm dis}\) the disorder average. The relative
sample variance

\[
R_X(L)=
\frac{[\langle X\rangle_{\rm th|r}^2]_{\rm dis}
      -[\langle X\rangle_{\rm th|r}]_{\rm dis}^2}
     {[\langle X\rangle_{\rm th|r}]_{\rm dis}^2}
\]

tests self-averaging. It can approach zero rapidly, weakly, or not at all.
Thermal sweeps inside one frozen sample do not increase the disorder
replication count.

### Topology, locality, and transport are separate operators

For a clean periodic band model, a Chern number can be written

\[
C=\frac{1}{2\pi}\sum_{n\in{\rm occ}}
\int_{\rm BZ}\Omega_n(\mathbf{k})\,d^2k.
\]

A finite disordered sample may instead use a real-space marker or Bott index.
The two-terminal service is separately conditioned by leads:

\[
G(E_F)=\frac{e^2}{h}
\mathrm{Tr}\!\left[
\Gamma_L G^r\Gamma_R G^a
\right].
\]

Agreement is a regime-dependent prediction, not an identity. Boundary
termination, contact self-energies, dephasing, temperature, interactions,
spectral/mobility gaps, disorder ensemble, and \(L\)-scaling remain visible.

### Plasma radiation is a kinetic forward model

For electron-energy distribution \(f_e(E)\),

\[
q_{ij}[f_e]=\int_0^\infty \sigma_{ij}(E)v(E)f_e(E)\,dE,
\]

and level populations obey a collisional--radiative system

\[
\dot{\mathbf n}=\mathbf K(f_e,n_e,\mathbf a)\mathbf n+\mathbf s,\qquad
\varepsilon_{ul}=n_uA_{ul}h\nu_{ul}.
\]

Measured spectral radiance is

\[
I_\lambda=
\mathcal R_\lambda\!\left[
\int_{\rm LOS}\mathcal T_\lambda(\ell)\,
\varepsilon_\lambda(\ell,t)\,d\ell
\right]+\eta_\lambda ,
\]

where \(\mathbf a\) denotes atomic data, \(\mathcal T\) opacity/transport, and
\(\mathcal R\) the instrument. A Maxwellian line-ratio table is a special case.

### Reconstruction must be fast relative to the controlled plasma

An axisymmetric equilibrium estimate satisfies a Grad--Shafranov-type
constraint

\[
\Delta^\star\psi=-\mu_0 Rj_\phi(\psi),\qquad
\mathbf y_k=\mathbf H\psi_k+\mathbf D\mathbf i_{v,k}+\boldsymbol\eta_k,
\]

while conducting-structure currents may evolve as

\[
\boldsymbol\tau_v\dot{\mathbf i}_v
=-\mathbf i_v+\mathbf G_v\mathbf u .
\]

The estimator/controller contract includes residual, constraint defect,
latency, timestamp, sensor validity, vessel-current model, actuator saturation,
and an admissible fallback. A fast regression with an old or wrong equilibrium
is not a fast state estimate.

### Beam emittance is inferred from transported moments

For horizontal phase space \(\mathbf z=(x,x')^\mathsf T\),

\[
\boldsymbol\Sigma_x=\mathrm{Cov}(\mathbf z),\qquad
\varepsilon_x=\sqrt{\det\boldsymbol\Sigma_x},\qquad
\varepsilon_{n,x}=\beta_{\rm rel}\gamma_{\rm rel}\varepsilon_x .
\]

A screen after optics row \(\mathbf r_j\) measures

\[
\sigma_{x,j}^2=
\mathbf r_j^\mathsf T\boldsymbol\Sigma_x\mathbf r_j
+\sigma_{{\rm PSF},j}^2+\sigma_{{\rm bg},j}^2 .
\]

At least three well-conditioned projections are required for an uncoupled
second-moment fit; 4-D coupling, dispersion, space charge, nonlinearity, halo,
and selection need richer observations. Slits, pepper pots, screens, and wire
scanners may be destructive or interceptive, so bunches, deposited energy,
availability, and protection constraints belong in the acquisition budget.

## Negative findings and non-transfers

1. More clock digits do not imply less bias. Shared BBR, field, reference, or
   transfer-chain errors can survive synchronous comparison.
2. A closed transition-network cycle can identify inconsistency, not uniquely
   name the bad edge or correct assignment.
3. A disconnected spectroscopic component cannot acquire an absolute relative
   energy from internal transitions.
4. A classical shadow is not a lossless quantum-state file. Unsupported
   nonlocal observables can have prohibitive variance.
5. Reusing the same shots for many observables does not multiply the independent
   sample size, and drift can violate the IID theorem used for bounds.
6. One striking quantum-gas image, one lattice size, or one temperature sweep
   cannot distinguish phase, metastability, finite-size rounding, and crossover.
7. A disorder average does not describe tails or a particular fabricated
   sample; a time average is not a disorder ensemble.
8. Band topology does not imply useful conductance after contacts, disorder,
   finite temperature, dephasing, or interactions.
9. Edge response does not by itself identify a bulk topological invariant;
   trivial boundary channels and contact effects are alternatives.
10. A Boltzmann plot or two-line ratio does not establish a unique plasma
    temperature out of equilibrium.
11. A line-integrated diagnostic cannot resolve a local profile without
    geometry, multiple views, symmetry, a dynamics model, or regularization.
12. A force-balance-consistent equilibrium is not a complete turbulence,
    runaway, kinetic, or disruption state.
13. Lower inference latency is not control value if the model omits vessel
    currents, actuator constraints, sensor invalidity, or off-support shapes.
14. One beam width cannot determine emittance; three ill-conditioned widths can
    be worse than a well-designed scan.
15. A normalized rms emittance can hide halo, coupling, slice structure, and
    non-Gaussian loss risk.
16. “Non-destructive” does not mean zero backaction, zero calibration cost, or
    enough information for the decision.
17. Quantum coherence, plasma self-organization, topology, and strong focusing
    are not licenses for anthropomorphic or substrate-free AI mechanisms.

### C-1422

- **Statement:** A precision atomic-frequency result is conditional on a
  versioned interrogation sensitivity, duty cycle, servo history, signed
  influence-quantity intervention matrix, shared-reference covariance, and
  systematic-shift budget; synchronous or differential comparison can reject
  local-oscillator noise without rejecting a common systematic shift.
- **Status:** established
- **Evidence note:** The measurement boundary is established; the systems
  transfer is unproven.
- **Primary sources:** `LudlowEtAl2015OpticalClocks`,
  `NicholsonEtAl2015Clock`, `BIPMStdFreq2026`,
  `jcgm100`.
- **Rationale:** optical-clock evaluations explicitly separate short-term
  instability, dead-time/LO effects, and interleaved evaluations of lattice,
  magnetic, blackbody, density, and other shifts. BIPM recommended values are
  adjusted from comparison evidence, not read from one indication.
- **Proposed AI translation:** store each high-precision estimate with the
  signed probes that identify nuisance directions, sampling kernel, shared
  roots, support envelope, and independent-operation evidence.
- **Efficiency mechanism:** spend expensive reference or counterfactual probes
  only on nuisance directions that remain rank-deficient or decision-relevant;
  reuse common-mode cancellation without mistaking it for accuracy evidence.
- **Failure modes:** collinear interventions; shared BBR/calibrator bias;
  aliased oscillator noise; servo transient; post-selection of stable windows;
  covariance dropped after differencing; zero-support extrapolation.
- **Measurable prediction:** under held-out shared-shift and duty-cycle changes,
  a sensitivity- and root-aware estimator will reduce false precision-qualified
  releases at equal interrogation count versus naive averaging, ordinary
  synchronous differencing, and robust GLS.
- **Open question:** does any proposed cross-layer record improve the frontier
  after lock-in design, GLS, GUM uncertainty, clock-network closure, and
  independent comparisons are fully implemented?
- **Used by:** [PS-W01](#ps-w01--timed-precision-interrogation-matrix),
  [Candidate 014](../../experiments/candidates/014-versioned-observation-contract.md),
  with [Candidate 009](../../experiments/candidates/009-graded-assurance-envelopes.md)
  and [Candidate 012](../../experiments/candidates/012-latency-qualified-authority.md)
  as consumers.
- **Disposition:** scoped central claim; no principle or candidate promotion.

## Cross-protocol promotion decision

A protocol passing its numeric gate only warrants a higher-fidelity test. It
does not promote a principle, candidate, physical capability, legal
authorization, or energy claim. Before any central adoption:

1. all nine confirmation seed commitments and raw artifacts must verify;
2. the result must survive the named mature null, not only Arm A;
3. the effect must appear in at least two hostile generator families and in the
   fresh replication;
4. protected coverage, false-safe, constraint, abstention, and resource
   outcomes must all pass;
5. a generator/process implementation independent of the proposing team must
   reproduce the conclusion;
6. any claimed energy benefit must be measured in J on representative physical
   hardware with uncertainty and a complete service boundary; and
7. a field expert must confirm that state labels, units, operators, regimes,
   safety boundaries, and conventional nulls were not simplified into an
   artificial win.

The expected result is substantial deduplication: C-1423, C-1424, C-1425,
C-1426, C-1427, C-1428, C-1429, and C-1430 should normally collapse into their
field-standard graph, shadow, scaling, hierarchical, transport, kinetic,
equilibrium, and phase-space methods. C-1422 should normally collapse into
ordinary precision metrology. The claims remain useful because they specify
what must not be collapsed in the evidence record.
