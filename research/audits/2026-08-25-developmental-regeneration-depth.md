## Executive finding

The earlier breadth audit correctly separated signal from competence,
specification from implementation, and present function from evolutionary
origin. It also already owns generic morphogen thresholds, lateral inhibition,
segmentation clocks, commitment, apoptosis, planarian polarity, stem-cell
capacity, lineage restriction, metamorphosis, Hsp90-associated canalization,
plasticity, and generic reaction--diffusion. Repeating those mechanisms under
new names would add no durable residue.

Ten narrower observations survive deduplication:

1. positional memory can be causally rewritten during a bounded repair window,
   persist across later injury, and exhibit asymmetric reversibility;
2. replacement capacity and positional instructions can occupy different
   cell populations;
3. opposed signal sources under reciprocal regulation can compensate for
   partial source loss;
4. receiver-side feedback can scale a morphogen field only over a finite
   operating envelope;
5. geometry and receptor placement can change which otherwise similar
   receivers can observe;
6. load can recruit the actuator that reinforces the load path;
7. a soft identity boundary can be consolidated into an active physical
   barrier;
8. apparently redundant regulators can become non-redundant under a shifted
   environment or weakened input;
9. local periodic self-organization can be composed with a global positional
   field; and
10. local feedback can plausibly break a global symmetry without a preassigned
    leader, although the cited molecular model is not uniquely established.

These observations sharpen existing project mechanisms. They do not show that
an artificial system will repair itself, use less energy, or outperform a
checkpoint, distributed controller, graph algorithm, robust optimiser, or
ordinary redundancy scheme. The only promoted artifact is a bounded
preimplementation contract that can reject the proposed translations.

## Construct, symbol, and unit firewall

F-022 uses artificial spatial graphs. Biological quantities are not imported
as generator constants.

1. $G=(V,E)$ is a finite connected graph. Node $i\in V$ is an artificial
   module, not a cell. Edge length $\ell_{ij}$ is metres [m] in the synthetic
   geometry and adjacency $A_{ij}\in\{0,1\}$ is dimensionless.
2. $y_i\in\{0,1,2,3\}$ is evaluator-only target role. $\hat y_i$ is a proposed
   role. Role error is dimensionless.
3. $u_i(t)$ and $v_i(t)$ are dimensionless transported fields. $D_u,D_v$ are
   synthetic diffusivities [m$^2$/s], and $t$ is simulated time [s].
4. $m_i(t)\in[-1,1]^d$ is durable local positional memory. A write is counted
   in components and bytes; it is not a biological epigenetic event.
5. $g_i(t)\in\{0,1\}$ is a repair-write gate. It records authority to propose a
   write, not proof that the write is correct.
6. $q_{ij}(t)$ is accepted or attempted traffic [tasks/s], $C_{ij}$ is edge
   capacity [tasks/s], and $\rho_{ij}=q_{ij}/C_{ij}$ is dimensionless load.
7. $b_{ij}(t)\in[0,1]$ is artificial boundary strength. It changes routing
   cost and cross-boundary leakage; it is not stiffness in pascals.
8. Service is counted in accepted tasks. Latency is seconds [s], messages and
   stored state are bytes [B], CPU time is seconds [s], and peak resident set
   size is bytes [B].
9. Synthetic action cost is reported in operation-count units and never called
   energy. Workstation electricity is joules [J] only if measured at the
   frozen boundary by a calibrated meter; otherwise it is `unmeasured`.

## Shared mathematical skeleton

### Transported fields on a nonuniform graph

For field $u_i$ and edge length $\ell_{ij}>0$,

$$
\frac{du_i}{dt}
=D_u\sum_{j\in\mathcal N_i}
\frac{u_j-u_i}{\ell_{ij}^{2}}
+f_u(u_i,v_i;\theta)+s_i(t).
$$

$D_u$ is [m$^2$/s], the weighted graph-Laplacian term is [m$^{-2}$],
$f_u$ and source $s_i$ are [s$^{-1}$], and $du_i/dt$ is [s$^{-1}$] because
$u_i$ is dimensionless. F-022 uses a declared fixed-step integrator and rejects
nonconverged worlds; the equation is a synthetic pattern generator, not a
validated tissue simulator.

### Repair-gated persistent memory

For candidate write $\tilde m_i(t)$ and gain $\alpha\in[0,1]$,

$$
m_i(t+\Delta t)
=m_i(t)+g_i(t)\alpha[\tilde m_i(t)-m_i(t)].
$$

All terms are dimensionless. The manifest records the gate opener, evidence
vintage, old/new hash, written components, bytes, validation result, and
rollback target. A write outside an open gate is an invalid run.

### Load-dependent and reversible boundary state

For normalized load $\rho_{ij}$, conflict indicator $c_{ij}\in[0,1]$,
reopening signal $r_{ij}\in[0,1]$, and rates $\eta_b,\lambda_b$ [s$^{-1}$],

$$
\frac{db_{ij}}{dt}
=\eta_b\rho_{ij}c_{ij}-\lambda_b r_{ij}b_{ij},
\qquad b_{ij}\in[0,1].
$$

The numerical projection to $[0,1]$ is logged. Boundary writes, maintenance,
blocked useful traffic, reopen time, and rollback failure remain separate.

### Pattern, repair, and resource outcomes

For surviving evaluator set $V_s$ and role weights $w_i>0$,

$$
e_{role}=\frac{\sum_{i\in V_s}w_i\mathbf1[\hat y_i\ne y_i]}
{\sum_{i\in V_s}w_i},
\qquad
A_{service}=\frac{\int_0^H S_{accepted}(t)\,dt}
{\int_0^H S_{demand}(t)\,dt}.
$$

$e_{role}$ and $A_{service}$ are dimensionless; $S$ is [tasks/s] and horizon
$H$ is [s]. Mean quality cannot erase wrong-pole repair, duplicate organizer,
unsafe write, corruption miss, or failed rollback. Protocols retain these as
protected outcomes before scalarisation.

## Claim-ledger proposals

### C-1506

- **Statement:** In the studied axolotl limb interventions, transient Sonic
  hedgehog exposure during regeneration rewrote anterior blastema cells into a
  persistent Hand2-positive posterior-memory state observable after complete
  regeneration and re-amputation; comparable exposure did not rewrite
  uninjured cells.
- **Status:** established in the cited axolotl interventions; asymmetric
  Hand2--Shh positive feedback is a source-supported mechanistic account, not a
  universal positional-memory law.
- **Primary source:** [Otsuki et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09036-5).
- **Rationale:** the result adds causal writing, a repair-specific competence
  window, persistence, and directional hysteresis beyond static lineage or
  positional labels.
- **Proposed AI translation:** retain local role metadata through ordinary
  operation, but permit versioned writes only inside an authenticated repair
  window with independent validation and rollback.
- **Efficiency mechanism:** valid retained context may reduce repair search,
  communication, and parameter updates.
- **Failure modes:** poisoned memory, false repair-window opening, wrong-pole
  propagation, one-way lock-in, and hidden access to absolute coordinates or a
  target snapshot.
- **Measurable prediction:** under valid surviving memory, gated persistence
  reduces time, messages, and writes to restore service relative to a
  context-free local reconstruction null without increasing wrong-role repair;
  the advantage must disappear or trigger abstention under common-mode memory
  corruption.
- **Open question:** can support and corruption be detected cheaply enough to
  justify retaining writable positional state?
- **Used by:** [DEV-T01](../../experiments/fixtures/022-regenerative-positional-memory.md#dev-t01-hysteretic-positional-memory).
- **Disposition:** scoped central claim proposal; no new principle.

### C-1507

- **Statement:** In the studied planarian system, pluripotent neoblasts supply
  replacement capacity while differentiated body-wall muscle cells express
  position-control genes and alter that expression after injury even in the
  absence of neoblasts.
- **Status:** established for the cited expression, irradiation, and injury
  experiments.
- **Primary source:** [Witchley et al. 2013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2013.07.022).
- **Rationale:** repair capacity and repair instruction are separable roles;
  neither a stem-cell pool nor a positional map alone is a complete repair
  system.
- **Proposed AI translation:** store reconstruction constraints in surviving
  task modules while a replaceable worker pool performs repair.
- **Efficiency mechanism:** distributed instruction may reduce central
  checkpoint state and allow repair-worker replacement.
- **Failure modes:** coupled deletion of workers and instruction holders,
  stale constraints, instruction/task interference, and undisclosed global
  templates.
- **Measurable prediction:** a two-plane design tolerates worker-pool deletion
  with fewer replicated bytes than a checkpoint null at matched restoration
  quality, while explicitly failing or abstaining when too little instruction
  support survives.
- **Open question:** what minimum surviving support makes the distributed
  instruction state identifiable rather than merely suggestive?
- **Used by:** [DEV-T02](../../experiments/fixtures/022-regenerative-positional-memory.md#dev-t02-capacityinstruction-separation).
- **Disposition:** scoped central claim proposal; reinforces maintenance-plane
  separation.

   generator, observations, actions, resources, and transfer support.
2. A protocol FAIL can reject the proposed AI translation; it cannot refute the
   biological source.

   or effect gate is not resolved. It is not a PASS.
4. `INVALID` is required for leakage, changed seeds, unit/schema violations,
   nonconvergence beyond the declared rule, hidden failures, or a broken
   measurement boundary. It is not a FAIL of the hypothesis.

   successful build, smoke run, or workstation-readiness check is not evidence
   of an effect.

   validation, narrower support, hidden preprocessing, or uncharged state
