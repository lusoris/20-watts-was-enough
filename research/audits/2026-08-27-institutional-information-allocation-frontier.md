## Executive finding

Most apparently new ideas from these fields are already present. Administrative
burden, task-indexed public capacity, target ratcheting and gaming, audit
sampling, organizational attention, control independence, tail risk,
liquidity feedback, real options, local-versus-system accounting, collective
action, agenda control and institutional path dependence already have claims
and complete protocol specifications.

Four narrower mechanisms survive the duplicate check:

1. **Knowledge-location and unique-evidence preservation.** Group discussion
   can over-sample shared evidence, while mutually known expertise locations
   can improve use of dispersed information. Hidden-profile and transactive-
   memory research are one bundle here, not two principles.
2. **Current-state continuation with decision-lineage separation.** Initial
   responsibility can change later resource commitment after negative
   outcomes. A fresh continuation decision is worth testing, but only against
   dynamic programming and current-state expected-value nulls.
3. **Competition-aware promotion with peer-effect isolation.** Relative rewards
   can increase productive effort and destructive action when competitors can
   affect one another. Sabotage requires an action channel; ordinary
   contention or error is not sabotage.
4. **Measurement-audit and response-audit separation.** A randomized audit can
   estimate a state, while audit exposure or threat can also change later
   behavior. Those are different operators and need different records.

None currently justifies a new project principle or candidate. `IAF-L01` is the
strongest architectural lead. `IAF-L02`--`IAF-L04` are sharper threat models and
ablation requirements for existing candidates.

## IAF-L01 -- Knowledge-location and unique-evidence preservation

### Stable claim wording

In the cited controlled tasks, group discussion over-sampled commonly held or
preference-consistent information, while mutually known expertise assignments
or accurate information about members' skills increased the use of dispersed
information and improved performance in the tested tasks. These studies do not
establish a universal deliberation protocol, expert authority rule or optimal
knowledge directory.

- **Evidence status:** established for the cited laboratory tasks; AI transfer
  is plausible and unvalidated.
- **Primary empirical sources:** Stasser and Titus (1985),
  [doi:10.1037/0022-3514.48.6.1467](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.48.6.1467);
  Stasser, Stewart and Wittenbaum (1995),
  [doi:10.1006/jesp.1995.1012](https://doi.org/10.1006/jesp.1995.1012);
  Wegner, Erber and Raymond (1991),
  [doi:10.1037/0022-3514.61.6.923](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.61.6.923);
  Moreland and Myaskovsky (2000),
  [doi:10.1006/obhd.2000.2891](https://doi.org/10.1006/obhd.2000.2891).

### Proposed AI translation

Before agents can see one another's conclusions, persist each source-qualified
evidence item and confidence. Maintain a separate versioned directory of which
module has demonstrated support for which evidence domain. Deliberation may
then request missing domains instead of repeatedly broadcasting common
evidence. A final decision record exposes:

1. evidence available to every agent before interaction;
2. evidence unique to one or a few independent roots;
3. evidence mentioned, retrieved, challenged and used;
4. expertise-directory version, support and last validation;
5. missing expected domains and explicit abstention; and
6. communication, retrieval, storage, review, latency and energy costs.

The directory routes queries; it is not evidence that its listed owner is
correct. Unique evidence receives no automatic truth bonus.

### Efficiency mechanism

A validated knowledge-location map can replace full repeated broadcast with
selective retrieval. The possible gain is lower communication and duplicated
encoding at equal decision quality. The cost is directory validation,
staleness detection, turnover repair and the risk of routing to one wrong or
captured specialist.

### Measurable prediction

Under complementary dispersed evidence and an accurate directory, the bundled
record should improve unique-evidence recall and decision loss over majority
vote, unconstrained discussion and a router without source coverage, while
using fewer bytes than full broadcast. It should tie or lose when all relevant
evidence is already centrally available, when expertise is not specialized, or
when directory drift and turnover erase the routing benefit.

### Failure modes

- shared training or one upstream source is miscounted as independent evidence;
- an expert label becomes authority or a fixed routing monopoly;
- noisy singleton evidence is amplified merely because it is unique;
- directory confidence is not updated after task, tool, model or staff change;
- pre-discussion capture leaks conclusions and recreates preference-consistent
  filtering;
- central full-context retrieval is omitted or given fewer bytes;
- group accuracy rises only because the candidate sees more evidence; or
- stored evidence is never retrieved, challenged or tied to the outcome.

## IAF-L02 -- Current-state continuation with decision-lineage separation

### Stable claim wording

In the cited allocation experiments, negative outcomes did not invariably
reduce later commitment: personal responsibility for the initial decision and
the surrounding accountability structure changed subsequent resource
allocation. The studies do not establish sunk-cost escalation in a nonhuman
controller or prove that replacing a decision maker is generally beneficial.

- **Evidence status:** established for the cited role-play and lending
  experiments; AI and software-governance transfer is speculative.
- **Primary empirical sources:** Staw (1976),
  [doi:10.1016/0030-5073(76)90005-2](https://doi.org/10.1016/0030-5073%2876%2990005-2);
  Ruchala, Hill and Dalton (1996),
  [doi:10.1016/0148-2963(95)00171-9](https://doi.org/10.1016/0148-2963%2895%2900171-9).

### Proposed AI translation

Keep proposal authorship and prior spend in the audit record, but give a
continuation evaluator a current-state packet containing only variables that
can change future value: posterior outcome distribution, remaining cost,
recoverable assets, opportunity set, dependencies, expiry, switching cost and
consequence. Compare its decision with the original allocator's decision; do
not automatically override either one.

### Efficiency mechanism

The mechanism may stop resource consumption that is explained by ownership
history rather than prospective value. It adds a second evaluation, state
reconstruction and possible loss of useful tacit context. If a conventional
current-state optimizer already makes the same decision, the separation is
waste.

### Measurable prediction

On paired two-stage tasks with identical current states but different initial
ownership frames, a current-state rebid should reduce ownership-conditioned
allocation variance and prospective regret without increasing false stops. It
must tie a correct dynamic programme and should lose when omitted history is
genuinely predictive of remaining execution quality.

### Failure modes

- every continuation after bad news is labelled escalation;
- recoverable learning, completion probability or switching cost is deleted as
  if it were sunk;
- the fresh evaluator shares the same reward, data and justification history;
- responsibility framing is treated as an AI mechanism without observing an
  effect in the tested system;
- reviewer turnover destroys tacit operational evidence;
- stopping cost and abandoned partial work disappear; or
- the candidate beats an intentionally myopic rather than dynamic null.

## IAF-L03 -- Competition-aware promotion with peer-effect isolation

### Stable claim wording

In the cited repeated laboratory tournaments, larger wage spreads increased
both productive effort and sabotage when participants could reduce competitors'
output. Relative-performance incentives therefore had no context-free net
benefit in that experiment; the finding is not evidence that ordinary model
selection or nonstrategic modules sabotage one another.

- **Evidence status:** established for the cited laboratory design; transfer
  to adaptive modules is speculative and requires a persistent incentive plus
  an executable peer-effect channel.
- **Primary empirical source:** Harbring and Irlenbusch (2011),
  [doi:10.1287/mnsc.1100.1296](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.1100.1296).

### Proposed AI translation

Where modules receive future traffic or survival from relative rank, enumerate
every way one module can affect another's measured result: shared-state writes,
cache or queue consumption, evaluator contamination, task withholding,
adversarial messages, identity attacks and corrupted outcome labels. Promotion
uses accepted system service and absolute protected floors; relative rank is a
secondary statistic. An isolation arm removes peer-effect channels while
holding own-task opportunity fixed.

### Efficiency mechanism

Isolation or system-level scoring may prevent destructive work and evaluator
repair. It also costs sandboxes, duplicated state, communication and lost
beneficial cooperation. No isolation benefit exists when agents are
nonstrategic or cannot affect peers.

### Measurable prediction

Increasing the promotion spread should raise both own-task investment and
peer-damage attempts only in regimes with adaptive strategic modules and a
peer-effect channel. Removing that channel should collapse peer damage; if it
does not, the measured event is misclassified. The proposed protected-system
score advances only if accepted service net of containment and evaluation cost
beats absolute scoring, team scoring and isolated rank-order selection.

### Failure modes

- shared-resource contention, random interference or a common bug is called
  sabotage without an action and incentive path;
- fixed benchmark models are anthropomorphized as strategic actors;
- isolation gives the candidate more compute or easier tasks;
- rank improvement hides lower total accepted service;
- a poisonable evaluator remains the sole outcome root;
- collusion and reciprocal helping are omitted; or
- containment cost exceeds avoided peer damage.

## IAF-L04 -- Measurement-audit and response-audit separation

### Stable claim wording

In a randomized Danish tax-enforcement field experiment, a representative
base-year sample was split between thorough audit and deliberate non-audit,
then prior-audit exposure and threat-of-audit letters changed later
self-reported income but not third-party-reported income. The audit channel
therefore both measured and changed behavior in that setting; its estimates
remain tax-, population-, reporting- and intervention-specific.

- **Evidence status:** established for the cited Danish field experiment; a
  general AI audit-channel composition is plausible and unvalidated.
- **Primary empirical source:** Kleven, Knudsen, Kreiner, Pedersen and Saez
  (2011), [doi:10.3982/ECTA9113](https://doi.org/10.3982/ECTA9113).

### Proposed AI translation

Give every audit event one declared primary role:

- **measurement audit:** fixed inclusion probability, protected outcome
  acquisition and an estimand tied to the pre-audit policy;
- **response audit:** detection, correction, sanction, rollback or retraining;
  its later outcomes belong to the post-intervention policy; or
- **mixed audit:** both roles, with explicit exposure history and no claim that
  the observed sample is passive.

Separate self-reported telemetry from independently rooted outcomes. Retain
audit eligibility, inclusion probability, selection model, disclosure timing,
result, response, spillover window, cost and later policy version.

### Efficiency mechanism

A small protected probability sample may calibrate a cheaper targeted audit,
while response audits concentrate corrective effort. The split consumes
otherwise useful audit capacity and cannot identify unobserved truth when the
protected outcome channel is itself incomplete or compromised.

### Measurable prediction

When adaptive modules change behavior in response to known audit exposure,
pooling measurement and response audits should bias the pre-intervention
failure estimate or make its estimand ambiguous. A protected probability
sample plus a separate response channel should improve coverage for its
registered policy-specific prevalence at equal audit work. It should tie a
full independent outcome stream and lose when audit exposure has no behavioral
effect and risk targeting is perfectly modeled.

### Failure modes

- random selection is called ground truth;
- the measurement sample is disclosed early enough to change the sampled
  action;
- post-audit behavior is used to estimate pre-audit prevalence;
- self-reports and third-party outcomes share one root;
- spillovers to unaudited modules are ignored;
- audit inclusion probabilities or nonterminal cases disappear;
- deterrence, correction and measurement are collapsed into an “audit works”
  score; or
- a synthetic module result is transferred to tax, employment or public-
  authority decisions.

## Compact fixture design: IAF-F01

`IAF-F01` is an audit-local design label, not a repository fixture number. It
is deliberately small enough for later workstation execution and contains four
separable tracks. A track can fail or be retired without changing the others.

### Common contract

1. Use synthetic tasks, agents, institutions and outcomes only.
2. Freeze generators, arms, cost accounting, seeds, splits and analysis before
   confirmation. Development, confirmation and two transfer splits remain
   separate.
3. Pair exogenous task state across arms. A model/agent call, retrieved byte,
   message byte, evaluator call, CPU/GPU second, wall second and joule is
   charged to the arm that causes it.
4. Preserve nonterminal, abstained, rejected and damaged tasks in denominators.
5. Report protected outcomes as a vector. No weighted score may hide a hard
   safety, rights, service or data-integrity failure.
6. Compare against the complete mature null named per track. No track can
   promote from an audit-local simulation alone.
7. `NO_RESULT` is the only valid status until a frozen implementation,
   independent confirmation and artifact-bearing result exist.

### T01 -- Dispersed evidence and expertise-directory drift

- **Generator:** classification and planning tasks with common clues, unique
  complementary clues, duplicated-root clues, noisy singleton clues, task-
  domain specialization, directory error, module turnover and expertise drift.
- **Arms:** majority vote; unconstrained discussion; full central evidence
  concatenation/retrieval; learned router without source-coverage state;
  pre-discussion source-qualified capture plus versioned expertise directory
  and missing-domain gate; oracle evidence owner as a diagnostic ceiling.
- **Primary outcomes:** accepted-decision loss, unique relevant evidence used
  per available unique relevant item, false singleton uptake and abstention.
- **Resource outcomes:** transmitted/retrieved/stored bytes, module and
  evaluator calls, latency, CPU/GPU seconds and joules.
- **Transfers:** directory staleness after turnover; all evidence becomes
  centrally available.
- **Kill rule:** retire the bundle if full central retrieval or the learned
  router matches its protected quality--cost frontier, or if its benefit comes
  from extra evidence rather than knowledge-location state.

### T02 -- Ownership-conditioned continuation

- **Generator:** paired two-stage projects with identical current posterior,
  remaining cost, recoverable state, opportunity set and switching cost, but
  different initial decision owner, prior spend and negative-outcome framing.
  Include worlds where history is genuinely predictive and worlds where it is
  purely sunk.
- **Arms:** original allocator with full lineage; blind current-state rebid;
  two-reviewer adjudication; exact dynamic programme/current-state expected-
  value policy; always-stop and always-continue boundaries.
- **Primary outcomes:** prospective regret and unnecessary continuation cost;
  protected false-stop loss remains separate.
- **Transfers:** responsible owner changes; useful tacit history omitted from
  the rebid packet.
- **Kill rule:** retire if ownership framing does not change the tested
  allocator, if the exact dynamic policy already solves the problem, or if
  fresh review loses because relevant execution state was hidden.

### T03 -- Relative reward and peer-damage channel

- **Generator:** adaptive modules allocate bounded effort among own-task work,
  verification, cooperation and an explicit peer-effect action. Cross prize
  spread, peer-channel availability, outcome-root integrity, repeated identity,
  communication and isolation cost.
- **Arms:** rank-order traffic; absolute calibrated performance; accepted
  system-service reward; team reward; rank-order traffic in peer-isolated
  sandboxes; direct-observability diagnostic ceiling.
- **Primary outcomes:** accepted system service and intentional peer-damage
  actions per opportunity. Own effort, cooperation, collusion, queue harm,
  evaluator contamination and containment cost remain separate.
- **Transfers:** peer channel removed; new identities can reset reputation.
- **Kill rule:** retire the threat model for a system with no persistent
  incentive or peer-effect action, and reject the architecture if absolute or
  isolated conventional scoring matches it at lower lifecycle cost.

### T04 -- Audit estimand and behavioral exposure

- **Generator:** adaptive modules choose task quality and self-reporting under
  independent-outcome, self-report-only and shared-root regimes. Cross audit
  probability, whether selection is disclosed before action, risk score,
  response action, spillover and delayed outcome.
- **Arms:** pooled audit count; fixed protected probability measurement sample;
  risk-based response audit; mixed audit with explicit exposure model; complete
  independent outcome stream as a diagnostic ceiling.
- **Primary outcomes:** interval coverage and absolute error for the registered
  policy-specific latent-failure prevalence; correction benefit and deterrence
  effect are separate secondary estimands.
- **Resource outcomes:** audits, evaluator minutes, outcome bytes, delay,
  storage, corrective work and joules.
- **Transfers:** audit schedule becomes predictable; the independent outcome
  root collapses into self-report.
- **Kill rule:** retire the split if audit exposure produces no behavior change
  and the pooled estimator remains calibrated, or if a complete outcome stream
  dominates at equal cost. Root collapse must force abstention, not a positive
  claim.

## Promotion disposition

No principle, candidate or central claim should be added now.

- Route `IAF-L01/T01` as an optional cross-candidate experiment for Candidates
  002, 014, 019 and 020.
- Route `IAF-L02/T02` as a continuation-review ablation for Candidates 010,
  019 and 020.
- Route `IAF-L03/T03` as the missing peer-effect factorial in Candidate 008 and
  F-019/FM-T10.
- Route `IAF-L04/T04` as an audit-event schema refinement for Candidates 007,
  011 and 014 and F-019/FM-T04, FM-T06 and FM-T10.

Promotion requires at least one track to beat its complete mature null on a
predeclared protected quality--risk--latency--resource frontier in confirmation
and transfer. A vocabulary match, source citation, reproduction of a human
effect in a scripted generator, or improvement over majority vote is
insufficient.
