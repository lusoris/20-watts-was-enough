## Executive finding

Direct social research does not supply a portable recipe for making a model
"social." It supplies a demanding account of how observations are produced,
whose actions can change because they are observed, which people and settings
are reachable, which categories make an answer possible, how networks violate
unit independence, how organizations decouple formal rules from enacted work,
and how media systems select opportunities long before an attitude or action is
measured.

The durable residue is a **situated, authority-qualified social-observation
record**. A defensible record keeps together:

1. the construct, estimand, population, setting, time, comparison, and evidence
   target;
2. the instrument wording, response scale, category system, codebook, language,
   order, administration mode, and versioned mappings;
3. the frame, recruitment path, field gate, eligibility, contact attempts,
   access failures, participation, item missingness, attrition, and support;
4. the researcher's role, declared positional relations, participant awareness,
   interaction history, reactivity checks, field events, negative cases, and
   interpretation trail;
5. network membership, tie and exposure definition, interference mapping,
   assignment, spillovers, common causes, and unsupported counterfactuals;
6. formal organizational rules, actual performances, roles, resources,
   exceptions, workarounds, reporting incentives, and temporal scope;
7. content production, editorial selection, platform eligibility, ranking,
   delivery, opportunity to view, attention, comprehension, belief, and action
   as distinct stages;
8. the methods used, their shared data and failure roots, disagreements,
   adjudication rule, and unmeasured alternatives; and
9. individual agreement, applicable legal basis, information duties, ethics
   review, organizational permission, named community authority, purpose,
   expiry, refusal, withdrawal, derivative propagation, and human decision.

These elements are established or mature practices in survey methodology,
psychometrics, ethnography, causal inference with interference, organizational
sociology, communication research, mixed methods, data protection, and
research ethics. Their proposed composition is speculative. It earns project
status only if it reduces false comparisons, false population claims, false
peer-effect claims, false policy-compliance claims, false media-effect claims,
or unauthorized operations beyond the complete mature stack at equal data,
compute, storage, review time, and participant burden.

## Terminology firewall

1. A **construct** is the theoretical attribute a measure is intended to
   represent. It is not identical to an item, score, label, or embedding.
2. An **estimand** states the target quantity for a population, comparison,
   exposure, intervention, and time. An estimator is a procedure for estimating
   it.
3. A **response** is produced through comprehension, retrieval or judgement,
   formatting, and the interactional context of the instrument. It is not a
   context-free reading of an internal state.
4. A **category** is an operational classification with inclusion, exclusion,
   mapping, and version rules. It is not automatically a natural kind.
5. **Reliability**, construct validity, measurement invariance, predictive
   validity, and decision utility answer different questions.
6. **Measurement invariance** is relative to a model, items, groups, time,
   language, mode, and target comparison. One successful test is not a global
   certificate.
7. A **sampling frame** is the operational list or mechanism from which units
   can be selected. It is not necessarily the target population.
8. A **response rate** is a participation statistic under a declared
   denominator. It is not nonresponse bias.
9. **Poststratification**, weighting, and propensity adjustment cannot recover
   a population region with no support without additional assumptions or data.
10. **Ethnography** is sustained, situated inquiry using participation,
    observation, interaction, records, and interpretation. It is not a synonym
    for unstructured anecdotes.
11. **Participant observation** changes the researcher's access and relation
    to activity; "participant" and "observer" are not mutually exclusive
    error-free states.
12. **Reflexivity** records and analyses how position, relations, intervention,
    and power enter knowledge production. It does not erase those dependencies
    or make an interpretation self-validating.
13. **Positionality** concerns historically and relationally situated access,
    power, identity, and interpretation. A demographic match or mismatch alone
    neither validates nor invalidates a claim.
14. **Reactivity** is change associated with measurement, observation,
    disclosure, or study participation. It is not always removable and is not
    always the same as deliberate deception.
15. A **gatekeeper** can enable or deny field or organizational access. That
    permission does not automatically substitute for each participant's rights
    or a named community's authority.
16. A **social-network tie** is defined by a measurement process and time
    window. Contact, friendship, follow, co-presence, communication, and
    inferred similarity are not interchangeable edges.
17. **Interference** means one unit's treatment or exposure can affect another
    unit's outcome. It requires counterfactuals indexed by more than the unit's
    own assignment.
18. **Homophily**, shared context, common exposure, and contagion can produce
    similar observed correlations. Temporal diffusion alone does not identify
    peer influence.
19. A formal organizational **rule**, its shared account or ostensive form,
    and concrete performances are distinct.
20. **Compliance evidence** is not the same as a written policy, self-report,
    certification, or absence of recorded incidents.
21. **Gatekeeping** selects content through source, editor, organization,
    platform, ranking, moderation, and audience stages. It need not imply one
    centralized human gatekeeper.
22. **Availability**, eligibility, ranking, delivery, opportunity to view,
    attention, comprehension, recall, belief, and action are different media
    variables.
23. **Reach**, impressions, clicks, and sharing are not interchangeable with
    persuasion or durable behaviour change.
24. **Triangulation** can pursue convergence, complementarity, development,
    initiation, or expansion. More methods do not mechanically create
    independent evidence.
25. **Participant checking** can reveal misquotation, disagreement, changed
    context, harm, or contested interpretation. It is not a universal truth
    oracle and may itself be shaped by power and risk.
26. **Ethics review**, professional guidance, legal compliance, scientific
    validity, and legitimate community authority are separate assurance layers.

## Evidence synthesis

### Survey responses are instrument-qualified observations

Schwarz reviews experiments showing that question wording, response
alternatives, reference periods, question order, and the conversational logic
of the interview affect self-reports. Krosnick formalizes satisficing as a
response to cognitive burden and links it to observable response patterns
([Schwarz 1999](https://doi.org/10.1037/0003-066X.54.2.93);
[Krosnick 1991](https://doi.org/10.1002/acp.2350050305)). These are not claims
that a respondent lacks a view. They establish that the instrument participates
in producing the recorded answer.

For an ordinal or continuous item $j$, person $i$, and group $g$, a minimal
measurement model is

$$
Y_{ijg}^{*}=\nu_{jg}+\lambda_{jg}\eta_{ig}+\epsilon_{ijg},
\qquad
Y_{ijg}=k\;\text{iff}\;\tau_{j,k-1,g}<Y_{ijg}^{*}\leq\tau_{j,k,g}.
$$

Here $Y^*$ is a latent response propensity, $\eta$ is the target factor,
$\lambda$ is the loading, $\nu$ is the intercept, $\tau$ are category
thresholds, and $\epsilon$ is residual variation. Meredith's invariance
framework makes the core comparison boundary explicit: equal patterns,
loadings, intercepts or thresholds, and residuals support different
comparisons. A latent-mean comparison generally needs invariant loadings and
intercepts or thresholds for an identified anchor set, not merely acceptable
fit of the same factor pattern
([Meredith 1993](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02294825)). Partial invariance can
support a named contrast under stated anchors; it is not universal
comparability.

Category design is also an observation operation. Westbrook and Saperstein
show that simply adding response categories does not solve ambiguities among
sex, gender identity, presentation, and classification by others
([Westbrook and Saperstein 2015](https://doi.org/10.1177/0891243215584758)).
Telles and Paschel find that the relation among skin colour, status, and racial
classification differs across four Latin American national contexts
([Telles and Paschel 2014](https://doi.org/10.1086/679252)). The project-level
translation is narrow: store the question, category scheme, classifier,
context, language, version, and mapping uncertainty. The sources do not supply
one universal category ontology.

### Response rate is not nonresponse bias

For outcome $Y$ and response indicator $R\in\{0,1\}$,

$$
\mathbb{E}[Y\mid R=1]-\mathbb{E}[Y]
=\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(Y,R)}{\Pr(R=1)}.
$$

The response rate $\Pr(R=1)$ is only the denominator in this expression. Bias
also depends on how response relates to the outcome. Groves and Peytcheva's
meta-analysis of 59 methodological studies found that lower response rates do
not mechanically imply larger nonresponse bias
([Groves and Peytcheva 2008](https://doi.org/10.1093/poq/nfn011)).

A social-data record therefore separates:

1. target population and estimand;
2. frame inclusion probability;
3. selection and contact probability;
4. field or gatekeeper access;
5. unit response, item response, and attrition;
6. observed adjustment variables and support;
7. weighting, imputation, outcome, and variance models; and
8. sensitivity to non-ignorable missingness.

Poststratification can correct an observed imbalance under adequate support and
models. It cannot identify the mean of a population stratum that was never in
the frame or never reachable without external data or extrapolation. This
deduplicates directly to [C-129](../claims.md#c-129),
[C-220](../claims.md#c-220), [C-221](../claims.md#c-221), and
[C-231](../claims.md#c-231).

### Ethnographic access and researcher role are part of the observation process

Schwartz and Schwartz describe participant observation as registering,
interpreting, and recording through continuing observed–observer transactions
([Schwartz and Schwartz 1955](https://doi.org/10.1086/221566)). Becker's
classic account treats inference and proof as explicit problems for participant
observation rather than assuming that prolonged presence solves them
([Becker 1958](https://doi.org/10.2307/2089053)). Burawoy's extended case
method treats intervention, process, structuration, theory reconstruction, and
power effects as features to analyse, while also stating the limitations of
the reflexive model
([Burawoy 1998](https://doi.org/10.1111/0735-2751.00040)).

Let $A_i(r,x)$ indicate whether event or person $i$ is observable under
researcher role $r$ and context $x$, and let $Y_i(r)$ be behaviour that can
depend on that role. The observed field distribution is conditional on both:

$$
\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{field}}(r,x)
=\{Y_i(r):A_i(r,x)=1\}.
$$

Changing role can change access and behaviour simultaneously. A fieldnote is
therefore incomplete without time, setting, role, participant awareness,
access route, interaction, recording delay, selection event, coding version,
negative case, and uncertainty about what was not observable.

Reflexivity is valuable when it makes those dependencies inspectable and
contestable. It is not a transformation that maps $Y_i(r)$ to an unobserved
role-free truth. Positionality is relational and can change trust, risk,
disclosure, access, and interpretation, but an identity match alone is neither
a validation statistic nor a universal access key.

Jerolmack and Khan warn against inferring situated action directly from verbal
accounts, calling that move the attitudinal fallacy
([Jerolmack and Khan 2014](https://doi.org/10.1177/0049124114523396)). The
project should retain the narrower claim: an interview account, observed
practice, trace, retrospective explanation, and normative self-description can
answer different questions. Disagreement is evidence about context,
interpretation, memory, identity, incentives, or practice—not automatic proof
that one modality is true and another false.

### Networks require interference and exposure mappings

Manski's reflection problem shows why group behaviour and individual behaviour
can mirror one another without identifying an endogenous social effect
([Manski 1993](https://doi.org/10.2307/2298123)). Shalizi and Thomas show that
latent homophily and contagion are generically confounded in unrestricted
observational social-network studies
([Shalizi and Thomas 2011](https://doi.org/10.1177/0049124111404820)).

With assignment vector $\mathbf{z}$ and network $G$, an interference-aware
potential outcome can be written

$$
Y_i\!\left(z_i,g_i(\mathbf{z}_{-i},G)\right),
$$

where $g_i$ is a registered exposure mapping. Two distinct estimands are

$$
\tau_D(g)=\mathbb{E}[Y_i(1,g)-Y_i(0,g)],
\qquad
\tau_S(z;g,g')=\mathbb{E}[Y_i(z,g)-Y_i(z,g')].
$$

$\tau_D$ is a direct effect at fixed neighbourhood exposure; $\tau_S$ is a
spillover contrast. Hudgens and Halloran formalize direct, indirect, total,
and overall effects under partial interference
([Hudgens and Halloran 2008](https://doi.org/10.1198/016214508000000292)).
The exposure mapping, network measurement, interference clusters, assignment,
attrition, and target population must travel with the estimate.

Centola's randomized online-community experiment found stronger adoption with
multiple-neighbour reinforcement and farther/faster spread in the clustered
condition for the studied health-registration behaviour
([Centola 2010](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1185231)). This is a valuable
bounded source fact and already appears in [C-430](../claims.md#c-430). It is
not a general claim that clustering improves truth, safety, innovation, or
social welfare.

### Formal organizations and enacted work are different records

Meyer and Rowan argue that formal structures can reflect institutionalized
rules and become decoupled from ongoing activity
([Meyer and Rowan 1977](https://doi.org/10.1086/226550)). Feldman and Pentland
distinguish the ostensive aspect of a routine from concrete performances and
show how their interaction can generate both stability and change
([Feldman and Pentland 2003](https://doi.org/10.2307/3556620)).

For project use, a policy record $P_v$ and a performance record $X_t$ remain
separate:

$$
X_t\sim p(X_t\mid P_v,\text{role}_t,\text{resources}_t,
\text{incentives}_t,\text{exceptions}_t,\text{history}_t).
$$

A policy document can constrain action, supply a shared account, signal
legitimacy, or be ignored in a local episode. An event log can omit informal
coordination, unreported failure, discretionary work, or work outside the
system. The transferable rule is not "organizations lie." It is to measure
formal rule, shared account, actual performance, resources, exceptions,
reporting, and outcome separately, then localize the gap.

This field content is largely a domain-specific refinement of the existing
high-reliability and institutional corpus, especially
[C-173](../claims.md#c-173), [C-179](../claims.md#c-179), and the social-choice
claims C-355–C-395. It does not justify a new organizational-intelligence
principle.

### Media exposure is a staged, selected process

White's gatekeeper case study demonstrated selection among candidate news
items at an editorial gate
([White 1950](https://doi.org/10.1177/107769905002700403)). McCombs and Shaw's
agenda-setting study linked patterns of issue emphasis and public issue
salience in a bounded election setting
([McCombs and Shaw 1972](https://doi.org/10.1086/267990)). These sources support
selection and salience hypotheses, not a generic equation between prominence
and persuasion.

A modern exposure chain should distinguish at least

$$
C\rightarrow S\rightarrow E\rightarrow R\rightarrow I\rightarrow
V\rightarrow A\rightarrow H\rightarrow B\rightarrow O,
$$

where $C$ is produced content, $S$ editorial/source selection, $E$ platform
eligibility, $R$ ranking, $I$ impression delivery, $V$ opportunity to view,
$A$ attention, $H$ comprehension or recall, $B$ belief or attitude, and $O$
action or later outcome. The chain is definitional, not an assumption that
each transition is independent or correctly observed.

Bakshy, Messing, and Adamic separated friend-network composition, algorithmic
ranking, and individual clicking in a large Facebook study
([Bakshy et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaa1160)). Slater's
reinforcing-spirals model makes selection and effects mutually endogenous over
time
([Slater 2007](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1468-2885.2007.00296.x)). Both make a
cross-sectional "content caused belief" claim unsafe without an identifying
design.

Bail and colleagues' Twitter field experiment found increased polarization
among one treatment subgroup and a smaller, statistically uncertain movement
among another after incentivized exposure to opposing elites
([Bail et al. 2018](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1804840115)). Kalla and
Broockman's meta-analysis and field experiments found minimal average
persuasive effects of campaign contact in the studied general elections
([Kalla and Broockman 2018](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0003055417000363)). These
are mature hostile cases against universal persuasion, universal
depolarization, and universal backfire claims.

### Mixed methods need an error-root graph, not a vote

Campbell and Fiske's multitrait–multimethod matrix separates convergence for a
trait from correlations induced by a method
([Campbell and Fiske 1959](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0046016)). Jick describes
triangulation through qualitative and quantitative integration
([Jick 1979](https://doi.org/10.2307/2392366)). Greene, Caracelli, and Graham
identify triangulation, complementarity, development, initiation, and
expansion as distinct mixed-method purposes and find misuse where declared
triangulation was not matched by design
([Greene et al. 1989](https://doi.org/10.3102/01623737011003255)).

If method $m$ estimates target $\theta$ as

$$
T_m=\theta+b_{r(m)}+\epsilon_m,
$$

then $b_{r(m)}$ is a failure root shared by every method with mapping $r(m)$.
Adding methods that share the same frame, construct, platform, participants,
coder, codebook, calibration, or preprocessing does not average out that root.
This is already the general result in [C-1208](../claims.md#c-1208).

The operational integration record should declare:

1. the purpose for combining methods;
2. target constructs and estimands per method;
3. shared and method-specific failure roots;
4. expected agreement, complementarity, silence, and contradiction;
5. the integration and adjudication rule frozen before results; and
6. which disagreement triggers instrument repair, new data, bounded plurality,
   or abstention.

Agreement can increase confidence only under an explicit dependence model.
Disagreement must not be deleted merely to produce a single narrative.

### Participant, gatekeeper, community, legal, and ethics authority are distinct

Direct social research often crosses several authority layers:

- a person may agree to one interview or observation purpose;
- an organization may permit access to a site or record system;
- a research ethics body may approve a protocol within its remit;
- a controller must have an applicable GDPR legal basis and satisfy every
  other applicable GDPR requirement;
- special-category processing may require an Article 9 condition and national
  safeguards;
- a named community may have legitimate collective authority over specific
  knowledge, relationships, materials, or purposes; and
- funders, professions, repositories, and contracts may impose additional
  duties.

No layer automatically grants the others. A manager cannot waive a worker's
data rights. One participant cannot authorize use on behalf of every member of
a group. An ethics approval does not establish scientific validity. A GDPR
legal basis does not exhaust community authority, as already established in
C-1223–C-1230. Conversely, a collective decision cannot remove an individual's
applicable rights.

The EASA data-governance statement treats informed consent in ethnography as an
ongoing process and emphasizes relational custodianship and context-sensitive
access
([EASA 2018](https://easaonline.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/EASA-statement-on-data-governance.pdf)).
This is professional guidance, not legislation. It does not create a general
exception from GDPR or a universal community decision rule.

## Proposed situated social-observation record

The speculative record is

$$
\mathcal{S}=(K,Q,F,R,N,O,E,M,U,L,D),
$$

with:

- $K$: construct, estimand, population, setting, time, contrast, and invariance
  support;
- $Q$: question, scale, category, language, mode, order, codebook, classifier,
  and mapping versions;
- $F$: frame, sample, contact, access, response, missingness, attrition,
  weights, and support;
- $R$: researcher role, positional relations, participant awareness,
  interaction, reactivity, field events, negative cases, and interpretation;
- $N$: network definition, assignment, exposure mapping, interference set,
  homophily/common-cause assumptions, and estimands;
- $O$: organization, policy version, shared account, performance, role,
  resources, exception, workaround, and reporting process;
- $E$: media source, content, selection, eligibility, ranking, delivery,
  attention, comprehension, belief, and action stages;
- $M$: method purpose, shared roots, method-specific roots, disagreement,
  adjudication, and unmeasured alternatives;
- $U$: person agreement, legal basis, special-category condition, information
  duty, ethics review, gatekeeper access, named collective authority, purpose,
  refusal, expiry, remedy, and human decision;
- $L$: lineage, copies, transformations, derivatives, and affected claims; and
- $D$: decision scope, assurance class, unsupported directions, supersession,
  and invalidation edges.

This tuple is a schema proposal, not a causal model or ethical decision maker.
It composes only through typed operations. Unknown authority remains unknown;
failed measurement invariance blocks the unsupported comparison; zero sampling
support blocks population reconstruction; an unregistered exposure mapping
blocks a spillover claim; and a changed policy, category, codebook, consent,
or authority version invalidates only the descendants that depend on it.

## CPU-only workstation falsification package

### Common execution contract

Every world is synthetic and deterministic from committed seeds. Pilot uses 8
paired seeds; confirmation uses 32 fresh paired seeds frozen before pilot
inspection. One run uses at most 8 logical CPU threads, 16 GiB peak resident
memory, and 80 GiB combined temporary plus durable storage. GPU, network,
proprietary data, human participants, scraped traces, manual result editing,
and hidden expert labels are prohibited.

Every arm receives identical latent worlds, observed channels, split,
tuning-call count, and CPU ceiling. Generation, failed fits, tuning,
calibration, sensitivity analysis, invalidation, reruns, serialization, and
review are inside the lifecycle boundary. Every run records:

- target estimands, denominators, support, false claims, abstentions, interval
  coverage, subgroup outcomes, and adverse decisions;
- p50/p95/p99 latency [s], throughput [episodes/s], CPU and wall time [s], peak
  RSS [bytes], and bytes read, written, and retained;
- generator, seed, split, software, operating system, CPU, thread count,
  configuration, codebook, category, rule-set, and artifact hashes;
- prescribed setup, coding, adjudication, legal/ethics-routing, and review time
  [person-s]; and
- plug-level whole-workstation energy [J] only when a calibrated external
  meter, idle baseline, integration interval, and uncertainty are available.

energy is `not measured`; CPU and wall ceilings still bind. Electricity caps

### WS-DSR-01 — instrument, category, and measurement-invariance stress test

**Generator.** Per seed create six populations of 20,000 synthetic respondents
with three correlated latent constructs, 18 five-category items, 3 languages,
2 administration modes, 2 question orders, and 4 category-system versions.
Draw group- and time-specific loadings, intercepts, thresholds, acquiescence,
primacy, nondifferentiation, recall error, and social-desirability response.
Twenty-five percent of populations are fully scalar-invariant controls; 25%
have loading drift; 25% threshold/intercept drift; and 25% category splitting,
merging, or classifier change that preserves some but not all contrasts.

**Comparators.** Raw summed score; pooled CFA; configural-only CFA; registered
multi-group CFA/ordinal IRT; partial-invariance model with development-only
anchor selection; alignment approximation; and Candidate-014/015 record with
versioned instrument and mapping. Equalize 160 fit/tuning calls per arm. The
true latent scores and invariant items are ineligible ceilings.

**Splits and hostile cases.** Hold out one language, one mode, a reversed
question order, threshold drift without loading drift, loading drift with equal
observed means, category merge that creates a rank reversal, and a group with
no invariant anchor among the development items.

**Metrics.** Latent-mean contrast bias, observed-rank reversal, 95% interval
coverage, invariance-violation precision/recall, false comparability rate,
false universal-category mapping, calibration, abstention under no anchor,
CPU, bytes, and person-minutes.

**Success.** Achieve 92.5–97.5% coverage for supported registered contrasts in
every held-out family; make zero latent-mean claim when no identifying anchor
exists; reduce false group ranking by at least 30% versus the best eligible
invariance model; and show a strict false-claim, compute, state, or review Pareto
gain after schema overhead.

**Kill.** Reject if a conventional multi-group CFA/IRT plus an instrument
version table matches all outcomes; if confirmation truth selects anchors; if
acceptable global fit is scored as invariance; or if a category mapping is
called lossless when it changes a registered estimand.

## Confirmatory analysis and decision contract

The independent unit is the complete latent population-, organization-,
network-, exposure-, method-, or governance-world episode—not an item,
response, edge, impression, case event, or derivative. Pair arms on identical
worlds and cluster units sharing a population, frame, organization, network,
instrument, method root, platform policy, rule set, or authority root.

Hold out whole:

- languages, modes, category revisions, and invariance-failure families;
- frames, access gates, missingness mechanisms, and zero-support strata;
- field roles, organizations, disclosure regimes, and reactivity forms;
- network topologies, exposure mappings, saturations, and latent-homophily
  families;
- policy versions, routines, workarounds, resources, and reporting regimes;
- media sources, ranking policies, channels, effects, and missing-exposure
  processes;
- method-root graphs, construct mismatches, coders, and integration purposes;
  and
- governance schemas, purpose changes, plural-authority cases, and derivative
  topologies.

Freeze generator hashes, eligible models, hyperparameter spaces, tuning-call
counts, estimands, subgroup cuts, confidence/credible intervals, equivalence
margins, resource caps, success gates, and kill rules before confirmation.
Report every seed and failure. Use paired world-level contrasts with cluster-
robust or randomization-based uncertainty. Control the familywise error rate at
5% for each track's declared superiority family. Resource noninferiority and
scientific noninferiority are joint gates; no weighted composite score can
trade a rights violation, unsupported claim, or coverage failure for speed.

Promotion requires:

1. all track-specific mandatory safety and support gates;
2. simultaneous 92.5–97.5% coverage where a 95% target is registered;
3. no hidden truth, held-out outcome, legal answer, or authority label used in
   model, schema, threshold, anchor, or exposure-map selection;
4. superiority or prespecified noninferiority to the strongest eligible mature
   baseline, not merely to a naive comparator;
5. a strict Pareto improvement in at least one consequential false-claim,
   resource, state, or human-review dimension without worsening mandatory
   rights and scientific gates; and
6. replication under one unseen world family and a fresh implementation of the
   scoring harness.

## Mandatory mature null stack

1. **Survey/psychometric null:** cognitive pretesting assumptions; exact
   instrument versions; probability or explicitly bounded nonprobability
   sampling; design weights; response adjustment; MNAR sensitivity;
   multi-group CFA/IRT with partial-invariance rules; and transparent category
   mappings.
2. **Ethnographic null:** declared access and role; dated fieldnotes; negative
   cases; reflexive memos; codebook and coder history; channel comparison;
   participant disagreement; privacy controls; and bounded transfer claims.
3. **Causal/network null:** randomized or clearly justified observational
   design; measured network; registered exposure mapping; direct and spillover
   estimands; partial-interference/randomization inference; positivity and
   sensitivity analysis.
4. **Organizational null:** versioned policy; process/event-log conformance;
   resource and reporting model; interviews and direct checks; exceptions and
   unlogged-work sensitivity; outcome validation.
5. **Media null:** content-universe and selection account; stage-specific
   exposure telemetry; randomized encouragement or perturbation where possible;
   longitudinal/sequential causal model; missing and cross-channel exposure;
   heterogeneity and adverse effects.
6. **Mixed-method null:** predeclared integration purpose; construct/estimand
   crosswalk; multitrait–multimethod or correlated-error model; shared-root
   graph; disagreement matrix; independent negative controls and adjudication.
7. **Governance null:** applicable law and institutional review by qualified
   humans; consent/information materials; data-management and access control;
   named gatekeeper and community processes where relevant; purpose/retention/
   transfer rules; provenance, withdrawals, derivatives, and remedies.

If these ordinary stacks match the proposed record, the proposed composition
is killed. Documentation neatness is not an independent scientific gain.

## Cross-track failure modes

1. **Construct collapse:** item, score, category, behaviour, trace, belief,
   identity, and outcome are treated as one variable.
2. **Instrument amnesia:** wording, mode, order, scale, language, category, or
   codebook changes without invalidating comparisons.
3. **Response-rate fetish:** a higher response rate is reported as lower bias
   without a target statistic and nonresponse analysis.
4. **Support laundering:** weights or a model create precise estimates for
   people, settings, or exposure regimes never observed.
5. **Reflexivity as immunity:** a positionality statement substitutes for
   testing access, reactivity, coding, alternatives, or harm.
6. **Observation supremacy:** observed action is assumed to reveal motive,
   private experience, counterfactual behaviour, or population frequency.
7. **Talk supremacy:** self-report is assumed to establish situated action or
   frequency without a reference period and observation model.
8. **Edge realism:** a platform follow, co-location, or inferred similarity is
   treated as a stable social relation or causal exposure.
9. **SUTVA by omission:** peer effects are estimated while neighbours'
   treatment, spillovers, or overlapping groups are ignored.
10. **Policy literalism:** written procedure or certification is treated as
    enacted compliance.
11. **Deviation moralization:** every workaround is labelled misconduct, or
    every adaptation is celebrated as resilience, without outcomes and
    authority.
12. **Exposure collapse:** availability, impression, view, attention,
    comprehension, belief, and action become one "exposed" Boolean.
13. **Attribution after selection:** platform ranking, user choice, and common
    events are ignored when attributing an outcome to content.
14. **Method-count confidence:** five methods sharing one frame or codebook are
    counted as five independent confirmations.
15. **Consensus deletion:** contradiction, silence, and negative cases are
    removed to make a coherent result.
16. **Authority flattening:** one consent or compliance score overwrites legal
    basis, individual rights, gatekeeper scope, ethics conditions, and named
    community authority.
17. **Synthetic-to-human leap:** performance in a generated world is claimed
    to validate real participant research, legal compliance, or ethical
    legitimacy.
18. **Invisible lifecycle cost:** questionnaire design, translation, coding,
    callbacks, field access, adjudication, legal/ethics review, participant
    burden, telemetry, and remedy are excluded from efficiency.

## Proposed central claims

These are exact proposed wordings for later review. They are not added to the
central ledger by this audit.

1. **Established:** A survey response is conditional on the instrument's
   wording, order, response scale, mode, reference period, language, and
   interactional context; it is not a context-free readout of a latent
   attribute.
2. **Established:** Response rate is not nonresponse bias; bias depends on how
   frame inclusion, access, contact, participation, item response, and attrition
   relate to the target statistic.
3. **Established method boundary:** Group or time comparisons of latent scores
   require measurement-invariance evidence adequate for the declared contrast,
   or an explicit partial-invariance model and anchor set.
4. **Plausible synthesis:** A social category is a versioned measurement
   operation whose question, response options, classifier, context, mappings,
   exclusions, and uncertainty must remain attached to downstream claims.
5. **Established method boundary:** In participant observation, field access,
   researcher role, participant awareness, and observer–observed interaction
   are part of the observation process and can alter both visibility and
   behaviour.
6. **Plausible method synthesis:** Reflexive and positional records can expose
   access, power, role, and interpretation dependencies, but they do not remove
   those dependencies or independently validate an interpretation.
7. **Established for the scoped literature:** Verbal accounts and situated
   action can diverge because they answer different questions under different
   contexts; disagreement is not by itself evidence of deception or of one
   method's universal priority.
8. **Established method boundary:** Social-network effects require explicit
   network measurement, exposure mappings, interference sets, and direct or
   spillover estimands; homophily, shared context, and contagion are not
   identified by diffusion correlation alone.
9. **Plausible-to-established organizational boundary:** Formal organizational
   rules, shared accounts of routines, and concrete performances can diverge
   and therefore require separate measurement with roles, resources,
   exceptions, reporting, and outcomes.
10. **Established method boundary:** Media production, editorial selection,
    platform eligibility, ranking, delivery, opportunity to view, attention,
    comprehension, belief, and action are distinct stages; reach or impression
    counts alone do not establish persuasion.
11. **Established method boundary:** Mixed-method agreement is not independent
    corroboration when methods share constructs, frames, participants, coders,
    calibrations, platforms, preprocessing, or other material failure roots.
12. **Established method boundary:** Mixed-method disagreement can reveal
    construct mismatch, selection, context, or mechanism and must be retained
    until a predeclared integration or adjudication rule resolves or bounds it.
13. **Established governance boundary:** Individual study agreement, GDPR
    consent, another GDPR legal basis, ethics review, gatekeeper permission,
    and named community authorization are distinct and cannot soundly be
    collapsed into one consent or compliance flag.
14. **Speculative project claim:** A versioned, situated social-observation and
    authority record may reduce unsupported comparisons, causal claims, media
    attributions, and unauthorized reuse, but only if it beats complete mature
    method and governance stacks at equal lifecycle cost.

## Audit disposition

- **No new principle:** observation dependence, selection, interference,
  common-mode evidence, versioning, authority, and invalidation are already in
  the registry and candidate set.
- **No new candidate:** the proposed record is a stricter composition test for
  Candidates 007, 009, 014, 015, 019, and 020.
- **Immediate experimental value:** eight complete CPU-only contracts now cover
  instrument drift, measurement invariance, nonresponse, field access,
  reactivity, interference, policy–practice gaps, media stages, triangulation,
  and authority composition.
- **Coverage recommendation:** mark DFG 1.23, OECD 5.4, and bounded ANZSRC 44 as
  dedicated but incomplete; mark ANZSRC 4701 as directly touched but Division
  47 incomplete; keep DFG 1.16 and DFG 1.23-04 adjacent.
- **Promotion gate:** no project claim advances until a registered track beats
  its complete mature null under fresh held-out worlds, mandatory scientific
  and rights gates, and full compute, storage, human, and measured-energy
  accounting.
