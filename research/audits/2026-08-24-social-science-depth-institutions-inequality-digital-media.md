## Executive finding

The gap was real, but it is not a missing catalogue of metaphors. These fields
add **operators between a formal rule, social condition, or event and the
recorded outcome**:

1. public policy is delivered through organizations, frontline judgement,
   capacity, citizen knowledge, compliance work, and psychological cost;
2. inequality and mobility depend on the resource, unit, equivalence rule,
   class or category scheme, support, geography, time, cohort, and absolute or
   relative contrast;
3. a household total does not identify each member's access, time burden,
   bargaining position, or decision authority;
4. migration stocks, flows, first moves, repeat moves, returns, usual
   residence, registration, and administrative revision are different
   quantities;
5. public participation is interdependent and cost-conditioned, so visible
   action is not a direct vote on private support;
6. recorded crime is filtered through visibility, reporting, deployment,
   detection, legal classification, counting, and recording, while deployment
   can change future observations;
7. digital traces inherit a platform, interface, API, ranking, eligibility,
   user practice, policy, and version; and
8. content, exposure, and response sit inside media systems with ownership,
   financing, professional, political, regulatory, and distribution
   structures.

Most mechanisms deduplicate into the project's existing observation,
selection, feedback, versioning, governance, memory, and authority bundles.
The durable contribution is domain-specific refusal logic: do not infer
delivered service from a rule, individual welfare from a household total,
private support from public participation, latent incidence from recorded
crime, a population from an API, or portable media effects from one content
item and one platform.

## Terminology firewall

1. An adopted **policy rule** specifies an authorized formal condition. It is
   not the same as implementation, take-up, treatment, service quality, or
   outcome.
2. **Discretion granted** by an institutional arrangement and **discretion
   used** in a case are different variables. Neither is automatically good or
   arbitrary.
3. **Administrative burden** names learning, compliance, and psychological
   costs in citizen--state interaction. It is not identical to fiscal cost,
   staff workload, or legal invalidity.
4. An **inequality operator** is a declared mapping from a resource and
   population to a contrast or index. There is no unit-free scalar called
   inequality that survives changes in resource, unit, denominator, category,
   time, and support.
5. **Absolute mobility** describes observed origin--destination movement;
   **relative mobility** or social fluidity concerns relative chances after
   accounting for marginal distributions. They are not interchangeable.
6. **Equivalised household income** is a household total transformed by a
   declared equivalence scale and commonly attributed to household members for
   indicator construction. It is not a direct observation of within-household
   allocation.
7. **Paid work**, **unpaid care**, housework, availability, and decision
   authority are distinct quantities with distinct time and valuation units.
8. A migration **stock** is a state at a time; immigration and emigration are
   flows over an interval; first, repeat, and return migration are event types.
9. Eurostat's statistical **usual residence** uses a twelve-month actual or
   intended-stay rule. It is not citizenship, registration, asylum status, or
   every ordinary-language use of residence.
10. **Participation** is observed action under a setting, expectation, cost,
    network, risk, and opportunity. It is not a direct readout of private
    preference, legitimacy, or consent.
11. A **threshold model** is a scoped generative model of interdependent
    choice. It is not evidence that every protest, vote, strike, migration, or
    diffusion follows one threshold mechanism.
12. A latent **incident**, a victim or witness report, a police-discovered
    incident, an offence under law, a recorded offence, a suspect, a case, a
    prosecution, a conviction, and a prisoner count are different units and
    stages.
13. **Police-recorded crime** is an administrative output. It is not a census
    of all criminal incidents and has no sampling error merely because it is
    administrative data.
14. A **digital trace** is an event made recordable by a technical and social
    system. It is not automatically an action, intention, identity, person, or
    population unit.
15. An **API sample** has endpoints, permissions, fields, filters, quotas,
    time windows, ranking or eligibility rules, and versions. Public
    availability does not establish representativeness, lawful reuse, or
    research ethics.
16. **Digital ethnography** retains field role, context, interaction,
    interpretation, and ethics; downloading large trace tables does not by
    itself make a study ethnographic.
17. A **media system** is a comparative analytical object involving political,
    economic, professional, legal, organizational, and distribution
    structures. It is not a permanent national essence.
18. **Media pluralism risk**, news diversity, item exposure, persuasion, and
    democratic quality are different constructs. A normative monitor is not a
    causal outcome model.

## Evidence synthesis

### Political science and public administration: a rule is not its delivery

Moynihan, Herd, and Harvey define administrative burden through learning,
psychological, and compliance costs in citizen--state interaction. The
construct matters because a formally available programme can still require
people to discover it, determine eligibility, produce documents, wait, travel,
repeat information, tolerate stigma or stress, and navigate correction or
appeal. Those costs can affect take-up and distribution; their existence does
not establish one magnitude or one remedy for every programme.

Andreotti, Coletto, and Rio compare implementation of the same Italian minimum-
income policy in two regional welfare systems. Their fieldwork distinguishes
discretion granted from discretion used and links variation in frontline
practice to micro-, meso-, and macro-level conditions. This is scoped evidence
from one policy and two regions, not a universal coefficient for discretion.
It nonetheless blocks a rule-only representation of policy delivery.

The inferential chain is therefore:

```text
formal rule -> institutional arrangement -> organizational capacity
            -> frontline encounter/practice -> citizen burden and take-up
            -> delivered service -> near and downstream outcomes
```

Each arrow can fail or vary. A formal compliance check observes only part of
the chain. This extends, but does not replace, the prior audit's distinction
between formal rules and enacted routines.

**Evidence status:** the administrative-burden dimensions are established in
public-administration scholarship; the Italian comparison is direct but
context-bounded empirical evidence; any effect size, distribution, or proposed
AI translation remains a hypothesis.

**Failure modes:** policy literalism; form-length proxies for burden; treating
all discretion as error; celebrating all discretion as responsiveness;
excluding non-applicants from an uptake analysis; conditioning only on people
who survived the process; and counting administrative completion as welfare.

### Stratification and inequality: declare the operator

Eurostat's EU-SILC methodology shows why an income indicator is an operator,
not a raw fact. The target is people in private households. Total disposable
household income aggregates personal and household income and subtracts taxes
and transfers. Equivalised disposable income divides that total by the
modified-OECD equivalence scale and attributes the result to household
members. Countries may combine interviews, administrative sources, and
imputation within the common framework.

Breen's comparative European mobility volume and Bukodi and Goldthorpe's
analysis of thirty European countries distinguish absolute mobility from
relative class-mobility chances and condition conclusions on origin and
destination classes, marginal distributions, sex, country group, and model.
The cited studies establish neither one timeless European mobility rate nor a
universal class ontology. They establish that the contrast and classification
are part of the result.

A defensible inequality or mobility record must therefore bind at least:

- resource or destination: income, wealth, consumption, deprivation,
  occupation, education, health, time, power, or another declared outcome;
- unit: person, tax unit, benefit unit, household, family, organization,
  region, or cohort;
- transformation: currency/base year, equivalence scale, deflator, rank,
  category collapse, top-coding, imputation, and weighting;
- target population, frame, exclusions, geography, support, and missingness;
- time: measurement window, age, period, cohort, and origin/destination stage;
- contrast: level, gap, ratio, percentile, concentration index, absolute
  mobility, relative odds, or another declared operator; and
- uncertainty, sensitivity set, and version.

**Evidence status:** established official-statistics and stratification-method
boundary. A sign reversal is possible in constructed or empirical cases but is
not asserted for every reasonable operator.

**Failure modes:** treating equivalised income as observed individual command
over resources; mixing wealth and income; comparing category systems after a
lossy crosswalk; using absolute mobility as social fluidity; hiding cohort and
geography; Simpson-style aggregation reversal; zero-support extrapolation;
and interpreting a precise index as construct completeness.

### Family, work, care, and migration: totals hide allocation and movement

Chiappori's collective household model gives household members their own
preferences and represents decisions as Pareto efficient with a sharing rule,
yielding testable restrictions for labour supply. It is a formal economic
model, not proof that every household is Pareto efficient. Its value here is a
non-identification boundary: the unitary household is a substantive
assumption, and a household total alone does not reveal individual allocation.

Eurofound's 2025 EU report distinguishes paid from unpaid care, recipient from
carer, and legal or policy definitions across Member States. The report uses a
declared survey and correspondent evidence base and documents definitional and
visibility problems. It does not make every minute of care commensurable or
establish that one household member has a fixed burden because of group
membership. It does require time use, recipient need, intensity, co-residence,
employment, wellbeing, and policy scope to remain separate.

Migration introduces a second aggregation hazard. Eurostat defines immigration
and emigration through establishment or cessation of usual residence for at
least twelve months and allows countries to use available national sources
while adhering to common definitions. Its 2026 metadata documents source and
implementation variation. Massey and Espinosa separately model first, repeat,
and return migration through individual, household, community, and
macroeconomic predictors. Their Mexico--US evidence is comparative scholarship,
not an EU legal or demographic rule.

**Evidence status:** the household-total non-identification is established
mathematically; the EU care distinctions are authoritative descriptive
evidence with survey and definition limits; migration semantics and source
dependencies are established official-statistics boundaries; migration causal
mechanisms remain population- and design-qualified.

**Failure modes:** unitary-household default; equal-per-capita allocation by
fiat; unpaid care disappearing from work models; double-counted simultaneous
care; currency without base year; stock/flow confusion; citizenship treated as
residence; non-registration treated as non-movement; first, repeat, and return
moves pooled; and migrant outcomes conditioned on selected movers without a
target estimand.

### Collective action and institutions: visible action is not private support

Granovetter's threshold model demonstrates that groups with similar average
preferences can produce sharply different aggregate outcomes when the
distribution of interdependent thresholds differs. The project already holds
this result as `C-429`; it is not a new principle here.

Opp and Roehl supply a hostile empirical case against a universal repression
coefficient. In their West German anti-nuclear-power panel, repression enters
as a direct cost that can deter protest, while perceived illegitimacy, group
support, and micromobilization can create indirect mobilizing effects. The
study is bounded by its population, period, measures, and model. It supports a
conditional mechanism test, not a prediction for every protest setting.

The additional inference boundary is that nonparticipation may mean opposition,
indifference, insufficient expected participation, lack of resources,
unacceptable risk, exclusion, timing conflict, or strategic silence. Likewise,
participation does not prove endorsement of every goal, tactic, leader, or
institution. Surveys, field observation, organizational records, network
measures, and event counts answer different questions and retain their own
selection processes.

**Evidence status:** established formal threshold result and scoped empirical
support for sign-heterogeneous repression mechanisms. Private-support
inference is a plausible-to-established synthesis that must be tested under
each observation design.

**Failure modes:** ecological inference from turnout or protest; silence as
consent; attendance as complete endorsement; average-threshold collapse;
fixed-network assumption; repression assigned one sign; event count as movement
strength; and institution legitimacy inferred from participation alone.

### Criminology: incidence, recording, and intervention are different processes

Eurostat warns that its crime figures concern offences recorded by authorities
and that direct cross-country or time comparisons can mislead because law,
definitions, reporting, institutions, counting rules, recording stages, and
production systems differ. Its current metadata distinguishes police,
prosecution, courts, and prisons, plus offence, person, case, proceeding, and
stock-like prison measures. Administrative completeness does not remove those
construct and comparability dependencies.

Ensign and colleagues formalize a separate feedback risk: if discovered
incidents update where police are deployed, deployment affects what is later
discovered. Their result is a mathematical and simulation result for the
declared predictive-policing model, not evidence that every real police system
has the same feedback magnitude or that reported incidents are unbiased.

A minimal observation chain is:

```text
latent incident -> visibility/report -> deployment and detection
                -> legal classification -> counting and recording
                -> prosecution/court/prison stages
```

No recorded count alone identifies every upstream stage. Independent or
purposefully varied observation can help, but no single victimization survey,
administrative source, or model is complete.

**Evidence status:** established authoritative administrative-statistics
boundary; established feedback possibility in a scoped formal model; any
real-world causal magnitude is unresolved.

**Failure modes:** recorded-crime realism; arrest as offence incidence;
offences, people, and cases mixed; reporting changes treated as incidence
changes; enforcement intensity omitted; algorithmic deployment trained on its
own discoveries; protected-group disparity interpreted without selection and
measurement analysis; and compliance claimed from predictive accuracy.

### Digital ethnography and platform methods: the platform is in the instrument

Tufekci identifies single-platform overreach, hashtag selection, denominator
ambiguity, field effects, cross-platform ecology, algorithmic invisibility,
and ambiguous meanings of online actions as threats to social-media inference.
Freelon shows that platform API closure can make a previously teachable,
terms-compliant collection method obsolete. These are not merely missing
metadata: the provider can change the observable universe and the feasible
replication path.

Digital ethnography adds rather than removes the prior social audit's field
requirements. Researcher presence, account identity, disclosure, interaction,
moderation, community norms, interface walkthrough, off-platform context,
screenshots or deletions, and interpretation history can all affect what is
visible and what an action means. An API table can complement field inquiry;
it cannot silently replace it.

The DSA and its delegated regulation create a specific vetted-researcher route
for data held by VLOPs and VLOSEs in systemic-risk research. That legal route
does not guarantee access to every desired datum, historical API
reproducibility, a population denominator, independence from provider-produced
fields, or a valid inference. GDPR, ethics, platform conditions, security, and
other law remain distinct.

**Evidence status:** established methodological and infrastructure dependency;
the value of any proposed platform-context record remains a hypothesis.

**Failure modes:** public-equals-free; API-equals-population; account-equals-
person; hashtag-equals-movement; trace-equals-intention; deletion-equals-
absence; platform version omitted; recommender eligibility treated as user
choice; provider dashboard treated as independent corroboration; scraping
used to evade a denied or unavailable lawful route; and ethics reduced to
anonymizing usernames.

### Media systems: item effects are nested in political economies

Brüggemann and colleagues operationalize dimensions from comparative media-
system research and identify four empirical clusters among the Western systems
they study. Their result revises a typology; it does not establish immutable
national types. Humprecht and Esser analyse 1,660 stories from 48 sites in six
countries and associate online-news diversity with ownership and media-system
context, including public-service media. The design is comparative content
analysis, not randomized identification of every macro-level cause.

The 2025 Media Pluralism Monitor is a diagnostic, normative risk instrument
with declared dimensions including fundamental protection, market plurality,
political independence, and social inclusiveness. It is useful context and an
authoritative European research-policy source; its risk scores are not ground
truth labels for causal model training. The European Media Freedom Act supplies
binding institutional requirements within scope, not evidence that a media
system has achieved diversity or independence.

Media inference therefore needs at least three levels:

1. **micro:** item, source, user choice, opportunity to view, attention,
   comprehension, belief, and action;
2. **meso:** newsroom, ownership, financing, employment, editorial routines,
   platform and distributor relationships; and
3. **macro:** market structure, public-service provision, political
   parallelism, professional norms, regulation, language, and historical
   institutions.

**Evidence status:** established existence of system-level variation and
bounded empirical association with diversity; causal portability of a content
or platform effect across systems remains unresolved.

**Failure modes:** national essence; country fixed effect as explanation;
ownership name without control structure; public/private as the only axis;
pluralism-risk score as causal truth; item-only transport; platform-only media
model; media law as observed compliance; and democratic value collapsed into
clicks, reach, or diversity count.

## Mathematical and measurement frame

The expressions below are audit scaffolds for falsification. They are not
claimed universal laws of society.

### Public-service delivery

For person or case (i), organization (o), frontline role (j), and time
(t), represent a delivered outcome as

\[
Y_{iojt}=F(R_{it},A_{ot},K_{ot},D_{jot},B_{it},Q_{iot},U_{it}),
\]

where:

- (R_{it}) is the formal rule and declared eligibility state;
- (A_{ot}) is the multilevel institutional arrangement and authority;
- (K_{ot}) is organizational capacity, with explicit staff-time or service-
  capacity units where applicable;
- (D_{jot}) is discretion granted and used;
- (B_{it}) is a vector of learning, psychological, and compliance costs;
- (Q_{iot}) is encounter and implementation quality; and
- (U_{it}) contains other declared determinants and uncertainty.

Observing (R_{it}) alone does not identify the other arguments or (Y). A
cost must carry its unit: minutes, visits, euros at a named price/base year,
documents, failures, or a validated scale. These are not silently summed.

### Inequality and mobility operators

Write an estimate as

\[
\widehat{I}=\mathcal I(X; u,e,c,w,S,g,t,k,m),
\]

where (X) is the resource or origin--destination table, (u) the unit, (e)
an equivalence or scaling rule, (c) the category system, (w) the survey or
analysis weights, (S) the supported target population, (g) geography,
(t) period, (k) cohort or life stage, and (m) the declared contrast or
model. Comparisons are admissible only when these arguments are compatible or
a registered sensitivity analysis exposes their effect.

For household (h),

\[
X_h=\sum_{i\in h}x_{ih}
\]

does not identify the vector ((x_{1h},\ldots,x_{n_hh})), time-use vector, or
decision-authority vector. Many allocations have the same total. Equivalising
(X_h) changes the comparison unit; it does not observe those allocations.

### Migration observation

Let latent movement events be (M^ast). An official series is more accurately
represented as

\[
M^{obs}_{g,t}=\mathcal O_M(M^ast; r_{12},s_g,a_g,d_g,v_t),
\]

where (r_{12}) is the twelve-month usual-residence criterion, (s_g) the
source system, (a_g) arrival registration, (d_g) departure de-registration
or estimation, and (v_t) the statistical version/revision. Stock is measured
in persons at a date; flow in person-events over an interval. A person can
contribute multiple flow events, so the units must not be merged.

### Interdependent participation

A synthetic threshold world may use

\[
A_i=\mathbf 1\!left[
E_i\{G_i(A_{-i},N_i)\}-C_i-R_i+L_i \ge 0
\right],
\]

where (A_i) is observed participation, (A_{-i}) others' participation,
(N_i) a declared network or reference set, (G_i) expected gain, (C_i)
ordinary participation cost, (R_i) repression or risk cost, and (L_i) a
mobilizing term such as group support or perceived illegitimacy. This equation
defines a hostile generator, not a validated psychological model. Neither
(A_i=0) nor (A_i=1) identifies every latent term.

### Recorded crime under adaptive observation

For place/group (g) and time (t), use

\[
Y^{rec}_{g,t}=\mathcal O_C
\left(I^ast_{g,t};q^{vis}_{g,t},q^{rep}_{g,t},d_{g,t},q^{det}_{g,t},
L_t,K_t,V_t\right),
\]

where (I^ast) is latent incidence, (q^{vis}) visibility, (q^{rep})
reporting, (d) deployment, (q^{det}) conditional detection, (L_t) the
legal/classification regime, (K_t) counting rules, and (V_t) the recording
version. If

\[
d_{g,t+1}=\pi(Y^{rec}_{g,\le t},Z_{g,\le t}),
\]

then future observations depend on prior recorded outputs through policy
(pi). Independent audits, randomized or otherwise justified exploration,
and separate reported/discovered channels are comparator methods; none is
assumed lawful or sufficient for real deployment.

### Platform traces and media-system transport

Represent a trace dataset as

\[
T_v=\mathcal O_P(B;P_v,I_v,E_v,A_v,H_v,Q_v,t),
\]

where (B) is the broader behaviour of interest, (P_v) the platform version,
(I_v) interface, (E_v) eligibility/ranking, (A_v) API/access path, (H_v)
user practices affecting visibility, (Q_v) query/filter, and (t) time. A

## CPU-only hostile and equal-budget test programme

These are complete audit-level contracts, not implemented fixtures. They may
refine Candidates 007, 009, 011, 014, 019, and 020 only after ordinary candidate
authority and promotion review. All worlds are generated; no real social or
administrative record is downloaded.

### Common protocol

- **Independent unit:** one generated world seed. Repeated households, cases,
  incidents, people, blocks, queries, platforms, countries, or time points
  inside a seed are dependent observations and are never counted as independent
  (n).
- **Split:** freeze generator families, train/development seeds, fresh
  confirmation seeds, estimands, subgroup contrasts, sensitivity grid,
  hyperparameter calls, stopping rules, and claim wording before confirmation.
- **Equal budget:** each arm receives the same eligible source variables,
  generator draws, labels, interventions or audits, compute ceiling, storage,
  reviewer minutes, and measured workstation energy. If a method uses more
  metadata, interviews, independent audits, or observation channels, that cost
  is inside its arm.
- **Uncertainty:** estimate paired arm--baseline differences at seed level.
  Report all seeds, point estimates, compatible intervals, multiplicity control
  for the registered claim family, and sensitivity to generator family. Do not
  treat rows or time points within a seed as independent replicates.
- **Mandatory outcomes:** false substantive claims, invalid transfers,
  coverage/refusal errors, calibration, protected or subgroup harm in the
  generator, rights-rule violations, compute, storage, human review, latency,
  and measured energy. No weighted score can trade a rights failure for speed.
- **Typed refusal:** refuse a claim when the target, unit, denominator,
  operator version, support, uncertainty, authority, or required observation
  stage is absent or inconsistent. Abstention is scored for both necessary and
  excessive refusal.
- **Promotion gate:** no claim becomes `claim_eligible` merely because a track
  passes. Existing review, authority, fresh-seed, multiplicity, resource, and
  candidate-promotion gates remain controlling.

### SSD-T01 — Policy implementation and administrative burden

**Question.** Does an operator-qualified implementation record reduce false
service and take-up claims beyond a complete ordinary implementation model?

**Generator.** Produce programmes with identical or changed formal rules;
multilevel authority; staffing and queue capacity; discretion granted and used;
learning, compliance, and psychological costs; eligible non-applicants;
application failure; beneficial and harmful exceptions; appeals; delivered
service; and downstream outcomes.

**Arms.** (A) formal rule/eligibility only; (B) mature policy-implementation
null with process map, capacity, burden survey assumptions, administrative-
data linkage, subgroup uptake, and sensitivity analysis; (C) the proposed
record with the same information and budget.

**Hostile cases.** Stable rule plus capacity collapse; policy expansion plus
unchanged delivery; burden concentrated in one subgroup; discretion correcting
an edge case; discretion introducing arbitrary disparity; non-applicants absent
from administrative data; reported completion without service; beneficial
workaround; and appeal reversal.

**Success and kill.** C must reduce false rule-to-delivery and eligibility-to-
take-up claims relative to B, preserve beneficial exceptions, flag unsupported
subgroups, and stay within joint resource noninferiority. If B matches it, the
composition is killed. No result establishes which real policy is lawful or
desirable.

### SSD-T02 — Stratification and mobility operator sensitivity

## Cross-track failure modes

1. **Rule-to-reality collapse:** a statute, policy, model card, or procedure is
   treated as delivered action or outcome.
2. **Discretion moralized in advance:** all variation is error, or all
   adaptation is beneficial, without case authority and outcomes.
3. **Survivor-only administration:** only applicants, completed cases, or
   recorded users enter the denominator.
4. **Burden scalarization:** minutes, money, documents, stress, stigma, and
   autonomy loss are added without units, measurement, or value judgement.
5. **Inequality without an operator:** a result omits resource, unit,
   population, scale, support, geography, time, and contrast.
6. **Household-person substitution:** equivalised household income is described
   as observed individual access or authority.
7. **Care invisibility:** paid hours are measured while unpaid care and
   simultaneous or non-co-resident care vanish.
8. **Stock-flow-person confusion:** people at a date, movement events over a
   year, citizenship, and registration are pooled.
9. **Ecological support inference:** public participation, turnout, silence, or
   event counts become private preference or legitimacy.
10. **One-sign repression:** risk and intervention are assumed always to deter
    or always to mobilize.
11. **Recorded-crime realism:** administrative records become latent incidence
    without reporting, enforcement, law, units, and recording versions.
12. **Offence-person-case collapse:** offences, suspects, prosecutions,
    convictions, cases, and prisoners share one count or denominator.
13. **Feedback blindness:** an intervention chooses where and what the next
    dataset can observe, then treats those data as passively sampled.
14. **API census:** returned rows or accounts become a population of people.
15. **Provider-root corroboration:** API, dashboard, and archive are counted as
    independent even when all inherit one platform pipeline.
16. **Versionless field:** interface, endpoint, permissions, ranking,
    moderation, query, or policy changes do not invalidate descendants.
17. **Digital-context deletion:** irony, screenshots, silent use, deletion,
    off-platform activity, researcher presence, and community norms disappear.
18. **Public-data permission fallacy:** visibility is treated as lawful basis,
    ethics review, platform authorization, and scientific validity.
19. **National media essence:** a fitted cluster or country label becomes a
    stable causal property.
20. **Pluralism-score realism:** a normative diagnostic becomes a causal truth
    label or complete democratic outcome.
21. **Micro-only media transport:** the same item token is assumed to have the
    same exposure and effect across ownership, financing, distribution,
    language, and regulatory systems.
22. **Law-as-evidence:** legal compliance, a legal prohibition, or a regulated
    category is presented as proof of a scientific mechanism.
23. **Documentation victory:** a larger context record is declared superior
    without false-claim reduction and full lifecycle cost.
24. **Synthetic-to-authority leap:** generated-world performance is used to
    make a real eligibility, policing, employment, political, media, or rights
    decision.

- **Status:** established.
- **Primary sources:** `granovetter1978threshold`,
  `OppRoehl1990RepressionProtest`.
- **Rationale:** the threshold result separates average disposition from the
  distribution that produces aggregate action, while the bounded West German
  panel supports direct deterrent and conditional indirect mobilizing paths;
  neither result identifies every real movement or private stance.
- **Open issue:** compare aggregate, threshold, partial-identification,
  multi-channel survey/field/network, and context-record models across equal-
  mean/different-distribution, deterrence, mobilization, cancellation,
  strategic silence, exclusion, network change, and non-threshold worlds.
- **Used by:** [social-science depth audit](2026-08-24-social-science-depth-institutions-inequality-digital-media.md),
  [Candidate 014](../../experiments/candidates/014-versioned-observation-contract.md),
  [Candidate 019](../../experiments/candidates/019-audited-cumulative-inheritance.md),
  [Candidate 020](../../experiments/candidates/020-constitutional-control-plane.md).

### C-1374

- **Statement:** Police-recorded crime is an output of incident visibility,
  victim or witness reporting, deployment, detection, legal classification,
  counting, and recording; it does not by itself identify latent incidence or
  support direct place/time comparison, and adaptive deployment can make later
  observations endogenous.
- **Status:** established.
- **Primary sources:** `Eurostat2024CrimeMethodologicalGuide`,
  `Eurostat2026CrimeInformation`, `Eurostat2026CrimeMetadata`,
  `EnsignEtAl2018RunawayFeedback`.
- **Rationale:** Eurostat expressly documents administrative-stage, definition,
  unit, recording, and comparability limits, while Ensign and colleagues prove
  and simulate deployment--discovery feedback in their declared predictive-
  policing model; the feedback magnitude in any real system remains
  unresolved.
- **Open issue:** compare recorded-count, multichannel official-statistics,
  independent-audit or victimization-like, feedback-aware, and versioned-
  observation methods under reporting, patrol, law, counting, unit, source,
  feedback, and regime shifts, with no real person-level risk or deployment
  output and with qualified EU/German legal review as a mandatory gate.
- **Used by:** [social-science depth audit](2026-08-24-social-science-depth-institutions-inequality-digital-media.md),
  [Candidate 007](../../experiments/candidates/007-endogenous-observation-surveillance.md),
  [Candidate 014](../../experiments/candidates/014-versioned-observation-contract.md).

### C-1375

- **Statement:** A digital trace dataset is conditional on platform, interface,
  eligibility and ranking, API or access route, query, user visibility
  practices, policy, and version; it is not a census of behaviour, and access
  or version changes can invalidate collection methods and descendant claims.
- **Status:** established.
- **Primary sources:** `Tufekci2014SocialMediaBigData`,
  `Freelon2018PostAPIAge`.
- **Rationale:** single-platform and hashtag frames have denominator,
  representativeness, meaning, and field-effect limits, while provider-
  controlled API closure supplies a direct example of an external dependency
  making a method obsolete.
- **Open issue:** compare returned-row, mature frame-aware computational and
  digital-ethnographic, and dependency-bearing records under API closure,
  eligibility drift, account/person mismatch, silent and off-platform action,
  deletion, common events, provider-correlated roots, and equal researcher,
  data, storage, compute, and energy budgets.
- **Used by:** [social-science depth audit](2026-08-24-social-science-depth-institutions-inequality-digital-media.md),
  [Candidate 009](../../experiments/candidates/009-graded-assurance-envelopes.md),
  [Candidate 014](../../experiments/candidates/014-versioned-observation-contract.md).

### C-1376

- **Statement:** News diversity, exposure, and effects are nested in media-
  system context including ownership and control, financing, professional and
  political institutions, regulation, public-service provision, market and

- **Status:** plausible.
- **Primary sources:** `BruggemannEtAl2014MediaSystems`,

- **Open issue:** compare item/platform-only, complete multilevel comparative,
  transport-sensitivity, and context-record methods under ownership-control,
  financing, public-service, language-market, distribution, regulatory,
  typology-outlier, and system-transition shifts at equal lifecycle cost.
