# Research audits

Audits are dated, scoped passes through primary literature or authoritative
engineering sources. They preserve disagreements, boundary conditions, and
failed deduplications that would be lost in the shorter claim ledger.

An audit does not itself promote a principle. Major observations receive stable
`C-` claims, candidate mechanisms are deduplicated against the
[principle registry](../principle-registry.md), and transfers must survive an
equal-budget [experiment contract](../../experiments/candidates/).
