## Executive finding

Forestry, fisheries, aquaculture, and aquatic food systems do not justify a new
architecture by analogy. They do expose a common failure of aggregate control:

> A renewable-resource controller can improve current yield while eroding age
> structure, spatial refugia, future recruitment, carbon pools, protected
> habitat, genetic integrity, food value, or the institutions that make the
> harvest limit enforceable.

The useful translation is therefore not “manage an AI like a forest” or “use
maximum sustainable yield.” It is a typed control record in which:

1. stock, gross inflow, natural loss, intervention, withdrawal, transfer, and
   inventory correction remain separate;
2. cohort or age, reproductive contribution, spatial cell, connectivity, and
   disturbance history remain visible rather than being reduced to biomass;
3. observation support, selectivity, reporting, discard, mortality, and
   detection processes accompany every inferred state;
4. biological feasibility, production capacity, ecological limits, protected
   outcomes, and authorized social choices are different layers;
5. access, withdrawal, management, exclusion, transfer, monitoring, sanction,
   and appeal rights are assigned to named actors and versions;
6. a feedback rule is tested across alternative operating models, observation
   errors, implementation errors, non-stationarity, and strategic response;
7. food-system credit ends at edible nutrient and protected outcome, not at
   live weight, landed mass, or farm-gate output; and
8. all monitoring, enforcement, displacement, treatment, energy, maintenance,
   and human work is charged to the policy that requires it.

Every component already has mature nulls: age- or size-structured state-space
models, spatial population models, forest growth and carbon-pool accounting,
harvest-control rules, management-strategy evaluation, constrained and robust
control, life-cycle and material-flow assessment, typed IAM, quota ledgers,
randomized audits, and ordinary monitoring and enforcement. The project earns
residue only if an existing candidate composition improves the held-out
quality--risk--cost frontier beyond those nulls.

## Evidence status and transfer boundary

- **Established** means supported for the stated empirical or formal scope. It
  does not mean universal across species, fleets, forests, farms, or climates.
- **Plausible** means a bounded synthesis with evidence for its components but
  no direct test of the complete project translation.
- **Speculative** means a proposed AI systems mechanism or benchmark advantage.
- **Disputed** means material evidence or interpretation conflicts remain.

This audit distinguishes three kinds of transfer:

1. **measurement transfer:** reuse a way of recording stocks, flows, age,
   space, rights, or uncertainty;
2. **control transfer:** compare a feedback structure with existing control and
   operations-research methods; and
3. **metaphor transfer:** borrow field vocabulary without a discriminating
   mechanism.

Only the first two enter experiments. Metaphor transfer is rejected.

## Terms that must remain distinct

### Resource and population state

- **Stock** is an amount present at a specified time and support: organisms,
  biomass, carbon, nutrient, habitat, or stored product in declared units.
- **Flow** is an amount per time or an event-resolved transfer. Growth,
  recruitment, mortality, migration, harvest, discard, decomposition, and
  processing loss are different flows.
- **Cohort** identifies units entering or originating in a shared interval.
  Cohort membership is not age, size, calendar period, or survivor status.
- **Biomass** sums mass. It does not preserve number, age, size, reproductive
  value, genetic structure, or spatial distribution.
- **Spawning-stock biomass** is a model-defined reproductive proxy. Equal mass
  need not imply equal reproductive contribution.
- **Recruitment** is entry into a defined life stage, survey, or fishery. It is
  not birth, total reproduction, or observation probability.
- **Yield** is withdrawn product in declared units and interval. It is not
  profit, edible mass, nutrition, welfare, biodiversity, or future capacity.
- **Sustained yield** is a policy-relative temporal result, not proof that all
  protected ecological or social states remain acceptable.

### Space, property, and action

- **Physical support** is the area, volume, network, migration route, or water
  body over which a state or observation applies.
- **Resource system** is the reproducing or renewing system; a **resource unit**
  is a withdrawn fish, tree, volume, carbon amount, or service. They are not
  interchangeable.
- **Open access** is absence of effective exclusion. It is not the same as
  common property, public ownership, or communal governance.
- **Property or use right** is a socially and legally assigned authority. It
  does not change biological dynamics unless behavior and enforcement change.
- **Quota** is an authorized withdrawal quantity or share. It is not an
  independently measured stock limit, a guarantee of compliance, or ownership
  of the underlying ecosystem.
- **Harvest control rule** maps observed or estimated state to an authorized
  action. Its result depends on observation, assessment, implementation, and
  response error.
- **Protected area or retention patch** changes spatial exposure and allowed
  actions. It does not guarantee connectivity, recruitment export, persistence,
  or net system benefit.
- **Intervention** includes harvest, closure, thinning, planting, stocking,
  treatment, fallowing, feed change, habitat restoration, monitoring, and
  enforcement. Each can change both state and observation.

### Aquaculture and food output

- **Physical carrying capacity** concerns suitable space and physical
  conditions.
- **Production carrying capacity** concerns target output under a production
  model.
- **Ecological carrying capacity** depends on declared ecological components
  and acceptable-change rules.
- **Social carrying capacity** is an authorized trade-off, not an empirical
  constant inferred by the model.
- **Feed-conversion ratio** relates feed mass to farmed biomass under a declared
  boundary. It does not report edible yield, ingredient opportunity cost,
  nutrient retention, pollution, disease, or wild-fish dependence.
- **Integrated multi-trophic aquaculture** couples fed and extractive organisms.
  Co-location is not proof that nutrients are captured, exported in harvest, or
  safe across seasons.
- **Blue/aquatic food** denotes heterogeneous capture and culture systems. A
  category-average footprint is not a product-, gear-, site-, or nutrition-
  specific result.

## Evidence map

### Stock--recruitment and cohort structure

Ricker's stock--recruitment work formalized density-dependent and potentially
overcompensatory relations in particular populations
([doi:10.1139/f54-039](https://doi.org/10.1139/f54-039)). It established a model
family, not a universal law or known parameterization for every fishery.
Environmental variation, observation error, life-history mixing, and changing
selectivity remain separate.

In a 50-year larval-fish record, Anderson and colleagues compared mechanisms
for the amplified variability of exploited populations; age/size truncation and
altered demographic parameters were among the supported pathways
([doi:10.1038/nature06851](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature06851)). A meta-analysis
of 39 Northeast Atlantic stocks found that age-structure effects on recruitment
were not universal
([doi:10.1093/icesjms/fsq032](https://doi.org/10.1093/icesjms/fsq032)). The
defensible systems lesson is therefore conditional: a scalar stock can hide
predictive cohort state, but age structure must earn its complexity by
out-of-sample prediction and intervention performance.

Berkeley and colleagues summarize evidence that older females, spatially
structured reproductive units, and varying recruitment sources can matter for
groundfish persistence
([doi:10.1577/1548-8446(2004)29%5B23:FSVPOA%5D2.0.CO;2](https://doi.org/10.1577/1548-8446%282004%2929%5B23%3AFSVPOA%5D2.0.CO%3B2)).
This does not authorize a universal reserve design or imply that every older
individual has higher reproductive value. It requires size/age, reproductive
output, location, and dispersal to remain testable rather than pre-aggregated.

**Transferable mechanism:** preserve a minimal sufficient structured state only
where it predicts future inflow, fragility, or safe action beyond total stock.

**Nearest project routes:** P-012; Candidates 003, 007, and 014.

### Closed-loop harvest under uncertainty

A management procedure is not just a fitted stock model. It couples monitoring,
assessment or indicator processing, a pre-agreed decision rule, implementation,
and the resource response. Management-strategy evaluation tests that loop
against multiple operating models and errors. Butterworth gives the classical
case for simulation-tested procedures
([doi:10.1093/icesjms/fsm003](https://doi.org/10.1093/icesjms/fsm003)); Goethel
and colleagues also delimit when full MSE is worth its considerable time and
staff cost
([doi:10.1093/icesjms/fsad031](https://doi.org/10.1093/icesjms/fsad031)).

MSE can still be overconfident. Assigning probability distributions to poorly
known quantities or pooling structurally different models can conceal tail and
model-form uncertainty
([doi:10.1093/icesjms/fsp023](https://doi.org/10.1093/icesjms/fsp023)). Thus a
simulator is not evidence about its own completeness. Omitted spatial mixing,
recruitment failure, illegal removal, fleet adaptation, climate regime, or
enforcement capacity remains alternative-model debt.

**Transferable mechanism:** precommit the observation--decision--action loop,
test it against explicit alternative worlds, and retain exceptional-circumstance
and retirement triggers.

**Nearest project routes:** P-003, P-006, P-007, P-009, and P-012; Candidates
007, 011, and 014.

### Forestry: pools, time, disturbance, and spatial retention

Forest growth, standing carbon, harvest, product storage, substitution, and
emissions occur on different supports and horizons. Harmon, Ferrell, and
Franklin's Pacific Northwest simulations found that converting old-growth to
young fast-growing stands reduced on-site carbon and did not recover the old-
growth storage capacity for at least two centuries in that model
([doi:10.1126/science.247.4943.699](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.247.4943.699)).
The number is not a global constant; the enduring boundary is that faster gross
growth does not imply greater total stock or immediate atmospheric benefit.

Mixed-stand effects also resist a slogan. Across 93 European pine--beech plots,
species interactions increased community-level temporal stability, while
population- and individual-level effects differed
([doi:10.1111/1365-2745.12727](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2745.12727)). Other
European studies find drought responses depend on species identity, hydraulic
traits, site, and drought sequence. “More species” is therefore not a control
law; composition, functional differences, scale, and regime must be explicit.

The DEMO variable-retention experiment crossed retained amount and spatial
pattern. Two decades of data show that both can shape the abundance and
dynamics of standing deadwood
([doi:10.1016/j.foreco.2022.120575](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.foreco.2022.120575)).
That experiment is specific to Douglas-fir systems, but it cleanly demonstrates
that equal harvested or retained proportion does not imply equal spatial and
structural outcome.

Storm-risk modeling in Western European forestry also changes optimal thinning
and rotation relative to a no-risk objective
([doi:10.1016/j.forpol.2013.08.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.forpol.2013.08.002)).
Such an optimum remains criterion-, price-, risk-model-, and owner-specific; it
cannot silently replace biodiversity, carbon, soil, water, or public-interest
constraints.

**Transferable mechanism:** conserve pool identity, cohort/structure, spatial
configuration, and delayed liabilities through intervention; never infer total
system benefit from current increment or output alone.

**Nearest project routes:** P-001, P-006, P-008, P-009, P-012, and P-013;
Candidates 001, 003, 014, and 018.

### Rights, monitoring, and enforcement

Property regimes are bundles. Access, withdrawal, management, exclusion, and
alienation can belong to different actors; common property is not open access
([doi:10.2307/3146375](https://doi.org/10.2307/3146375)). This is a
typing and authority lesson, not evidence that one bundle is universally best.

A global observational analysis of 11,135 fisheries associated catch-share
implementation with a halt or reversal in the collapse trend
([doi:10.1126/science.1159478](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1159478)). The
study does not randomize institutions, prove that transferability is the active
component, or eliminate allocation, enforcement, concentration, displacement,
and reporting questions.

Framed field experiments with Chilean artisanal fishers found that co-enforcement
could reduce poaching, while insiders' monitoring investments could offset
harvest earnings
([doi:10.1007/s10640-021-00591-9](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10640-021-00591-9)).
Another artefactual field experiment found that an enforcement institution can
shift behavior toward more destructive gear and faster depletion in the tested
Tanzanian setting
([doi:10.1016/j.ocecoaman.2017.10.021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ocecoaman.2017.10.021)).
Direction and cost depend on the coupled rules, alternatives, monitoring, and
local institution—not on the word “enforcement.”

**Transferable mechanism:** bind each control action to a typed holder, resource
scope, evidence, duty, expiry, audit path, sanction, and contest route; measure
strategic response and distribution beside aggregate performance.

**Nearest project routes:** P-002, P-008, P-009, and P-013; Candidates 008, 011,
012, and 020.

### Aquaculture capacity and farm--wild coupling

McKindsey and colleagues distinguish physical, production, ecological, and
social carrying capacity for bivalve culture
([doi:10.1016/j.aquaculture.2006.06.044](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aquaculture.2006.06.044)).
The categories depend on different models and, for ecological and social
limits, declared components and acceptable change. Treating “capacity” as one
learned scalar would erase authority and failure semantics.

Aquaculture is spatially open even when the inventory is privately held.
Krkošek, Lewis, and Volpe estimated elevated sea-louse infection pressure along
one wild-salmon migration corridor near an isolated farm
([doi:10.1098/rspb.2004.3027](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2004.3027)); a later
coupled analysis estimated farm-origin parasite effects in several sympatric
populations
([doi:10.1073/pnas.0603525103](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0603525103)). These
results are system-specific and historically disputed in magnitude, so farm,
wild host, hydrodynamic, temperature, treatment, migration, and alternative-
source uncertainty must remain explicit.

Escaped domesticated Atlantic salmon have introgressed into sampled wild
populations, with effects and detectability depending on population and
environment
([doi:10.1111/faf.12214](https://doi.org/10.1111/faf.12214)). Absence of a
visible phenotype is not evidence of no genetic change; escape count, survival,
reproduction, marker detection, and descendant fitness form a delayed chain.

Integrated multi-trophic systems can recover a fraction of released material,
but the result is geometry-, species-, season-, and boundary-dependent. A
60-day French recirculating experiment coupled sea bass, algal ponds, and
oysters and reported nitrogen/phosphorus bioremediation outcomes
([doi:10.1016/j.aquaculture.2019.02.013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aquaculture.2019.02.013)).
That experiment does not establish a closed loop or long-run disease, energy,
product-safety, or economic performance.

**Transferable mechanism:** type each capacity and externality, measure the
transport pathway, and verify realized capture or containment rather than
crediting nominal coupling.

**Nearest project routes:** P-006, P-007, P-008, P-009, and P-013; Candidates
003, 007, 013, and 014.

### Aquatic food systems: endpoint and opportunity cost

Standardized estimates across aquatic-food groups show large differences in
greenhouse-gas, nitrogen, phosphorus, freshwater, and land stressors, with
uncertainty ranges and functional-unit sensitivity
([doi:10.1038/s41586-021-03889-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03889-2)).
These are comparative model estimates, not a permanent ranking for every farm,
gear, location, or diet.

Feed efficiency has improved, but marine-ingredient dependence and new land-
based feed dependencies remain in the system boundary
([doi:10.1038/s41586-021-03308-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03308-6)).
A Norwegian salmon analysis found net consumption of several edible nutrients
available in wild feed fish even where fish-biomass balance appeared neutral
([doi:10.1038/s43016-024-00932-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43016-024-00932-z)).
Its accounting choices and species boundary remain explicit; the result is not
a universal verdict on salmon or aquaculture.

**Transferable mechanism:** carry the resource through processing yield,
edible fraction, nutrient vector, safety, substitution counterfactual, and
distribution rather than rewarding an intermediate mass proxy.

**Nearest project routes:** P-001, P-009, P-012, and P-013; Candidates 014,
017, and 018.

## Formal state contract

### Typed event record

Every synthetic or later empirical unit uses:

$$
e=(u,k,x,t^-,t^+,q,[q],a,c,r,o,i,v,p),
$$

where:

- $u$ is immutable unit, population, stand, farm, vessel, lot, or resource ID;
- $k$ is event kind: recruitment, growth, transfer, mortality, harvest,
  discard, planting, treatment, escape, processing, or correction;
- $x$ is spatial support in metres, square metres, cubic metres, a polygon, or
  an explicit graph subset;
- $[t^-,t^+]$ is event or integration support in seconds or declared dates;
- $q$ is signed quantity and $[q]$ its unit;
- $a$ is age, size, life-stage, or stand-structure state where applicable;
- $c$ is cohort or origin identifier;
- $r$ is rights and authority version;
- $o$ is observation/operator version and inclusion probability where known;
- $i$ is intervention and implementation state;
- $v$ is biological, accounting, model, and policy version;
- $p$ is provenance, uncertainty class, and supersession relation.

A state reconstructed from events must expose a conservation residual. Silent
inventory correction is forbidden.

### Age- and space-structured fish population

For age class $a$, spatial cell $x$, and year $t$:

$$
N_{a+1,x,t+1}
=\sum_y P_{a,y\rightarrow x,t}
N_{a,y,t}\exp[-(M_{a,y,t}+F_{a,y,t})]+\eta_{a,x,t},
$$

where $N$ is abundance in fish, $P$ is a dimensionless movement probability,
$M$ and $F$ are instantaneous natural and fishing mortality in
$\mathrm{year}^{-1}$, and $\eta$ is a correction or unmodeled flow in fish that
must be reported rather than absorbed. Recruitment $N_{0,x,t}$ follows a
registered competing family such as Ricker, Beverton--Holt, a regime model, or
a nonparametric relation; no family is designated truth across all fixtures.

Biomass and spawning proxy are different summaries:

$$
B_t=\sum_{a,x} w_{a,x,t}N_{a,x,t},\qquad
S_t=\sum_{a,x} m_{a,x,t}f_{a,x,t}w_{a,x,t}N_{a,x,t},
$$

where $w$ is mean mass in kilograms per fish, $m$ is mature fraction, and $f$
is a dimensionless, explicitly modeled reproductive-value modifier. Setting
$f=1$ is an assumption, not a measurement result.

Observed catch is an operator on latent removal:

$$
y_{g,a,x,t}=\rho_{g,a,x,t}
H_{g,a,x,t}+\epsilon_{g,a,x,t},
$$

where $H$ is total removal in kilograms, $\rho$ is the recorded fraction after
gear selectivity, discard, illegal/unreported removal, and reporting processes,
and $\epsilon$ is measurement error in kilograms. Landings alone do not identify
$H$.

### Forest pool and harvest accounting

For carbon pool $p$ and spatial cell $x$:

$$
C_{p,x,t+1}=C_{p,x,t}+G_{p,x,t}-L_{p,x,t}-H_{p,x,t}
+\sum_j T_{j\rightarrow p,x,t}-\sum_j T_{p\rightarrow j,x,t},
$$

where all terms are tonnes of carbon per cell per interval. Pools include at
least live above-ground biomass, live below-ground biomass, deadwood, litter,
soil organic carbon, and harvested-wood products when the declared boundary
requires them. $G$ is gross addition, $L$ natural or disturbance loss, $H$
export by harvest, and $T$ a pool transfer. Atmospheric flux, product lifetime,
displacement/substitution, leakage, albedo, and counterfactual land use are
separate records.

Current increment $G$ cannot substitute for $C$, net system change, or avoided
emissions. Carbon benefit is reported only against a versioned counterfactual
and sensitivity range.

### Aquaculture material and nutrient balance

For nutrient $n$ over farm boundary $\Omega$ and interval $T$:

$$
I_n+J_n
=\Delta A_n+E_n+W_n+D_n+X_n+R_n,
$$

where all terms are kilograms of nutrient $n$: $I$ feed and seed input, $J$
environmental import, $\Delta A$ inventory change, $E$ edible harvest, $W$
non-edible harvested material, $D$ dissolved/particulate discharge, $X$ escape
or uncontrolled export, and $R$ mortality, treatment, processing, and measured
residual losses. An extractive co-culture receives credit only through measured
$E_n+W_n$ exported from the receiving environment; standing biomass is not
permanent removal.

Capacity is a typed vector:

$$
\mathbf K=(K_{physical},K_{production},
K_{ecological}^{\mathcal V,\tau},K_{social}^{A,r}),
$$

where $\mathcal V$ is the registered ecological indicator set, $\tau$ its
authorized thresholds, $A$ the authority, and $r$ the rule version. The
components have different meanings and need not share units.

### Rights and enforcement record

For actor $j$, resource support $\Omega$, and rule version $r$:

$$
\mathcal R_{j,\Omega,r}
=(A,W,M,E,L,D,S,Q),
$$

where $A$ is access, $W$ withdrawal, $M$ management, $E$ exclusion, $L$
alienation or transfer, $D$ duties, $S$ sanctions/appeal, and $Q$ quota or action
envelope. Each field is typed, scoped, time-bounded, and authorized; a Boolean
“owns resource” field is forbidden.

### Outcome vector and uncertainty

Report without scalar collapse:

$$
\mathbf z=(Y,B,S,A_g,C,E_n,D_n,H_p,R_s,U,L,J),
$$

where $Y$ is harvested/produced output in its declared unit, $B$ total stock,
$S$ reproductive or regenerative structure, $A_g$ age/genetic/spatial structure,
$C$ carbon-pool vector in tonnes C, $E_n$ edible nutrient vector in kilograms,
$D_n$ discharge/loss vector in kilograms, $H_p$ protected-harm vector, $R_s$
rights/distribution vector, $U$ uncertainty/calibration outcomes, $L$ latency in
seconds, and $J$ lifecycle energy in joules. Human work is reported separately
in person-minutes.

Uncertainty is partitioned into process, observation, model-form, parameter,
implementation, compliance, rights/authority, counterfactual, and structural-
change classes. Pooling these into one variance is prohibited when different
actions follow from them.

## Proposed AI translation

The bounded translation is a **renewable-capability ledger** for a system whose
modules, data, memory, verification capacity, or external resources can grow,
age, move, be consumed, recover, and be governed.

It would:

1. track capability stock separately from training, maintenance, decay,
   withdrawal, replacement, and measurement correction;
2. preserve lineage/cohort and location when they predict future performance
   or correlated failure;
3. assign action envelopes through typed authority rather than an unscoped
   global score;
4. test allocation and retirement rules in a closed loop against alternative
   dynamics and strategic agents;
5. carry intermediate output through verified end-use value and downstream
   externalities; and
6. abstain when state, rights, or counterfactual support is insufficient.

This is a proposed evaluation composition, not evidence that ecological models
will outperform conventional schedulers, constrained control, inventory
systems, or governance tooling.

## Efficiency mechanism

The possible efficiency gain is selective prevention of expensive irreversible
errors:

- avoid consuming rare or slow-replenishing capability that a scalar average
  labels redundant;
- spend monitoring on state distinctions that alter safe action;
- reduce repeated relearning after unrecorded cohort or regime loss;
- target interventions to spatial or causal sources instead of global resets;
- reject nominal circularity when material is merely displaced or accumulated;
- reserve enforcement and review for rights conflicts and high-impact
  uncertainty rather than every routine action; and
- stop optimizing intermediate mass or throughput that does not reach the
  protected endpoint.

The typed ledger also has storage, latency, instrumentation, privacy, governance,
and human-attention cost. If ordinary state estimation plus constrained control
reaches the same frontier more cheaply, the ecological composition loses.

## Mature null stack

Every proposed arm must face the complete applicable stack:

1. scalar and age/size/stage-structured population models;
2. state-space, hidden Markov, Gaussian-process, and ensemble estimators with
   calibrated uncertainty;
3. spatial population, metapopulation, network-flow, and transport models;
4. ordinary inventory, event sourcing, material-flow analysis, life-cycle
   assessment, and carbon-pool bookkeeping;
5. deterministic, stochastic, robust, distributionally robust, and model-
   predictive control;
6. standard harvest-control rules and full management-strategy evaluation;
7. constrained multiobjective optimization with protected outcome limits;
8. fixed quotas, non-transferable permits, transferable shares, territorial
   rights, co-management, and centralized control with matched enforcement;
9. typed IAM/capabilities, separation of duties, append-only lineage,
   randomized audit, sanctions, appeal, and expiry;
10. ordinary surveillance design, independent surveys, remote/electronic
    monitoring, traceability, and value-of-information scheduling;
11. production and ecological carrying-capacity models with hydrodynamic,
    disease, nutrient, and genetic-spread components;
12. feed-conversion, edible-yield, nutrient-retention, and life-cycle accounting;
13. static reserve/retention policies and diversity portfolios; and
14. an oracle-information upper bound that is not eligible for deployment.

The candidate receives no credit for reimplementing any null under biological
names.

  every generated event.
- **Randomness:** Philox or PCG64 with 64-bit seed; development seeds 1--32,
  confirmation seeds 10,001--10,064, and held-out seeds 90,001--90,064.
- **Splits:** operating-model families, spatial maps, observation operators,
  actor policies, and protected-outcome weights are split before tuning.
- **Episode:** 160 yearly steps for forest tracks; 80 yearly steps for capture
  fisheries; 730 daily steps for aquaculture; 104 weekly steps for food-chain
  and rights tracks.
- **Replicate unit:** independently generated world, not time step, cell, fish,
  tree, farm, vessel, or transaction.
- **Compute parity:** same observations, simulator calls, action opportunities,
  solver deadline, CPU/GPU class, memory cap, and random seeds per paired arm.
- **Decision parity:** identical legal action set and protected limits. Oracle
  arms expose the information ceiling but cannot satisfy a promotion gate.
- **Freeze:** generators, arms, hyperparameters, exclusions, primary contrasts,
  multiplicity procedure, and kill rules are hashed before confirmation worlds
  are generated.

### WS-FF-01 — fish stock, cohort, and observation

Generate 12 age classes on a $12\times12$ toroidal cell grid with two spawning
areas, larval dispersal, adult movement, and four fleets. Cross:

- Ricker, Beverton--Holt, regime-switching, and weakly identified recruitment;
- constant versus age-dependent reproductive value;
- stable versus climate-shifting survival;
- uniform versus localized depletion;
- fixed versus adapting gear selectivity; and
- complete recording versus size-dependent discard, 5--25% unreported removal,
  survey catchability drift, and missing spatial cells.

Arms are biomass-only control, age-structured assessment plus standard HCR,
spatial age-structured MSE, robust HCR over model ensemble, Candidate-014
observation contract plus the best conventional HCR, and an oracle-state HCR.

Primary metrics are probability that spawning stock crosses its prespecified
limit, median edible catch, fifth-percentile catch, age entropy, oldest-spawner
share, spatial occupancy, calibration of one- and five-year forecasts, action
churn, bytes, joules, and person-minutes.

**Track success:** the contract arm must reduce limit breaches by at least 25%
relative to the best deployable null while retaining at least 95% of its median
edible catch; the 95% paired interval must exclude zero for both comparisons.

**Track kill:** retire if the robust conventional spatial MSE matches the
frontier, if benefit disappears when reproductive value is constant, or if it
depends on latent age, removal, or spatial state unavailable to its observation
operator.

### WS-FF-02 — forest pools, cohorts, space, and disturbance

Generate 900 one-hectare cells over 160 years. Each cell has age/size structure,
species/functional type, live/dead/soil/product carbon pools, habitat score,
edge exposure, road distance, and fire/storm/drought/pest hazard. Cross:

- even- and uneven-aged initial landscapes;
- synchronous and asynchronous species responses;
- clustered and dispersed hazards;
- stable and shifting climate;
- three product-lifetime distributions; and
- substitution factors spanning negative, zero, and positive values with
  declared uncertainty.

At each five-year decision point, actions are no harvest, thinning, clear
harvest, aggregated retention, dispersed retention, assisted regeneration, or
monitor only. Arms are current-increment maximization, Faustmann-style expected
value, deterministic multiobjective planning, robust spatial planning, the
typed pool/cohort/spatial contract, and oracle future hazard.

Primary metrics are discounted timber service, live/dead/soil/product carbon
by pool, atmospheric flux under every counterfactual, habitat/connectivity
limits, old/large-tree distribution, post-disturbance recovery, road and
operation energy, observation cost, solver time, and human review.

**Track success:** across both sign directions of the substitution factor, the
contract arm must weakly dominate the best deployable null on timber service
and reduce worst-case protected-limit breaches by at least 20%, without hiding
pool transfer or spatial displacement.

**Track kill:** retire if benefit depends on one substitution value, one hazard
model, future disturbance labels, or a composite carbon score that cannot be
reconciled to pool mass balance.

### WS-FF-03 — rights, quotas, monitoring, and strategic response

Generate 24 persistent harvesters around one spatially mobile renewable stock.
Private types vary in catchability, monitoring cost, dependence, safety cost,
and ability to misreport. Cross:

- open access, fixed licences, non-transferable quota, transferable shares,
  territorial group rights, and central/co-managed control;
- perfect, costly random, and endogenous peer monitoring;
- 0--30% outsider poaching;
- entry, exit, identity reset, lease, and quota concentration;
- correlated enforcement failure; and
- destructive alternative gear after sanction.

The candidate composition uses Candidate 008 for strategic reporting and
Candidate 020 for typed, revocable, scoped authority. It competes with the best
fixed institutional arm, committed randomized audit, concentration caps,
ordinary IAM/ledger/enforcement, and an oracle compliance monitor.

Primary metrics are stock-limit breaches, legal and total removal, safety
events, report calibration, enforcement precision/recall, monitoring and
sanction cost, evasion displacement, quota/benefit concentration, entrant
access delay, unresolved appeals, bytes, joules, and person-minutes.

**Track success:** no governance arm may pass on biological performance alone.
The candidate must reduce breaches or unreported removal by at least 20% versus
the best matched null without worsening the preregistered concentration,
protected-dependence, or safety bound.

**Track kill:** retire if ordinary fixed rules plus independent randomized
audit match the frontier, if monitoring cost erases benefit, or if a result
requires treating transferable withdrawal rights as ownership of the resource
system.

### WS-FF-04 — aquaculture capacity and externalities

Generate a 30-farm coastal network on a calibrated synthetic current field with
daily temperature, salinity, oxygen, nutrient transport, pathogen/parasite
stages, wild-migration pulses, and farm inventories. Cross:

- fed finfish, bivalve, seaweed, and integrated configurations;
- three stocking densities and two harvest schedules;
- correlated disease introduction and treatment resistance;
- 0--2% containment failure with delayed reproduction of escapees;
- seasonally reversing currents; and
- production, ecological, and protected wild-population thresholds that bind
  in different worlds.

Arms are farm-local production control, fixed regional cap, hydrodynamic
capacity model, robust regional MPC, typed capacity/externality contract, and
oracle transport/disease state. Fallowing, treatment, harvest, input reduction,
and spatial closure have explicit action and recovery costs.

Primary metrics are edible harvest, mortality and welfare-relevant severe
states, disease prevalence, antimicrobial/chemical use, wild-host exposure,
escape descendants, dissolved and particulate N/P mass, oxygen-limit time,
extractive harvest recovery, energy, water treatment, sensor calls, and human
work.

**Track success:** the contract must improve edible harvest by at least 5% or
reduce full lifecycle cost by 10% relative to the best null while satisfying
all wild-population, discharge, disease, and severe-state gates in at least 90%
of held-out worlds.

**Track kill:** retire on unbalanced nutrient mass, nominal rather than
harvested extraction credit, hidden welfare mortality, absent wild-host
observation, or advantage from future current/pathogen information.

### WS-FF-05 — circularity and aquatic-food endpoint

Generate weekly lots from forage capture, trimmings, crop ingredients, feed,
farm/capture production, processing, cold storage, retail, and consumption.
Track wet mass, dry matter, protein, energy, EPA/DHA, calcium, iodine, iron,
selenium, zinc, vitamins A/D/B12, contaminant flags, N/P losses, greenhouse-gas
equivalent, energy, price, and actor. Cross:

- direct human consumption versus feed diversion;
- whole fish versus by-product inputs;
- product yield and edible fraction;
- spoilage and cold-chain failure;
- nutrition substitution by income group; and
- allocation rules for coproduct burdens.

Arms are live-mass maximization, feed-conversion minimization, monetary-value
optimization, ordinary multiobjective life-cycle/nutrition optimization, the
typed endpoint contract, and an oracle consumer-response model.

Primary metrics are edible nutrient delivered by nutrient, inadequacy avoided
under the registered substitution model, waste and loss by stage, stressors by
physical inventory and allocation sensitivity, affordability distribution,
bytes, joules, and person-minutes.

**Track success:** the contract must improve the preregistered lower-tail
nutrient-delivery vector or reduce a physical stressor by at least 10% versus
the best null without worsening protected affordability or safety limits.

**Track kill:** retire if benefit exists only under one coproduct allocation,
one consumer substitution assumption, gross biomass, or removal of a nutrient
that becomes limiting under held-out diets.

### WS-FF-06 — monitoring and intervention value

Reuse hidden worlds from the first five tracks. At each decision, the controller
can buy one of six observations, wait, intervene, or abstain. Observation cost,
latency, false positives, spatial support, and shared calibration dependencies
are explicit. Interventions can alter both latent state and future observation.

Arms are fixed monitoring, uncertainty sampling, myopic value of information,
POMDP/dual control, Candidate 007 plus Candidate 014, and oracle information.

Primary metrics are protected-limit loss, information gain conditional on
action, wasted follow-up, false reassurance, intervention regret, abstention
quality, sensor and analyst cost, latency, bytes, and joules.

**Track success:** the candidate composition must reduce task-weighted
protected loss plus full observation/intervention cost by at least 10% versus
the best deployable null on held-out operating-model families.

**Track kill:** retire if apparent information gain is evaluated against the
same model that selected the observation, if action changes observation without
being modeled, or if ordinary POMDP/dual control matches the frontier.

## Confirmatory analysis

Pair arms on world seed, initial state, shocks, observation draws, actor types,
and action opportunities. Use the independently generated world as the
sampling unit. Estimate paired effects with a hierarchical model or world-level
bootstrap; time steps and cells remain clustered. Report effect estimates and
95% intervals, not only threshold passes.

Preregister one primary contrast per track and control familywise error over
the six track-level success tests with Holm's method. Protected-limit failures,
severe tails, mass-balance residuals, and rights violations are gate outcomes
and are never averaged away. A result in a development family cannot promote a
claim unless its direction survives the confirmation family and at least one
held-out model class.

Mechanism checks ablate, one at a time:

- cohort/age state;
- spatial support and connectivity;
- alternative operating models;
- observation/removal operator;
- typed rights and enforcement cost;
- protected outcome vector;
- end-use nutrient accounting; and
- full lifecycle resource charging.

If removal of a component does not change the claimed advantage where that
component should matter, the mechanism interpretation fails even if aggregate
performance remains high.

## Full cost boundary

Charge to the arm that causes it:

- sensing, surveys, tags, remote monitoring, laboratory work, calibration, and
  missing-data recovery;
- compute, memory, storage, network, solver overrun, model development, and
  simulation generation;
- road, vessel, gear, feed, water treatment, pumping, aeration, cold storage,
  processing, and transport energy;
- habitat, bycatch, discard, mortality, pollution, escape, disease, genetic,
  carbon, and displacement losses under declared physical units;
- enforcement, audit, identity, quota transfer, litigation/appeal, meeting,
  accessibility, and compliance work;
- intervention, fallowing, closure, rollback, restoration, replanting,
  decommissioning, and recovery delay;
- reserve capacity, unused quota, retained habitat, abstention, and foregone
  output; and
- every operator, scientist, reviewer, regulator, and stakeholder minute.

Costs with unlike units remain a vector unless an authorized conversion rule
and sensitivity range are registered before confirmation.

## Global success and kill rules

Retain the evaluation contract only if:

1. at least two tracks pass their confirmatory success rule;
2. one passing track is biological/resource and one is institutional or
   end-use, preventing one generator from carrying the whole result;
3. no protected-limit, conservation, authority, or leakage gate fails;
4. advantage survives at least one unseen operating-model family and one unseen
   observation or strategic-response family;
5. conventional nulls receive the same information, action, and human budget;
6. at least one typed component is causally necessary in its registered
   mechanism ablation; and
7. lifecycle cost does not reverse the direction.

Reject or narrow the contract if:

- scalar stock or ordinary robust control reaches the same frontier;
- stock improvement is produced by cohort removal, spatial export, unobserved
  mortality, accounting correction, or a changed denominator;
- harvest is called sustainable without future recruitment and protected-state
  evidence;
- a forest carbon result depends on an undeclared product, substitution,
  leakage, or time-horizon assumption;
- a property regime is credited without enforcement, concentration,
  displacement, and distribution outcomes;
- production, ecological, and social capacity are collapsed;
- an aquaculture loop credits standing extractive biomass or ignores off-site
  disease, escape, or nutrient flow;
- food benefit is measured at landed/live mass rather than edible endpoint;
- alternative-model debt is represented as a precise Monte Carlo probability;
- human authority is simulated or inferred from optimization; or
- the claimed gain requires extra state, future labels, or uncharged work.

## EU and German normative context

This section is a dated research boundary, not legal advice or a conformity
assessment. Applicability depends on the concrete actor, species, water, land,
product, intervention, and competent authority. Scientific limits and legal
authorization remain different records.

### Forests and land

- Germany's **Bundeswaldgesetz**, section 1, protects the economic,
  environmental, climate, water, soil, landscape, infrastructure, and
  recreation functions of forests and requires sustainable proper management
  ([official live text](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bwaldg/BJNR010370975.html)).
  A timber-only objective does not represent that statutory plurality.
- Germany's **Bundesnaturschutzgesetz**, section 5(3), states the objective of
  building near-natural forests and managing them sustainably without
  clear-cutting, with a sufficient share of site-native forest plants; section
  5(4) also addresses fishery use, native habitats, stocking, and inland fish
  culture
  ([official section](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bnatschg_2009/__5.html)).
- **Regulation (EU) 2024/1991** requires Member States to pursue forest-
  ecosystem restoration trends, including the common forest bird index and at
  least six of seven indicators such as deadwood, uneven-aged structure,
  connectivity, organic carbon, native dominance, and tree diversity
  ([EUR-Lex ELI](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1991/oj/eng)). The
  indicators are national obligations with specified exceptions; they are not
  automatic stand-level reward weights.
- The consolidated **LULUCF Regulation (EU) 2018/841**, as amended by Regulation
  (EU) 2023/839, defines accounting for emissions, removals, forest land, carbon
  pools, and harvested wood products and sets 2026--2030 Member-State targets
  ([EUR-Lex consolidated text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2018/841/2023-05-11/eng)).
  Regulatory accounting categories do not by themselves resolve a project-
  level causal counterfactual.

### Fisheries and aquatic products

- **Regulation (EU) 1380/2013**, the Common Fisheries Policy, combines
  environmental, economic, and social objectives, a precautionary and
  ecosystem-based approach, maximum-sustainable-yield language, the landing
  obligation, fishing opportunities, relative stability, and transparent,
  objective allocation criteria
  ([EUR-Lex legal act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=CELEX:32013R1380)).
  MSY is one policy reference within this system, not a license to omit
  multispecies, ecosystem, observation, or distribution outcomes.
- **Regulation (EU) 2023/2842** amends the Union fisheries-control system with
  vessel tracking, electronic recording, risk-based remote electronic
  monitoring, catch/landing control, traceability, inspections, and data-
  protection constraints
  ([EUR-Lex ELI](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/2842/oj)). Some duties
  have staged application dates, so any deployment must recheck the provision
  and date rather than label the whole act “active.”
- Germany's **Seefischereigesetz** implements and enforces Union fisheries law
  through licensing, monitoring, inspection, and sanctions; the official live
  page records a 2026 amendment whose documentary processing was not yet final
  on the audit date
  ([official live text](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/seefischg/BJNR008760984.html)).
- **Regulation (EU) 2016/429**, the Animal Health Law, covers registration,
  approval, traceability, movement, surveillance, mortality, treatments, tests,
  and biosecurity for aquaculture establishments where applicable
  ([EUR-Lex ELI](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/429/oj)).
- The Commission's **COM(2021) 236** strategic guidelines for EU aquaculture
  address resilience, health, climate, space/water access, environmental
  performance, food supply, and socioeconomic objectives. It is policy
  guidance, not a directly applicable production threshold
  ([EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=COM:2021:236:FIN)).

Legal minimum, scientific evidence, policy target, private certification, and
project experiment gate must each keep its own authority and version.

## Deduplication and routing

### Existing principles

- Scarce harvest and monitoring allocation maps to **P-001**.
- Local action with exception escalation maps to **P-002**.
- Temporary survey and trial evidence before slow commitment maps to **P-003**.
- Diversity selection and protection maps to **P-004**, but biodiversity does
  not create a new principle here.
- Slow feedback and harvest control map to **P-006**.
- Value-directed monitoring maps to **P-007**.
- Spatial refugia and farm/stand compartments map to **P-008**.
- maintenance, reserve, restoration, and delayed liability map to **P-009**.
- age/cohort and product-lifetime matching map to **P-012**.
- trails, ledgers, quotas, and external work records map to **P-013**.

### Existing candidates

- **Candidate 003:** disturbance and recovery signals; fisheries/forest fixtures
  add abrupt, hidden-mode, cohort, spatial, and intervention alternatives.
- **Candidate 007:** harvest, treatment, closure, and monitoring change the
  state and future observation channel.
- **Candidate 008:** quota reports, monitoring, strategic actors, and entrant
  opportunity fit its applicability gate.
- **Candidate 011:** resource incidents require containment plus verified
  management-rule learning and retirement.
- **Candidate 013:** deficit and capacity messages already cover resource
  routing; conservation and protected outcome gates are the added null.
- **Candidate 014:** stock, cohort, support, selectivity, reporting, intervention,
  and counterfactual versions are direct observation-contract fields.
- **Candidate 018:** product and carbon pools stress value/reconstructability
  tiering, but material conservation remains an independent accounting gate.
- **Candidate 020:** access, withdrawal, management, exclusion, transfer,
  protected standing, and spillovers satisfy its institutional applicability
  gate.

No non-duplicative invariant survives normalization. The narrow durable residue
is the evaluation fixture and typed state contract. No candidate file is edited.

## Conservative verdict

Forestry, fisheries, aquaculture, and aquatic food systems add substantial
discipline but no independent architecture principle. Their shared contribution
is adversarial to scalar optimization: current output can rise while renewal,
structure, space, protected state, enforcement capacity, or end-use value falls.

Retain the typed stock--flow--cohort--space--rights contract and `FFAFS-SYN-1`
as cross-candidate falsification work. Do not create another candidate unless
the composed existing candidates first beat structured population, spatial
planning, management-strategy evaluation, constrained control, ordinary
governance, material-flow, and life-cycle nulls with complete cost and authority
accounting. The residual OECD catch-all remains open after this audit.
