## Executive finding

Nature does not supply one “parasite algorithm.” The useful residue is a set of adversarial evaluation obligations:

1. **Surface observations and project identity must be separated in the artificial test.** *Trypanosoma brucei* populations can express many antigenically distinct surface coats. Treating an unchanged artificial lineage or function as latent beneath such rotations is a project synthesis used to construct a stress test, not a biological result about retained function.
2. **Lifecycle transitions have opportunity costs.** Malaria parasites causally gate a switch from continued within-host replication to a non-replicating transmission lineage. The AI analogue is not generic routing; it is a stateful, sometimes irreversible commitment whose future option value and current lost service must both be measured.
3. **The host can become part of the attack path.** A baculovirus gene can causally alter a host behavior that supports transmission. For AI systems, an adversary may alter sampling, telemetry, evaluator behavior, routing, or action authority without changing the focal model's weights.
4. **Removing the measured threat can release another threat.** In mixed rodent-malaria infections, chemotherapy removed susceptible competitors and released resistant parasites. Target success is therefore not system success; intervention evaluation needs the full interacting population and downstream transmission or harm.
5. **Adaptive stories need intervention and specificity tests.** *Toxoplasma gondii* changes rodent behavior, but experiments disagree on whether the effect is a felid-specific manipulation or a broader inflammation-associated loss of predator aversion. Benefit-shaped narrative is not causal mechanism.

All five findings already have mature neighbors in security, causal inference, ecology, hierarchical control, adversarial training, and operations research. No new project principle survives deduplication. The durable addition is the **joint test surface**: latent identity, lifecycle state, observation/control authority, opponent ecology, and total cost must remain visible in the same evaluation.

## Evidence synthesis

### 1. Antigenic surfaces: identity is not appearance

*Trypanosoma brucei* lives extracellularly in its mammalian host and changes its dense variant surface glycoprotein (VSG) coat. Mugnier, Cross, and Papavasiliou used VSG-seq to reconstruct expressed VSG populations during mouse infections. Their measurements found many rare variants alongside the dominant variants and evidence that recombination generated mosaic VSGs. In the first 30 days, each sampled population contained substantial variant diversity rather than one orderly dominant sequence. The result is a population-level observation with calibrated assay limits, not proof that every rare variant caused persistence ([doi:10.1126/science.aaa4502](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaa4502)).

The following latent-lineage construction is a **project synthesis** for the artificial test; the source establishes changing expressed surfaces, not an unchanged software function beneath them:

$$
o_t = g_{e_t}(z_t,\eta_t),
$$

where $z_t$ is latent lineage or function, $e_t$ is the current surface-expression epoch, $g_e$ is the epoch-specific observation operator, $\eta_t$ is noise, and $o_t$ is the measured surface. A detector trained only on $o$ may learn $e$ rather than $z$.

For epoch set $\mathcal E_{\mathrm{test}}$, report both mean risk and worst-epoch risk,

$$
R_{\mathrm{worst}}
=
\max_{e\in\mathcal E_{\mathrm{test}}}
\mathbb E\!\left[\ell\!\left(f(o),z\right)\mid e\right].
$$

Every symbol is defined above; $f$ is the detector and $\ell$ its declared loss. The equation does not assume that perfect invariant recovery is possible. If $g_e$ destroys identifying information, abstention or active challenge may be the only valid response.

**AI translation.** Treat surface rotation as an attacker/test generator, not as a mystical model architecture. Version visible signatures independently from latent behavior; retain lineage provenance; use active challenges that elicit harder-to-change behavior; and test unseen compositions of surface transforms.

**Mandatory nulls.** Domain randomization, data augmentation, group distributionally robust optimization, explicit canonicalization, adversarial training, retrieval over versioned signatures, moving-target defence, and abstention.

**Failure boundary.** Adding a larger signature bank can increase memory, update, and query energy while remaining vulnerable to new compositions. Diversity in the attacker does not imply diversity is the cheapest defender.

### 2. Stage commitment: preserve the cost of becoming something else

In *P. falciparum*, continued asexual blood-stage replication and differentiation into non-replicating sexual gametocytes are alternative lineages with different immediate and future functions. Kafsack et al. identified AP2-G as an essential causal regulator of sexual commitment through gene disruption and conditional stabilization in their tested system ([doi:10.1038/nature12920](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature12920)). This supports causal necessity and regulatory control without claiming that AP2-G alone is sufficient in every context. Sinha et al. independently identified the AP2-G role and a downstream regulatory cascade in *P. berghei* ([doi:10.1038/nature12970](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature12970)). Later single-cell and temporal work resolved commitment heterogeneity and more than one route into early sexual development rather than one universally timed switch ([doi:10.1038/nature24280](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature24280); [doi:10.1038/s41564-018-0291-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41564-018-0291-7)).

The AI analogue must retain that opportunity-cost structure. Let

$$
s_t=(x_t,q_t),\qquad q_t\in\{\text{exploit},\text{committed},\text{transfer}\},
$$

where $x_t$ is task/environment state and $q_t$ is lifecycle stage. Let $a_t$ be the action, $E_t$ measured energy, and $T(q_{t+1}\mid q_t,x_t,a_t)$ the stage-transition kernel. A prospective policy may optimize

$$
J(\pi)=
\mathbb E_{\pi,T}\!\left[
\sum_{t=0}^{H} \gamma^t
\left(r_t-\lambda E_t\right)
\right],
$$

where $H$ is the declared horizon, $\gamma$ the discount factor, $r_t$ reward or accepted-service value, and $\lambda$ the declared energy exchange rate. Commitment is meaningful only if $T$ constrains later choices or imposes switching, latency, capacity, or service cost.

**AI translation.** Test whether a small dynamically selected reserve can enter a future-facing mode that is deliberately poor at immediate throughput but useful after regime change. This may represent protected retraining capacity, a recovery lineage, a slow verifier, or an exploration branch.

**Mandatory nulls.** Flat POMDP policy, options/hierarchical reinforcement learning, mixture-of-experts routing, ensemble hedging, checkpoint restore, fixed reserve, periodic retraining, and oracle regime label.

**Failure boundary.** A false commitment signal can destroy immediate service; commitment may duplicate ordinary options; and a reserve that is never used is pure lifecycle burden. Biological commitment rates are not software hyperparameters.

### 3. Host manipulation: the observation and action boundary is attackable

Hoover et al. tested a gypsy-moth baculovirus in which infected larvae climb before death. Deleting the viral ecdysteroid UDP-glucosyltransferase gene, `egt`, prevented the elevated climbing behavior in the tested system, providing a causal gene-to-host-behavior intervention rather than correlation alone ([doi:10.1126/science.1209199](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1209199)). The result supports one extended phenotype; it does not establish universal precise behavioral control.

The adjacent *T. gondii* literature is a useful warning against easy adaptive stories. Rat experiments reported changes specific to cat odor and corresponding limbic activity ([doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0023277](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0023277)). A broader mouse experiment found reduced general anxiety and predator aversion without felid specificity, correlated with cyst burden and neuroinflammation ([doi:10.1016/j.celrep.2019.12.019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2019.12.019)). Neither result alone establishes the evolutionary effect on realized transmission.

For an AI system, let true environment state be $x_t$, model observation be

$$
o_t=h(x_t,a^{\mathrm{adv}}_t),
$$

system action be $u_t=\pi(o_t)$, and audit record be

$$
y_t=m(o_t,u_t,a^{\mathrm{adv}}_t).
$$

Here $a^{\mathrm{adv}}_t$ is adversarial influence, $h$ is the sensing operator, $\pi$ the focal policy, and $m$ the telemetry/recording operator. If the same influence can change both $h$ and $m$, agreement between policy and log is not independent evidence.

**AI translation.** Model compromise of sample selection, retrieval context, reward/evaluator state, router policy, tool authority, telemetry, and stopping rules. Preserve an independent witness or cryptographically rooted provenance where feasible.

**Mandatory nulls.** Access control, attestation, signed provenance, redundant independent sensors, canary transactions, causal logging, runtime assurance, control-flow integrity, and human challenge channels.

**Failure boundary.** “Parasite-like control” is an attack model, not a recommendation to make models manipulate operators. An independent monitor is not independent if it shares the same data, authority, model, or deployment path.

### 4. Competitive release: intervention changes the population

De Roode et al. coinfected mice with drug-sensitive and drug-resistant *P. chabaudi*. Without drug, the sensitive clone competitively suppressed the resistant clone; treatment removed that suppression and restored resistant-clone transmission ([doi:10.1186/1475-2875-3-33](https://doi.org/10.1186/1475-2875-3-33)). Wargo et al. later found that curative chemotherapy could release resistant parasites beyond their performance when no competitor had been present and increase transmission-stage densities ([doi:10.1073/pnas.0707766104](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0707766104)). These are direct rodent-model results, not effect estimates for human malaria.

A minimal synthetic stress model is

$$
n_{i,t+1}
=
n_{i,t}\exp\!\left(
r_i-\sum_j a_{ij}n_{j,t}-d_i(u_t)
\right),
$$

where $n_{i,t}$ is population or threat-family $i$, $r_i$ its intrinsic log-growth term, $a_{ij}$ the effect of population $j$ on $i$, $u_t$ the intervention, and $d_i(u_t)$ direct suppression. Competitive release is present for non-target $k$ when an intervention that increases suppression of target $i$ produces

$$
\frac{\partial n_{k,t+\tau}}{\partial u_{i,t}}>0
$$

for a declared horizon $\tau$, after separating direct effects from competitor removal. This model is a test generator, not a biological law.

**AI translation.** When a filter, router, moderation rule, firewall, expert ablation, or retraining intervention suppresses one failure family, track all other families, shared capacity, false positives, adaptation, and downstream harm. “Target attack rate fell” is not sufficient.

**Mandatory nulls.** Multi-objective robust optimization, game-theoretic best response, ecological competition models, model-predictive control, red-team population training, threat-budget reallocation, and ordinary post-intervention monitoring.

**Failure boundary.** Apparent release can be measurement substitution: a label, detector, or sampling process changes while latent harm does not. The test therefore needs stable outcome operators and explicit “unknown/other” mass.

### 5. Time-shift antagonism: already adopted, still mandatory

Decaestecker et al. resurrected *Daphnia* host clones and *Pasteuria* parasite isolates from dated pond-sediment layers and performed past/contemporary/future cross-infections. In the temporally pooled experiment, contemporary parasites were more infective than past and future isolates; the authors interpreted this as rapid parasite tracking of changing host genotypes, while individual-depth contrasts varied ([doi:10.1038/nature06291](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature06291)).

This result is already handled in the [animal, veterinary, and population-health audit](2026-08-21-animal-veterinary-population-health.md#host--pathogen-coevolution-and-changing-opponents), which requires a full cross-play matrix rather than only contemporary scores. It is retained here because antigenic variation, commitment, host capture, and competitive release must also be tested against archived opponents and interventions. It does not justify a new principle.

## Convergence and deduplication

The same problem structures already appear under different disciplinary names. The right move is to bundle them, then retain only the extra test boundary.

| Parasitology manifestation | Convergent field mechanisms | Mature AI/systems analogue | Residue retained here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| VSG surface switching and mosaic expression | immune escape, evolutionary diversification, ecology of rare variants | domain randomization, adversarial examples, moving-target defence, signature rotation | separate latent lineage/function from visible surface epoch and test unseen compositions |
| asexual-to-sexual commitment | developmental commitment, dormancy/dispersal, phenotypic switching | options, hierarchical POMDPs, mixture routing, reserved recovery capacity | measure irreversible/path-dependent transition, opportunity cost, future transfer, and unused reserve together |
| viral gene alters host climbing | extended phenotype, behavioral ecology, cyber-physical attack | reward hacking, sensor spoofing, data poisoning, control-plane compromise | perturb observation, evaluator, router, telemetry, and authority independently of focal weights |
| chemotherapy releases resistant clone | ecological competitive release, microbial resistance, cancer evolution, market substitution | multi-agent games, threat displacement, robust control, portfolio risk | distinguish direct target suppression from population-mediated rebound and total harm |
| host--parasite time-shift cross-play | Red Queen dynamics, continual learning, self-play | adversarial training, population-based training, exploit archives | require past/contemporary/future opponent matrix and detect cycling or forgetting |
| *Toxoplasma* “fatal attraction” dispute | causal inference, neuroinflammation, comparative ethology | interpretability stories, post-hoc mechanism labels | benefit-consistent behavior is not proof of targeted mechanism or adaptive function |

### Adoption decision

- **No new principle.** P-003, P-004, P-007, P-008, P-009, P-012, and P-013 already contain the general mechanisms.
- **No new candidate.** Existing security, endogenous-observation, lifecycle, population, and recovery candidates provide the correct experimental homes.
- **Retain four tests.** The joint ledger and perturbation designs below are not present as one parasitology-derived evaluation package.
- **Reject literal biomimicry.** Copying VSG switching, irreversible commitment, host manipulation, or competitive ecology without the stated system objective could increase evasion, fragility, harm, and energy.

## Prospective test package

These are complete written protocols, not implementations or results. All outcomes remain `NO_RESULT` until a frozen implementation, environment receipt, and raw artifacts exist.

### `PAR-X01` — Surface rotation without latent change

**Question.** Can a detector preserve lineage/function recognition when visible signatures rotate or recombine, without paying more total cost than mature robustness baselines?

**Generator.** Create latent entities $z\in\{1,\dots,K\}$ with fixed behavior kernels. Render observations through versioned surface operators $g_e$ that change vocabulary, serialization, feature order, superficial graph labels, and pairwise compositions while preserving the latent kernel. Training receives epochs $E_0$; development receives disjoint operators $E_1$; the sealed test contains unseen single operators and unseen compositions $E_2$. Include a non-identifiable linking arm in which the `same` and `different` task labels have equal declared priors and induce exactly the same permitted joint surface and challenge-response distributions.

**Systems.** Compare plain empirical-risk minimization; augmentation/domain randomization; group DRO; explicit canonicalization; a prior-only Bayes decision rule; active behavioral challenge; lineage-provenance retrieval; and the proposed joint surface/lineage ledger. Match model capacity, training examples, optimizer attempts, challenge budget, inference latency cap, and total measured energy.

**Endpoints.** Mean and worst-epoch balanced error; calibration error; full risk--coverage curves and selective risk at frozen coverage points; lineage-link precision/recall; false merge and false split rates; detection latency; bytes stored/transferred; wall time; and joules per accepted decision. In the non-identifiable arm, report the prior-only Bayes risk explicitly; the equal-prior control cannot support above-chance linking, and any safer claim must be carried by charged selective abstention rather than fabricated evidence.

**Prediction.** Surface-only classifiers will fail disproportionately on unseen compositions. Active challenge or provenance should reduce worst-epoch false merges when the latent kernels are behaviorally distinguishable, but no method should beat the prior-only Bayes rule where permitted observations remove the distinction; in the equal-prior control, accepted link decisions cannot beat chance, so reduced selective risk must trade away coverage through abstention.

**Falsification.** Reject the added ledger if a complete augmentation/canonicalization baseline matches worst-epoch and abstention performance at lower lifecycle cost, or if gains vanish under sealed operators.

### `PAR-X02` — Costed lifecycle commitment under a rare regime change

**Question.** Does stateful commitment of bounded capacity to a future-facing mode outperform ordinary options, ensembles, and fixed reserves?

**Generator.** Use a partially observed environment with a long exploitation phase and a rare transfer window. Capacity committed before the window loses current throughput and cannot return for a declared minimum duration. Signals have calibrated false-positive and false-negative rates. Sweep hazard rate, signal quality, transition delay, committed capacity, horizon, and energy price; seal one combined out-of-range regime.

**Systems.** Compare flat recurrent POMDP policy; learned options; mixture-of-experts gate; static reserve; periodic reserve; checkpoint/restart; oracle-change upper bound; and stateful commitment policy. Equalize total parameters, active capacity, training updates, environment interactions, wall-time limit, and measured energy.

**Endpoints.** Accepted service before change; probability of successful transfer/recovery; detection-to-readiness latency; cumulative lost throughput; false-commitment rate; stranded reserve; switching count; worst-seed outcome; and full training/inference/idle/recovery joules.

**Prediction.** Stateful commitment can win only in an intermediate region: regime changes are rare but not negligible, preparation delay matters, and signals carry enough information. It should lose when change is absent, instantly recoverable, or perfectly announced.

**Falsification.** Reject the mechanism if an option policy or fixed reserve dominates its service--recovery--energy Pareto frontier, or if the sealed hazard/signal sweep reverses the claimed region.

### `PAR-X03` — Host/control-plane capture

**Question.** Which assurance stack detects an adversary that changes observation, evaluation, routing, or logging without changing focal model weights?

**Generator.** Freeze a deterministic focal model. Independently inject: sample-selection bias; retrieval-context substitution; reward/evaluator perturbation; router redirection; tool-authority expansion; telemetry suppression; and coordinated observation-plus-log compromise. Include benign shifts with the same marginal output statistics and a separate witness-root-compromise stratum. Predeclare attack capability and cryptographic trust roots; root-compromise cells are outside the witness's detection claim and must be handled as uncovered support requiring fail-closed abstention, quarantine, or escalation.

**Systems.** Compare output anomaly monitor; duplicated monitor sharing the same input path; independent sensor/receipt; signed provenance; canary actions; runtime authority policy; and combined independent-witness stack. Match monitor compute, storage, latency, and operator-review budget.

**Endpoints.** Attack detection by class; false-alarm rate on benign shifts; time to containment; unauthorized effects completed before containment; provenance gaps; independence violations; service loss; bytes; operator minutes; and joules. Report attacks that fool both focal output and shared telemetry separately.

**Prediction.** Output-only and shared-path monitors will miss coordinated boundary capture even when focal weights are unchanged. Independently rooted receipts should improve detection for attacks inside their trust boundary. Compromised-root or unobserved-effect cells cannot support an advantage claim: success there means declaring the cell uncovered and abstaining, quarantining, or escalating before the protected harm limit, with all service and fallback cost charged.

**Falsification.** Reject any “independent” monitor whose in-boundary advantage disappears after shared dependencies are exposed, whose false alarms/service cost erase the containment benefit, or that counts a compromised-root cell as covered instead of invoking the preregistered fail-closed path.

### `PAR-X04` — Intervention-induced competitive release

**Question.** Can an intervention lower its target metric while increasing total system harm through competitor release or threat substitution?

**Generator.** Simulate three interacting threat families with known competition matrix $A=(a_{ij})$, resource ceiling, mutation/substitution channel, and observation operator. Apply an intervention with high direct efficacy against family 1 and weaker effects on families 2--3. Cross factorially: no competition; resource competition; detector-mediated apparent competition; direct interference; no latent release but label substitution. Seal matrices with a sign pattern unseen in development.

**Systems.** Compare target-only optimizer; summed-risk optimizer; minimax policy; model-predictive ecological controller; game-theoretic best-response policy; and an oracle with the true matrix. Equalize intervention opportunity, sensing, model updates, latency, and total energy.

**Endpoints.** Target-family burden; each non-target burden; unknown mass; total harm; tail harm; time to rebound; intervention volume; false positives; service loss; adaptation count; and lifecycle energy. Decompose direct suppression, mediated release, and observation substitution where the generator makes them identifiable.

**Prediction.** A target-only controller will appear best on family 1 while producing worse total harm in some competitive regimes. Population-aware controllers should reduce that failure only when interaction estimates are identifiable and stable enough to repay added sensing/control cost.

**Falsification.** Reject the ecological controller if robust target-plus-unknown monitoring or ordinary minimax control matches total harm at lower cost, or if apparent release is explained by the label-substitution arm.

## Shared artifact and reporting contract

Every implemented `PAR-X` test must preserve:

1. generator version, seed, latent lineages, visible surface epochs, lifecycle states, interaction matrix, intervention, and observation operator;
2. a sealed-test receipt before test execution;
3. exact training, inference, monitor, storage, communication, operator, recovery, and idle budgets;
4. all baseline hyperparameter searches under the same search budget;
5. per-epoch, per-stage, per-threat, and per-seed outcomes, not only aggregate means;
6. non-identifiable and benign-shift controls;
7. calibration intervals or bootstrap intervals with the resampling unit named;
8. failure and abstention counts, including unknown mass;
9. hardware, software, measurement interval, energy-meter boundary, and uncertainty; and
10. a `NO_RESULT`, `NEGATIVE`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, or `SUPPORTED` disposition that cannot be inferred from prose alone.

## Failure modes and negative lessons

| Failure mode | Why the biological analogy tempts it | Required guard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| optimize evasion rather than service | parasite fitness rewards escaping a host defence | state the system beneficiary and protected outcomes before transfer |
| equate changing appearance with new competence | antigenic variants look different to the host | retain latent lineage, behavior challenge, and false-split metrics |
| copy biological frequencies | a study reports proportions or switching rates | tune only inside a declared software environment; biological numbers are not priors without a transfer model |
| call any router “commitment” | both select a path | require path dependence, future constraint, and measured opportunity cost |
| infer adaptive manipulation from usefulness | changed behavior could aid transmission | require intervention, specificity, realized transmission, and alternative host/pathology explanations |
| trust the compromised observer | host/control effects can change evidence generation | use independent roots, explicit trust boundaries, and physical/effect receipts |
| celebrate target suppression | treated target falls | report all interacting populations, unknown mass, total harm, and rebound |
| mistake cycling for improvement | Red Queen trajectories remain dynamic | archive opponents and report the full time-shift cross-play matrix |
| ignore biological cost | evolved persistence sounds efficient | measure diversity, switching, surveillance, storage, and recovery cost end to end |
| generalize across taxa | “parasites do X” compresses diverse life histories | bind every result to organism, host, stage, assay, and environment |

## What remains outside this audit

This entry does not cover helminth immunomodulation, vector ecology, parasitoid development, marine parasites, social parasitism, brood parasitism, parasite genomics, zoonotic spillover, drug discovery, diagnostics, clinical treatment, veterinary control, crop parasites, parasitic plants, bacteriophage biology, gene drives, or broad virus evolution. It also does not resolve the neighboring virology gap.

High-value next passes would test whether parasite-induced immunomodulation adds a distinct context-authority mechanism after the immune audit; whether vector lifecycle bottlenecks change active-sensing/routing tests; and whether multi-host, multi-stage transmission produces an experiment not already captured by hierarchical POMDPs and supply-chain flow models.

## Audit disposition

Parasitology contributes no magic architecture and no energy claim. It contributes a stricter adversarial experiment boundary: visible identity can rotate; state can commit; the environment and evaluator can be captured; an intervention can release a competitor; and contemporaneous success can hide cycling. Those effects become useful to this project only when the corresponding conventional baselines, independent evidence paths, protected outcomes, and full lifecycle costs are tested together.
