## Executive finding

Animal and veterinary science do not support a new architecture by analogy.
They do expose a recurring experimental error that survives several existing
principles:

> A population intervention can improve a herd mean by reallocating resources,
> changing who remains observable, changing who reproduces, or changing which
> opponent can transmit. Credit requires typed individual, cohort, lineage,
> welfare, observation, and opponent ledgers through the delayed horizon.

The durable residue is therefore an **evaluation contract**, not a biological
module:

1. keep intake, reserves, maintenance, immune work, reproduction, output, loss,
   and recovery separately measurable across fast and slow horizons;
2. preserve the target population, sampling frame, inclusion mechanism, pool,
   diagnostic version, reporting path, and intervention history for every
   surveillance estimate;
3. treat welfare as a protected multidimensional outcome comprising health,
   injury, behavior, affect-relevant animal-based measures, preference or
   motivation where validated, severe-event tails, and attrition; productivity
   is never its proxy;
4. report genetic gain beside coancestry, realized inbreeding, effective
   diversity, rare-function retention, reproductive concentration, and
   descendant performance;
5. keep microbial composition, absolute load, substrate flow, fermentation
   products, host uptake, methane, and host outcomes distinct; and
6. assess interventions under interference, selective treatment/removal, and
   time-shifted opponents rather than only contemporaneous aggregate scores.

Every component has a mature null: multiscale state-space control, ordinary
risk-based surveillance, causal diagrams and interference estimands, optimum
contribution selection, quantitative microbial profiling and flux models, and
population/adversarial training with archived cross-play. The project earns
residue only if a composed existing candidate beats those nulls at equal total
work while the protected ledgers remain visible.

| Disposition | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **Promote** | none |
| **Experiment** | one coupled population-intervention benchmark with resource, surveillance, welfare, breeding, consortium, and opponent tracks |
| **Adopt as evaluation contract** | typed cohort flow, protected multidimensional welfare, contribution/coancestry, absolute consortium state and flux, intervention support, and time-shift cross-play |
| **Reject as universal** | productivity implies welfare; targeted surveillance estimates prevalence without weights; herd recovery can be credited after selective removal; a taxonomic shift identifies a function; genomic prediction preserves diversity; vaccination or treatment has only direct effects; current opponents measure coevolutionary robustness |

## Terms that must remain distinct

| Term | Operational meaning | Must not be inferred from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| homeostasis | regulation around a physiological variable or viable range on a declared time scale | every coordinated long-horizon allocation change |
| homeorhesis | coordinated change in tissue metabolism and nutrient partitioning that supports a physiological trajectory such as pregnancy or lactation | a universal optimizer, fixed set point, or proof of beneficial allocation |
| intake / accessible resource | material entering, or available to enter, the organism or subsystem | delivered service, internal reserve, or allocation to one function |
| reserve / mobilization | stored resource and its release over time | free capacity; mobilization can incur later recovery and health costs |
| maintenance / immune work / production / reproduction | different sinks with different units, horizons, and consequences | a single fungible reward unless a conversion model is registered |
| health | disease, injury, function, and physiological state under a declared definition | productivity, absence of a report, or survival alone |
| welfare | multidimensional state assessed through fit-for-purpose animal-based and contextual measures | milk yield, weight gain, feed efficiency, health alone, or a single sensor |
| preference / motivation | behavior under a particular choice, cost, deprivation, and test design | total welfare, moral authority, or unrestricted long-term choice |
| target population | animals, holdings, food-chain stages, people, or environments to which an inference refers | the units easiest to sample |
| sampling frame / sentinel | units eligible for selection, and a selected unit/channel intended to detect a declared event | a representative prevalence sample without known inclusion probabilities |
| pool | combined material whose test constrains the group | identification of which member is positive or each member's state |
| case / detection / report / response | successively defined diagnostic, observation, communication, and action events | one another; a detected signal is not a confirmed case or completed response |
| resistance | prevention or reduction of pathogen burden | tolerance, low symptoms, or reduced onward transmission |
| tolerance | reduced harm at a given pathogen burden | reduced pathogen burden or reduced infectiousness |
| direct / indirect intervention effect | effect through own assignment, and effect through others' assignments or changed exposure | a marginal before/after difference under interference |
| breeding value | predicted transmissible genetic merit for a stated objective and population | realized descendant performance, welfare, or diversity |
| coancestry / inbreeding | expected shared ancestry between parents / homologous ancestry within an individual under a stated estimator | census size or raw number of models |
| effective diversity | diversity after reproductive or exposure concentration is accounted for | headcount, parameter count, or number of checkpoints |
| microbial composition / absolute load | within-sample proportions / organism or gene amount per physical sample unit | each other, metabolic activity, or delivered flux |
| function / flux / host uptake | encoded or measured capacity / rate of transformation / material delivered to host | taxon label, concentration alone, or a contemporaneous association |
| coadaptation / coevolution | context-specific fit / reciprocal heritable change through time | current host--pathogen association or adaptation in only one party |

## Evidence synthesis

### Multi-scale homeostasis and resource allocation

Bauman and Currie's landmark review separated rapid maintenance of physiological
conditions from coordinated metabolic trajectories that support pregnancy and
lactation. The transferable content is not the word *homeorhesis*. It is the
requirement that an evaluation distinguish physiological horizon, tissue or
subsystem, source, sink, reserve, and delayed recovery
([doi:10.3168/jds.S0022-0302(80)83111-0](https://doi.org/10.3168/jds.S0022-0302(80)83111-0)).
Later animal-science syntheses make genotype, physiological trajectory, body
reserve, immune work, production, and robustness explicit, but remain models
and reviews rather than proof of one allocation law
([doi:10.1017/S1751731111001820](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1751731111001820),
[doi:10.3389/fgene.2012.00267](https://doi.org/10.3389/fgene.2012.00267)).

A small controlled feed-restriction study illustrates why output alone is
insufficient. Ten multiparous Holstein cows underwent a 14-day baseline; five
continued ad libitum feeding and five received 60% of their earlier intake for
four days before refeeding. Milk output, serum non-esterified fatty acids, and
mammary lipogenic expression changed on different time courses. The design is
valuable because intake, circulating mobilization, output, and recovery were
observed separately; its small sample and short perturbation bar a universal
claim ([doi:10.3168/jds.2016-11130](https://doi.org/10.3168/jds.2016-11130)).

For resource type $r$, individual $i$, and interval $t$, the minimal ledger is

$$
R_{i,r,t+1}=R_{i,r,t}+I_{i,r,t}+M^{+}_{i,r,t}
-M^{-}_{i,r,t}-U^{\mathrm{maint}}_{i,r,t}
-U^{\mathrm{immune}}_{i,r,t}-U^{\mathrm{repro}}_{i,r,t}
-U^{\mathrm{output}}_{i,r,t}-L_{i,r,t},
$$

where every term is in a declared resource unit. $I$ is accepted intake,
$M^+$ and $M^-$ are storage and mobilization, $U$ terms are typed uses, and $L$
is loss. A service score may be linked downstream but cannot silently be added
to this conservation equation. The observation interval and conversion model
must be registered; unmeasured terms remain residuals rather than “efficiency.”

**Project boundary.** P-006 already covers negative feedback; P-009 covers
maintenance; Candidate 002 covers fast/slow context; Candidate 013 covers typed
deficits. Animal science adds a mandatory reserve-and-recovery ledger and
protected welfare outcomes. It does not add another principle.

### Collective, sentinel, and One-Health surveillance

Risk-based surveillance changes who is observed. Stärk et al. distinguish
hazard prioritization, selection of higher-risk strata, and risk-based sample
size, with efficiency evaluated relative to the surveillance objective
([doi:10.1186/1472-6963-6-20](https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6963-6-20)).
Drewe et al.'s systematic review found heterogeneous evaluation of 101 systems
across 23 attributes; comprehensive evaluation was uncommon
([doi:10.1017/S0950268811002160](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0950268811002160)).
Bordier et al. reviewed organisational and functional characteristics of 41
One-Health surveillance systems, showing that multi-sectoral coordination is a
system property with barriers, roles, and information paths, not merely pooled
data ([doi:10.1016/j.prevetmed.2018.10.005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.prevetmed.2018.10.005)).

The corrected EU One Health 2024 Zoonoses Report makes the observation process
concrete. It records programme and country differences, target populations,
food-chain stages, reporting rules, and comparability qualifications. It also
reports that Salmonella and Campylobacter checks by competent authorities were
more frequently positive than food-business operators' own checks in specified
carcass sampling. This is evidence that sampler and design belong in the data
generating process, not evidence that either source directly estimates a common
prevalence. The report was published in December 2025 and amended by editorial
correction on 17 March 2026
([doi:10.2903/j.efsa.2025.9759](https://doi.org/10.2903/j.efsa.2025.9759)).

For channel $k$, the minimum observation contract is

$$
Y_{k,t}\sim p_k\!\left(\cdot\mid X_{\mathcal U_k,t},
S_{k,t},D_{k,t},A_{0:t},Q_{k,t}\right),
$$

where $X_{\mathcal U_k,t}$ is latent state in the channel's eligible units,
$S$ is selection/inclusion, $D$ is diagnostic and case-definition version, $A$
is intervention history, and $Q$ is collection/report quality. The record also
contains target population, sampling frame, unit, pool membership, collection
and receipt times, result time, non-response, denominator, and reporting path.

**Project boundary.** Candidate 007 already asks whether acting changes hidden
state and future observations; Candidate 014 already requires versioned
population observations. Animal surveillance contributes exact stress tests:
risk-targeted sampling, pooled tests, farmer/operator versus authority channels,
movement between holdings, selective culling, delayed lab confirmation, and
cross-sector handoff. Ordinary design-based weighting, hierarchical/state-space
models, sequential detection, and maximum-coverage sentinel placement are
mandatory nulls.

### Host--pathogen coevolution and changing opponents

Read et al. experimentally infected vaccinated and unvaccinated chickens with
Marek's disease virus strains. Direct or maternal vaccination prolonged survival
without necessarily preventing infection, replication, or transmission, which
allowed otherwise too-lethal strains to transmit. The authors explicitly did
**not** establish that vaccination caused the historical evolution of
hyperpathogenic strains
([doi:10.1371/journal.pbio.1002198](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.1002198);
data [doi:10.5061/dryad.4tn48](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.4tn48)).

Decaestecker et al. recovered Daphnia host clones and Pasteuria parasite
isolates from dated pond-sediment layers, then cross-infected past,
contemporaneous, and future combinations. That time-shift design is much closer
to evidence of reciprocal dynamics than a present-day correlation
([doi:10.1038/nature06291](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature06291)).

For host or policy generation $g$ and pathogen/opponent generation $h$, retain
the full cross-play matrix

$$
Z_{g,h}=
\left(B_{g,h},H_{g,h},T_{g,h},C_{g,h}\right),
$$

with burden $B$, host harm $H$, onward transmission or opponent success $T$,
and lifecycle cost $C$. Resistance requires burden; tolerance requires harm
conditional on burden; transmission blocking requires an onward-transmission
endpoint. A diagonal $Z_{g,g}$ alone cannot distinguish general improvement,
cycling, escalation, or forgetting.

**AI translation.** Archived adversaries and time-shift cross-play are a
measurement contract for Candidate 019 and population-based training, not
license to claim that software populations “coevolve.” A fixed-opponent suite,
quality-diversity search, self-play, population-based training, adversarial
training, and explicit archive sampling form the null stack.

### Welfare and adverse-state measurement

Animal welfare is not the negative of production loss. EFSA's 2022 methodology
uses welfare consequences and fit-for-purpose animal-based measures with
explicit reference assumptions and uncertainty. The 2023 dairy-cow opinion
examines locomotory disorders, mastitis, movement and resting restriction,
inability to perform comfort behavior, and metabolic disorders, while noting
causal-evidence gaps across housing systems
([doi:10.2903/j.efsa.2022.7403](https://doi.org/10.2903/j.efsa.2022.7403),
[doi:10.2903/j.efsa.2023.7993](https://doi.org/10.2903/j.efsa.2023.7993)).

Primary experiments show two narrower measurement paths. Dairy cows learned to
push a weighted gate and worked at least as hard for pasture access as for fresh
feed under the study's test conditions
([doi:10.1038/srep44953](https://doi.org/10.1038/srep44953)). In a nine-rat
proof-of-principle, unpredictable housing altered responses to ambiguous cues in
the pessimistic direction
([doi:10.1038/427312a](https://doi.org/10.1038/427312a)). Neither task is a
complete welfare measure; both show why observed choice, imposed effort,
learning, deprivation, physical ability, and test horizon must be exposed.

The benchmark therefore protects a vector rather than collapsing welfare into
reward:

$$
W_{i,t}=\left(
H^{\mathrm{disease}}_{i,t},H^{\mathrm{injury}}_{i,t},
B^{\mathrm{behavior}}_{i,t},A^{\mathrm{affect}}_{i,t},
P^{\mathrm{preference}}_{i,t},S^{\mathrm{severe}}_{i,t},
R^{\mathrm{recovery}}_{i,t}
\right).
$$

Each component retains instrument, scale, direction, missingness, observer,
support, and validation population. No weighted sum is primary. Milk, growth,
feed conversion, latency, throughput, and survival are reported separately.
Animals or agents removed, dead, unavailable, or unable to perform a task stay
in the eligible denominator and attrition ledger.

**AI translation.** The defensible transfer is protected adverse-state and
preference measurement with tail reporting. It does not transfer animal moral
status, sentience, or husbandry law to software. For human-facing systems,
legitimate human authority and refusal still route to Candidate 020 rather than
being inferred from animal preference tests.

### Breeding, genetic diversity, and inbreeding control

Meuwissen derived optimum contribution selection: choose parental
contributions to maximize predicted genetic level while constraining average
coancestry, which determines expected next-generation inbreeding under the
model. The paper reported larger simulated gain than BLUP-EBV selection at
matched inbreeding rates but also noted reproductive constraints
([doi:10.2527/1997.754934x](https://doi.org/10.2527/1997.754934x)). This is a
strong conventional null, not a novel population principle.

With contribution vector $c$, predicted breeding values $\widehat g$, and
relationship matrix $A$, the minimal null is

$$
\max_c\;c^\top\widehat g
\quad\text{subject to}\quad
\mathbf 1^\top c=1,\;c\ge 0,\;
c^\top A c\le \kappa,
$$

plus sex, mating, offspring, feasibility, and protected-trait constraints. The
coancestry cap $\kappa$ is dimensionless under the declared relationship
estimator. Predicted gain does not replace descendant evaluation.

Makanjuola et al. analyzed pedigreed and genotyped records spanning 1990--2018
for 205,755 Holsteins and 89,238 Jerseys. Rates of inbreeding and coancestry and
effective population size differed by breed, estimator, and period; estimates
of effective size were not identical across methods. The result supports
multi-estimator monitoring and reproductive-contribution ledgers, not a simple
claim that genomic selection alone caused the trends
([doi:10.3168/jds.2019-18013](https://doi.org/10.3168/jds.2019-18013)).

**Project boundary.** P-004 and Candidate 019 already cover diversity,
selection, protected functions, turnover, and accessible lineages. The animal-
breeding residue is an unusually strong null and an exact rule: compare
capability gain at the same realized coancestry/inbreeding and reproductive
concentration, then test descendants after source access is removed. Raw agent
count and checkpoint diversity do not suffice.

### Rumen and microbial-consortium dynamics

The rumen is a host-conditioned, continuously fed and drained consortium. Its
useful transfer is a material-and-function accounting problem, not the metaphor
of “agents as microbes.”

Henderson et al. applied a standardized marker-gene pipeline to 742 samples from
32 ruminant or foregut-fermenter species/subspecies in 35 countries. Widely
shared dominant groups coexisted with diet-, host-, and region-associated
composition. The samples were largely small local cohorts and the evidence was
cross-sectional relative abundance
([doi:10.1038/srep14567](https://doi.org/10.1038/srep14567)).

Weimer et al. exchanged approximately 95% of ruminal contents within three
high-/low-efficiency cow pairs. Ruminal pH and volatile-fatty-acid profiles
returned toward recipient pre-exchange patterns within about one day; in five
of six cows the solids-associated bacterial community moved back toward the
host's pre-exchange community within ten days. Milk-production-efficiency
responses were transient and heterogeneous. The experiment is a striking host-
context perturbation but only six cows
([doi:10.3168/jds.2017-12746](https://doi.org/10.3168/jds.2017-12746)).

Shabat et al. linked metagenomic, activity, metabolite, and efficiency features
in extreme animals selected from 146 cows; this is rich association and
prediction, not random assignment of a consortium
([doi:10.1038/ismej.2016.62](https://doi.org/10.1038/ismej.2016.62)). A
randomized 3-nitrooxypropanol trial showed a sustained methane decrease without
negative milk-production effects in its measured trial, but the production
endpoint does not prove complete welfare or ecological safety
([doi:10.1073/pnas.1504124112](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1504124112)).

For taxon or functional group $j$,

$$
a_{j,t}=\frac{M_{j,t}}{\sum_\ell M_{\ell,t}},
$$

so relative abundance $a$ cannot determine absolute mass $M$. A valid
consortium record contains substrate input, dilution/passage, absolute load,
composition, expression or activity, product flux, gas flux, host uptake, host
outcome, and recovery. Quantitative microbiome profiling demonstrates in
another host system that variable total load can reverse interpretations based
on relative profiles
([doi:10.1038/nature24460](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature24460)); the
compositional-data boundary is synthesized in
[doi:10.3389/fmicb.2017.02224](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmicb.2017.02224).

**Project boundary.** P-008, P-009, and P-013 already cover compartments,
maintenance, and external state; the microbial audit already covers ecological
consortia. Rumen evidence adds host-conditioning, passage, fermentation-to-host
flux, methane, and perturbation recovery as null-model requirements. A
chemostat/compartment model, generalized Lotka--Volterra model, absolute-load
model, flux balance, and host random effects must be compared before crediting
an agent consortium.

### Intervention, interference, and identifiability limits

Dynamic animal models often contain biologically named parameters that fit the
same observations. Muñoz-Tamayo and Tedeschi show that structural
identifiability is a property of the model and ideal measurement design, while
practical identifiability additionally depends on real sampling and noise. They
argue for testing identifiability before data collection and provide worked
animal-nutrition examples
([doi:10.1093/jas/skad320](https://doi.org/10.1093/jas/skad320)).

In infectious populations, one animal's assignment changes another's exposure.
Halloran and Struchiner formalized causal inference with infectious
interference, motivating distinct direct, indirect, total, and overall effects
([doi:10.1097/00001648-199503000-00010](https://doi.org/10.1097/00001648-199503000-00010)).
For an exposure mapping $G_i(\mathbf A)$,

$$
Y_i\!\left(a_i,G_i(\mathbf A_{-i})\right)
$$

cannot generally be replaced by $Y_i(a_i)$.

Management adds informative treatment, testing, isolation, sale, culling,
death, and replacement. For a herd count,

$$
N_{t+1}=N_t+B_t+I_t-D_t-C_t-O_t,
$$

where births $B$, entries $I$, deaths $D$, culls $C$, and other exits $O$ are
typed counts. Every mean outcome reports both the start-of-period cohort and the
survivor cohort. “Recovery” is not credited when the adverse tail was removed
from observation.

**AI translation.** These are evaluation limits shared with ordinary causal
inference, POMDPs, dual control, missing-data models, and system identification.
They strengthen Candidate 007 and Candidate 014 but do not establish a new
intervention architecture.

## The transferable residue

The audit's residue can be represented as one versioned population-event
record:

$$
\mathcal R_{i,t}=
(u_i,h_i,g_i,o_i,x_{i,t},r_{i,t},w_{i,t},
s_{i,t},d_{i,t},a_{i,t},e_{i,t},z_{i,t},c_{i,t},v_t),
$$

where:

- $u_i$ is immutable unit identity and $h_i$ its holding/cohort;
- $g_i$ is genetic, model, or artifact lineage and contribution history;
- $o_i$ is pathogen/opponent lineage and exposure history;
- $x$ is latent/estimated individual state with uncertainty;
- $r$ is the typed resource ledger;
- $w$ is the protected welfare/adverse-state vector;
- $s$ is inclusion, missingness, pooling, and sampling support;
- $d$ is diagnostic, sensor, case-definition, and evaluator version;
- $a$ is proposed, assigned, received, and completed intervention;
- $e$ is movement, birth, death, cull, replacement, or other cohort event;
- $z$ is contemporaneous and archived-opponent outcome;
- $c$ is lifecycle resource and human-work cost; and
- $v_t$ is the schema and policy version.

This record is useful only if the simulator or deployment can populate it. A
field absent by design is `not_observed`, not zero. A pooled observation links
to every eligible member but does not allocate a positive. A lineage is not
credited with gain until descendants pass an independent test. An intervention
record distinguishes proposal, assignment, receipt, adherence/engagement,
biological or system response, and subsequent inclusion.

## Workstation falsification contract: `AVPH-POP-01`

### Question and unit of inference

Can the relevant existing candidate composition use the population-event
record to reduce hidden failure and improve task-native outcomes beyond a
complete conventional stack, without worsening any protected welfare component
or purchasing the result through extra selection, lineage concentration,
observation, or lifecycle cost?

The benchmark is synthetic and contains no live animal decision. The unit of
randomization and uncertainty is the **paired simulation seed**, not the event
row. All generators, policies, estimands, exclusions, and thresholds are frozen
before confirmatory runs.

### Fixed configurations

| Configuration | Replicates | Population and horizon | Purpose |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| smoke | 8 paired seeds | 16 holdings x 32 units x 96 daily steps; 8 breeding and opponent generations | schema, conservation, determinism, and metric tests on an ordinary workstation |
| development | 16 new paired seeds | 32 holdings x 48 units x 180 days; 12 generations | tuning and failure discovery only; never reported as confirmation |
| confirmatory | 40 untouched paired seeds | 64 holdings x 64 units x 240 days; 20 breeding generations and 20 opponent generations | locked comparison and confidence intervals |

Seeds are generated once from a committed manifest. Each arm receives identical
initial latent states, lineages, holding graph, diets/resources, pathogens,
weather/context, movements, diagnostic errors, report delays, evaluator drift,
and perturbation schedule. Event-level bootstrap is prohibited.

### Generator tracks

1. **Resource--welfare track.** Four resource types feed maintenance, immune
   response, reserve, reproduction, and output. Fast disturbances last 1--4
   steps; slow physiological phases last 30--90 steps. Hidden reserve debt can
   preserve output for 7--21 steps before increasing recovery time and adverse
   events. Output and every welfare component have different observation noise.
2. **Surveillance--cohort track.** Six observation channels differ in inclusion
   probability, sensitivity/specificity, pool size (1, 5, or 16), report delay
   (0, 2, or 7 steps), and actor (unit, operator, sentinel, laboratory,
   competent-authority analogue, environmental channel). Risk targeting raises
   case yield while distorting naive prevalence. Interventions change testing,
   movement, burden, and later inclusion; death/cull/exit remain explicit.
3. **Breeding--turnover track.** Each generation has 512 candidates, 128 parent
   slots, and 512 descendants. Eight protected rare capabilities begin at
   prevalence 0.03--0.10. Merit prediction includes drift and environment
   interaction. Contribution concentration changes coancestry, realized
   inbreeding, and later capability loss.
4. **Consortium track.** Each of 48 hosts contains 64 microbial guilds, four
   substrates, passage, pH-like constraint, six product fluxes, host uptake,
   methane-like loss, and absolute-load variation. Exchange, diet, inhibitor,
   and washout perturbations factorially separate composition, load, flux, and
   host context.
5. **Opponent track.** Twenty host-policy and pathogen/opponent generations are
   archived. Resistance, tolerance, transmission blocking, and harm are
   independently variable. Confirmatory evaluation uses all 400 generation
   pairs, never only the diagonal.

At least 25% of confirmatory holdings are drawn from held-out parameter regimes;
at least 25% of resource contexts and 25% of opponent lineages are unseen during
tuning. Holding, lineage, and opponent families, not event rows, define splits.

### Arms and strongest nulls

| Arm | Registered contents |
| --- | --- |
| `N0` scalar aggregate | one mean performance reward; retained only as a weak failure control |
| `N1` complete typed conventional stack | multiscale state-space/POMDP estimator; constrained model-predictive resource controller; design-weighted and hierarchical surveillance; CUSUM/GLR alarms; maximum-coverage sentinels; pooled-test likelihood; causal DAG with interference/exposure mapping; optimum contribution selection; absolute-load plus chemostat/gLV/flux consortium models; population-based/adversarial training with uniform archived cross-play; ordinary database, versioning, and audit logs |
| `N2` centralized full-information oracle | same algorithms with latent state exposed, to bound observation rather than architecture loss; not a deployable comparator |
| `C` candidate composition | only already-registered Candidates 002, 007, 013, 014, and 019, connected through the population-event record; no new biological module |
| `C-minus` ablations | remove in turn cohort events, protected welfare vector, contribution/coancestry, absolute load/flux, archived opponents, observation support, and lifecycle charging |

`C` cannot call an oracle unavailable to `N1`. If one record field supplies new
information, authority, labels, intervention opportunities, memory, or compute,
the corresponding null receives it too. If `N1` can consume the full record and
match `C`, the architecture claim fails while the record may remain an adopted
evaluation contract.

### Perturbations

The confirmatory manifest contains, in balanced Latin-square order:

- abrupt 40% resource restriction followed by refeeding;
- slow phase change that redirects a typed resource without changing total
  supply;
- a hidden reserve-mobilization policy that temporarily preserves output;
- diagnostic sensitivity drift and a case-definition version change;
- risk-targeted sampling with a 4x inclusion odds ratio;
- pools containing 0, 1, or multiple positives plus one mislabeled member;
- a seven-step laboratory/report delay and a response-capacity queue;
- intervention-induced testing and selective removal of the worst 5% of units;
- movement of 10% of units across holdings during an outbreak;
- high predicted-merit founders sharing one concentrated lineage;
- loss pressure on each rare capability and one schema migration at generation
  10;
- near-total consortium exchange, diet change, inhibitor, and washout;
- a tenfold absolute-load shift with unchanged relative composition;
- a relative-composition shift with unchanged total functional flux;
- a leaky intervention that reduces harm but not transmission; and
- archived opponents whose virulence, exploit, and observation policies cycle.

### Budget and lifecycle parity

Each arm receives identical environment interactions, intervention slots,
labels, tests, diagnostic draws, evaluator calls, and tuning seeds. Match or
charge:

$$
\mathcal C_{\mathrm{life}}=
(t_{\mathrm{cpu}},t_{\mathrm{gpu}},t_{\mathrm{wall}},
E_{\mathrm{elec}},B_{\mathrm{hot}},B_{\mathrm{cold}},
B_{\mathrm{network}},N_{\mathrm{sensor}},N_{\mathrm{lab}},
N_{\mathrm{intervention}},h_{\mathrm{operator}},h_{\mathrm{vet}},
h_{\mathrm{analyst}},h_{\mathrm{review}},h_{\mathrm{migration}}),
$$

with time in seconds, energy in joules at a declared meter boundary, storage
and network traffic in bytes, events as counts, and human roles in person-hours.
Include failed runs, preparation, calibration, annotation, pool resolution,
false-alert review, lineage computation, archive maintenance, schema migration,
appeal/override, retraining, and retirement through the longest horizon. Report
hardware, software, carbon-intensity source if emissions are estimated, and
idle/facility inclusion. Electricity and metabolic energy are not conflated.

### Confirmatory analysis and success gates

Use paired seed differences with two-sided 95% bootstrap confidence intervals
over the 40 seed-level summaries and Holm correction within each track. Also
report raw seed points and uncorrected estimates. A hierarchical model may be a
secondary analysis but cannot replace paired summaries. Missing outcomes are
reported by cause; no last-observation-carried-forward.

There is no scalar “animal-inspired” win. All gates are conjunctive:

1. Against `N1`, `C` must reduce normalized primary regret by at least 5%, with
   the corrected 95% interval excluding zero, on at least three tracks including
   surveillance and either breeding or opponent cross-play.
2. Every protected welfare component must meet a one-sided non-inferiority
   margin of 0.10 pooled baseline standard deviations; the upper 95% confidence
   bound for the severe-event risk ratio must be below 1.05. Start-cohort and
   worst-decile gates both apply.
3. Surveillance must reduce median detection-to-verified-response delay by at
   least 10% at the same false-alert ceiling and analyst minutes, while
   target-population 95% interval coverage remains 90--98%.
4. Breeding must improve independently evaluated descendant task value by at
   least 5% at no greater realized $\Delta F$ or mean coancestry, with no rare
   capability losing more than 5 percentage points relative to `N1`.
5. Consortium intervention estimates must retain 90--98% coverage for nominal
   95% intervals in held-out hosts and regimes; relative composition alone
   cannot satisfy the track.
6. Archived-opponent regret must improve by at least 5% over `N1` across past,
   contemporaneous, and future strata separately; a diagonal-only win fails.
7. `C` reports every lifecycle component. Any unprovided component is treated as
   unknown, not zero, and blocks an efficiency claim.

The numerical margins are benchmark decision rules, not biological welfare
thresholds, clinical standards, or legal limits.

### Failure and kill criteria

Retire the proposed cross-domain residue as an architecture claim if any of the
following occurs:

- `N1` matches `C` after receiving the same typed record;
- the gain disappears under holding-, lineage-, or opponent-family splits;
- means improve only because high-risk units die, are culled, exit, are not
  tested, or cannot complete the welfare task;
- productivity, feed efficiency, survival, or low alert count is substituted
  for any protected welfare component;
- risk targeting is evaluated by case yield without prevalence bias,
  denominator, false-alert, and workload measures;
- pooled positives are assigned to members without resolution evidence or
  correct abstention;
- breeding gain uses predicted merit without realized coancestry, inbreeding,
  contribution concentration, rare-function survival, and descendant tests;
- relative abundance is interpreted as absolute load, activity, flux, or host
  delivery;
- contemporaneous cross-play is reported as coevolution, or archived-opponent
  gains are obtained by extra evaluation budget;
- intervention effects are reported without own assignment, others' exposure,
  receipt/adherence, movement, selective removal, and observation change;
- structural non-identifiability remains in the registered generator/model or
  practical intervals cannot distinguish the claimed mechanisms;
- any welfare gate fails, or severe/adverse tails are hidden by a composite;
- a benefit requires undeclared veterinary/operator authority, extra labels,
  more interventions, longer access to source lineages, or uncharged human work;
  or
- energy, storage, network, laboratory, migration, or maintenance costs reverse
  the efficiency conclusion.

temporarily easier opponent.
