## Executive finding

Polymer research adds a useful discipline to this project: **the current state
is rarely summarized adequately by one average, one topology, one timescale, or
one endpoint**. A material with the same mean molar mass can have a different
tail and different processing behaviour; a material with the same instantaneous
modulus can have a different relaxation spectrum; a network with the same edge
count can have a different percolation margin; and two parts with the same final
temperature and pressure can retain different morphology because their paths
were different. Those are established field results, not evidence that a new AI
architecture works.

The transferable residue is operational:

1. preserve the distribution, spectrum, topology, support, or path variable
   that can change the decision;
2. expose which measurement operator made that state visible;
3. distinguish reversible exchange from irreversible damage and finite repair;
4. compare against population-balance, system-identification, graph,
   reliability, optimization, and lifecycle nulls; and
5. require a quality--risk--resource gain on held-out mechanisms, not a visual
   analogy to chains, gels, memory, self-assembly, or healing.

| Disposition | Result | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Promote a principle** | none | Distribution-aware state, multi-timescale memory, adaptive topology, feedback, maintenance, structural offloading, and versioned observation already have project owners. |
| **Promote a candidate** | none | Every admissible translation routes to Candidates 001, 003, 005, 006, 009, 011, 014, 017, 018, or 019. |

The strongest negative result is that polymer terminology earns no architectural
credit. A proposed system must beat the mature null on the same observations,
actions, compute budget, failure strata, and lifecycle boundary.

## Evidence and interpretation contract

### Evidence statuses

| Status | Meaning here |
| --- | --- |
| **Established** | demonstrated in a declared mathematical model, controlled experiment, or accepted measurement framework; scope remains attached to assumptions and conditions |
| **Plausible** | a causal systems translation is coherent and testable but has not beaten mature AI or engineering nulls |
| **Speculative** | a mechanism may become useful after missing state, measurement, or implementation assumptions are supplied |
| **Disputed** | an attractive overclaim is unsupported or contradicted by the evidence boundary |
| **Authoritative method** | law, guidance, or a standard defines obligations, terms, or test/reporting procedures; it does not establish empirical performance outside its scope |

### Minimum transfer tuple

Every proposed transfer is normalized to

```text
<source material/system; state distribution and scale; observation operator;
causal intervention; conserved or budgeted quantity; response timescale;
reversibility; failure/zero-support boundary; mature null; lifecycle boundary>
```

A transfer is rejected if it preserves only the noun. For example, a “vitrimer
network” transfer must specify which connections exchange, which capacity is
invariant, what drives exchange, how relaxation and rollback are measured, and
why ordinary graph rewiring is not sufficient. A “self-healing” transfer must
name the detected damage, repair inventory, recovered property, verification,
recurrence limit, and replacement path.

### Terminology firewall

- **Molar-mass average** is not a molar-mass distribution. Number-, weight-, and
  higher moments weight chains differently.
- **Dispersity** is not uncertainty. It is a property of a population under a
  declared molecular definition and measurement method.
- **Chain growth** and **step growth** describe reaction mechanisms, not “fast”
  and “slow” learning.
- **Living/controlled polymerization** is a scoped chemistry term. It does not
  mean zero termination, perfect sequence control, or an immortal process.
- **Entanglement** is a topological constraint on chain motion. It is not generic
  feature interaction or parameter coupling.
- **Relaxation memory** is dependence of stress or another response on prior
  deformation. It is not addressable episodic or semantic memory.
- **Time--temperature superposition** is conditional on thermo-rheological
  simplicity over a tested regime. It is not permission to extrapolate every
  timescale from a temperature shift.
- **Phase separation** minimizes or evolves under a physical free-energy and
  kinetic model. It does not identify task relevance.
- **Self-assembly** produces structures under interactions and boundaries. It
  does not imply learned planning, global optimality, defect freedom, or cheap
  reset.
- **Gel point** may denote connectivity or rheological criteria under a declared
  system. It is not a universal threshold for useful coordination.
- **Vitrimer** denotes a class of covalent adaptable networks with associative
  exchange. Reprocessability does not imply no creep, no catalyst, or no ageing.
- **Self-healing** is recovery of a measured property after a specified damage
  and protocol. Crack closure is not complete functional recovery.
- **Shape memory** is stimulus-triggered recovery toward a programmed shape. It
  is not autonomous recall of arbitrary information.
- **Physical ageing**, **chemical degradation**, **fatigue**, **creep**, and
  **wear** are different processes and cannot share one scalar “age.”
- **Sequence-controlled** permits degrees of control; **sequence-defined** is a
  stronger claim of exact chain length and monomer order.
- **Disintegration**, **biodegradation**, **compostability**, **reprocessing**,
  **recycling**, **recycled content**, and **lower lifecycle impact** are
  different claims.
- **A standard test result** is conditional on specimen, conditioning,
  apparatus, method, environment, and report. It is not a universal material
  constant.

## Mathematical contracts and dimensional checks

The equations below define synthetic fixtures and evidence boundaries. They are
not universal constitutive laws.

### Step-growth conversion and a distribution, not one chain

For ideal bifunctional step growth with equal functional-group reactivity,
stoichiometric balance, no cyclization, and irreversible reaction, the
number-average degree of polymerization is

$$
X_n=\frac{1}{1-p},
$$

where conversion $p$ and $X_n$ are dimensionless. Under the same idealization,
the number fraction of chains of length $x\in\{1,2,\ldots\}$ is

$$
P(X=x)=(1-p)p^{x-1},
$$

and the ideal dispersity approaches $1+p$. These statements trace to Flory's
distribution treatment
([doi:10.1021/ja01301a016](https://doi.org/10.1021/ja01301a016)).
Stoichiometric imbalance, unequal reactivity, reversibility, side reactions,
cyclization, branching, and fractionation invalidate the simple form.

For chain counts $N_i$ at molar masses $M_i$ in kg/mol,

$$
M_n=\frac{\sum_i N_iM_i}{\sum_iN_i},
\qquad
M_w=\frac{\sum_iN_iM_i^2}{\sum_iN_iM_i},
\qquad
\mathcal{D}=\frac{M_w}{M_n}.
$$

$M_n$ and $M_w$ are kg/mol and $\mathcal{D}$ is dimensionless. Equal $M_n$ does not imply
equal $M_w$, tail mass, architecture, rheology, or failure risk.

### Chain-growth rate is mechanism- and transport-qualified

For an ideal steady-state free-radical model with bimolecular termination,

$$
R_p=-\frac{d[M]}{dt}
=k_p[M]\sqrt{\frac{2f k_d[I]}{k_t}}.
$$

$R_p$ is mol/(m$^3$ s); monomer and initiator concentrations $[M]$ and $[I]$
are mol/m$^3$; $k_p$ and $k_t$ are m$^3$/(mol s); $k_d$ is s$^{-1}$; and
initiator efficiency $f$ is dimensionless. The expression does not cover
diffusion-controlled termination, compartmentalization, chain transfer,
autoacceleration, oxygen inhibition, heat/mass-transfer gradients, or a change
of mechanism. Controlled radical methods such as RAFT demonstrate additional
reversible exchange, not exemption from side reactions
([doi:10.1021/ma9804951](https://doi.org/10.1021/ma9804951)).

For binary terminal-model copolymerization,

$$
F_1=
\frac{r_1f_1^2+f_1f_2}
{r_1f_1^2+2f_1f_2+r_2f_2^2},
\qquad f_2=1-f_1,
$$

where feed fractions $f_i$, instantaneous polymer fraction $F_1$, and
reactivity ratios $r_i$ are dimensionless. The Mayo--Lewis equation is scoped
to its terminal-model assumptions and instantaneous feed
([doi:10.1021/ja01237a052](https://doi.org/10.1021/ja01237a052)); conversion
changes feed composition, and penultimate or more complex effects require a
different model.

### Linear viscoelastic memory and an inverse spectrum

In one-dimensional linear viscoelasticity,

$$
\sigma(t)=\int_{-\infty}^{t}G(t-s)\dot\gamma(s)\,ds,
\qquad
G(t)=G_\infty+\sum_{j=1}^{m}G_j e^{-t/\tau_j}.
$$

Stress $\sigma$ and relaxation moduli $G,G_j,G_\infty$ are Pa; strain $\gamma$
is dimensionless; $\dot\gamma$ is s$^{-1}$; $t,s,\tau_j$ are seconds. Each
Maxwell mode is state, but fitting many modes from a finite noisy frequency
window is ill-posed. Regularized recovery of relaxation spectra is an
established inverse problem
([doi:10.1021/ma00201a036](https://doi.org/10.1021/ma00201a036)).

For a discrete spectrum, oscillatory storage and loss moduli are

$$
G'(\omega)=G_\infty+
\sum_jG_j\frac{(\omega\tau_j)^2}{1+(\omega\tau_j)^2},
\qquad
G''(\omega)=
\sum_jG_j\frac{\omega\tau_j}{1+(\omega\tau_j)^2}.
$$

$G'$ and $G''$ are Pa, angular frequency $\omega$ is rad/s, and radian is
dimensionless. Similar measured moduli over a narrow band can correspond to
different out-of-band spectra.

### Temperature shift is a fitted regime relation

The Williams--Landel--Ferry relation is commonly written

$$
\log_{10}a_T=
-\frac{C_1(T-T_{\mathrm{ref}})}
{C_2+(T-T_{\mathrm{ref}})}.
$$

Shift factor $a_T$ and $C_1$ are dimensionless; $T$, $T_{\mathrm{ref}}$, and
$C_2$ must use the same temperature-difference unit, kelvin here. The 1955
paper established an empirical correlation for scoped amorphous materials
([doi:10.1021/ja01619a008](https://doi.org/10.1021/ja01619a008)). Published
parameter values are not universal constants. Multiple mechanisms, phase
change, ageing during measurement, moisture, crystallinity, or vertical shifts
can break simple superposition.

### Polymer mixing and conserved phase evolution

For an incompressible polymer--solvent lattice model, a dimensionless mixing
free energy per lattice site is

$$
\frac{\Delta g_{\mathrm{mix}}}{k_BT}
=\frac{\phi}{N}\ln\phi
+(1-\phi)\ln(1-\phi)
+\chi\phi(1-\phi),
$$

where polymer volume fraction $\phi$, degree of polymerization $N$, and
interaction parameter $\chi$ are dimensionless. The model captures a molecular-
size asymmetry under declared lattice and mean-field assumptions
([doi:10.1063/1.1723621](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1723621)); it does not make
$\chi$ universal or replace measured phase behaviour.

The workstation phase fixture uses the nondimensional conserved gradient flow

$$
\frac{\partial\phi}{\partial t}
=\nabla\cdot\left(M\nabla\mu\right),
\qquad
\mu=\frac{\partial f}{\partial\phi}-\kappa\nabla^2\phi,
$$

with dimensionless space, time, free-energy density $f$, mobility $M$, gradient
penalty $\kappa$, and chemical potential $\mu$. A physical implementation must
restore units for composition basis, length, time, chemical potential, and
mobility. Cahn and Hilliard's gradient-energy treatment supports the formalism
([doi:10.1063/1.1744102](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1744102)); it does not prove
that a software partition is a material phase.

### Ideal gel threshold and its failure boundary

For equal-functionality branching with functionality $f>2$, independent equal-
reactivity bonds, no intramolecular cycles, and an infinite mean-field system,
a simple branching criterion gives

$$
p_c(f-1)=1,
\qquad
p_c=\frac{1}{f-1}.
$$

Conversion $p_c$ and functionality $f$ are dimensionless. Flory and Stockmayer
derived scoped molecular-size and gelation results
([doi:10.1021/ja01856a061](https://doi.org/10.1021/ja01856a061),
[doi:10.1063/1.1723803](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1723803)). Loops, finite size,
spatial correlation, unequal functionality/reactivity, dilution, reversible
bonds, and bond lifetimes shift or blur the criterion. A rheological critical
gel has an additional frequency-domain signature
([doi:10.1122/1.549853](https://doi.org/10.1122/1.549853)); connectivity and
usable modulus must not be silently equated.

### Dynamic-network relaxation is not free rewiring

A scoped Arrhenius relaxation model is

$$
\tau(T)=\tau_0\exp\!\left(\frac{E_a}{RT}\right),
$$

where relaxation times $\tau,\tau_0$ are s, activation energy $E_a$ is J/mol,
gas constant $R$ is J/(mol K), and temperature $T$ is K. Montarnal et al.
demonstrated associative exchange and Arrhenius-like viscosity in an epoxy
network
([doi:10.1126/science.1212648](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1212648)).
The fitted law is chemistry- and regime-specific. Faster exchange can enable
stress relaxation and processing while worsening creep or dimensional
retention; catalyst, heat, oxidation, side reactions, and repeated-cycle
property changes remain costs.

### Damage history and lifecycle closure

A generic fatigue-crack-growth fixture uses

$$
\frac{da}{dN}=C(\Delta G)^q,
$$

where crack length $a$ is m, cycle count $N$ is cycles, energy-release-rate
range $\Delta G$ is J/m$^2$, exponent $q$ is dimensionless, and $C$ has units
m cycle$^{-1}$(J/m$^2$)$^{-q}$. It is a synthetic law, not a universal polymer
constant. Lake and Thomas tied rubber fracture to chain scission under scoped
conditions
([doi:10.1098/rspa.1967.0160](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.1967.0160)); real
fatigue depends on material, geometry, environment, rate, temperature, crack
population, and loading history.

Any circularity track must close mass:

$$
m_{\mathrm{in}}=
m_{\mathrm{product}}+m_{\mathrm{recovered}}+m_{\mathrm{waste}}
+m_{\mathrm{emission}}+\Delta m_{\mathrm{inventory}}.
$$

All terms are kg on one frozen system boundary and time interval. Recovery
yield, product quality, energy, reagents, water, emissions, additives,
contamination, and number of achieved service cycles remain separate outputs.

## Evidence synthesis by field mechanism

### Polymerization controls populations, not only conversion

Flory's 1936 result makes the first transfer condition exact: even under a
simple step-growth mechanism, high conversion produces a chain-length
distribution, not one representative chain. The sensitivity of $X_n$ near
$p=1$ also means a small conversion or stoichiometric error can have a large
effect on average length. The Mayo--Lewis terminal model adds composition drift:
the instantaneous polymer composition depends on feed and reactivity ratios, so
a batch can contain a sequence/composition gradient even when one final bulk
composition is reported.

RAFT demonstrates that reversible addition--fragmentation transfer can improve
control of molar mass and end groups across scoped monomers and conditions. It
does not erase termination, transfer, oxygen, heat, diffusion, impurity, scale,
or analytical uncertainty. The transferable systems operation is therefore
**population-state control under mechanism uncertainty**, not “living
learning.”

For AI or distributed systems, the admissible translation is narrow: when tail
members create congestion, calibration, safety, or memory cost, preserve enough
distribution state to make the intervention. Quantile sketches, population
balances, distributional RL/control, robust optimization, and mixture models are
the mandatory nulls. WS-POLY-01 tests whether a proposed versioned distribution
contract beats those nulls.

### Entanglement supplies constrained mobility and broad timescales

De Gennes modelled a chain moving among fixed obstacles by reptation and derived
scoped mass scalings
([doi:10.1063/1.1675789](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1675789)). Klein then reported
an experimental diffusion test designed around the predicted curvilinear motion
([doi:10.1038/271143a0](https://doi.org/10.1038/271143a0)). These sources support
topological restriction and slow renewal in entangled regimes. They do not make
one tube model exact for every molecular weight, architecture, concentration,
rate, or timescale.

The systems residue is a hostile case: migration may be constrained by existing
dependencies, locks, non-crossing routes, or expensive intermediate states, so
edge count and instantaneous throughput can hide long recovery. Graph
algorithms, constrained scheduling, queueing, database locking, and network
calculus already own the general problem. WS-POLY-04 and WS-POLY-05 test the
remaining topology-margin and capacity-preserving-reconfiguration compositions.

### A relaxation spectrum is a measurable memory contract

Linear viscoelasticity provides a precise example of history dependence: the
present stress is a convolution of prior strain rate with a kernel. The useful
lesson is not “polymers remember.” It is that a response can need multiple
retention times and that those times are only identifiable over the excited and
observed frequency window. Honerkamp and Weese explicitly treated relaxation-
spectrum recovery as a regularized inverse problem.

Time--temperature shifting can extend an empirical window when one shift factor
aligns the same mechanism. It can also manufacture a persuasive but invalid
master curve when mechanisms, structure, or ageing change. DIN EN ISO
6721-1:2019-09 limits its general dynamic-mechanical principles to the linear
viscoelastic region and warns that deformation modes need not be directly
comparable.

The AI translation is therefore a tested memory kernel with read bandwidth,
uncertainty, overwrite/recovery, and state cost. Exponential moving averages,
state-space models, Prony and fractional models, temporal convolutions, and
Gaussian processes are the null stack. WS-POLY-02 requires held-out spectrum and
superposition-breakdown performance beyond that stack.

### Phase separation and self-assembly expose path and defect costs

Flory--Huggins theory supplies a scoped thermodynamic model for size-asymmetric
mixing. Cahn--Hilliard dynamics supplies conserved diffuse-interface evolution.
Leibler's block-copolymer theory and Matsen and Schick's self-consistent field
work show how architecture, composition, and interaction parameters constrain
possible microphases
([doi:10.1021/ma60078a047](https://doi.org/10.1021/ma60078a047),
[doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.72.2660](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.72.2660)).

None establishes fast or defect-free assembly. Majewski and Yager observed
path-dependent reordering and transient states in cylinder-forming films
([doi:10.1039/C5SM02441B](https://doi.org/10.1039/C5SM02441B)). Doise et al.
showed that defect-annihilation kinetics constrain directed assembly in a
specific high-$\chi$ system
([doi:10.1021/acsami.9b17858](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsami.9b17858)). The
equilibrium target, boundary pattern, annealing protocol, mobility, and defect
path all matter.

The transfer rule is strict: a local energy descent or emergent cluster may be a
useful optimizer only if its objective matches the task, its defects and reset
are counted, and it beats graph partitioning, annealing, clustering, and
distributed control. WS-POLY-03 deliberately separates free-energy reduction
from task utility and includes kinetic traps.

### Gelation separates connectivity, mechanics, and usefulness

Flory--Stockmayer theory connects local reaction probability to system-spanning
network formation under mean-field assumptions. Winter and Chambon add a
rheological criterion at a critical gel point. A giant connected component, an
infinite-network idealization, a measurable modulus, and a useful load-bearing
network are not synonyms.

For AI topology, the admissible residue is that local edge statistics can hide
global bridge failure and that topology can change sharply near a connectivity
boundary. Percolation theory, k-connectivity, reliability polynomials, spectral
gaps, graph sparsification, and explicit bridge/articulation monitoring are the
nulls. WS-POLY-04 uses loops, spatial correlation, unequal edge lifetimes, and
finite-size strata specifically to defeat a borrowed universal threshold.

### Vitrimers make a capacity-preserving tradeoff testable

Montarnal et al. designed a permanently crosslinked epoxy network whose topology
could rearrange through exchange reactions without depolymerization. This is a
genuine source operation: connection exchange while a covalent network remains.
It still requires chemistry, temperature, time, and a regime in which exchange
occurs. Reprocessability and welding are accompanied by relaxation, possible
creep, catalyst and thermal exposure, and repeated-cycle questions.

The closest software translation is graph rewiring under an invariant node/edge
or parameter budget. That is already Candidate 001 plus mature online graph
optimization. It becomes Candidate 006 only if a real physical substrate removes
a measured sensing--compute--actuation path. WS-POLY-05 can reject a bad logical
composition; it cannot prove material energy or lifetime.

### Healing and stimulus response are typed local interventions

White et al. demonstrated crack-triggered microcapsule rupture, agent flow, and
polymerization in a composite
([doi:10.1038/35057232](https://doi.org/10.1038/35057232)). Toohey et al. used a
microvascular network to address the single-use locality of capsules
([doi:10.1038/nmat1934](https://doi.org/10.1038/nmat1934)). Cordier et al.
demonstrated repeatable repair in a supramolecular rubber under stated contact,
time, and temperature conditions
([doi:10.1038/nature06669](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature06669)). These are
different repair mechanisms with different inventories and recovered
properties.

Stimulus response likewise has a physical state and envelope. Tanaka observed
collapse of polyacrylamide gels after changing temperature or fluid composition
and interpreted the scoped transition with a gel free-energy model
([doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.40.820](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.40.820)).
Lendlein and Langer demonstrated a biodegradable thermally actuated shape-memory
polymer system
([doi:10.1126/science.1066102](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1066102)). The
gel state or shape is programmed by chemistry and history, and a stimulus
triggers a material transition; this is not arbitrary content recall or
unpowered control.

The transfer is a typed local repair or mode switch with verified postcondition,
recurrence capacity, and fallback. Redundancy, checkpoint/restart, analog
thresholds, condition-based maintenance, replacement, and conventional
actuation are nulls. WS-POLY-06 tests repeated finite repair; WS-POLY-11 tests
whether hysteresis helps after drift, actuation, and false-switch costs.

### Damage and ageing make history a latent state

Polymer damage can accumulate through crack growth, chain scission, oxidation,
hydrolysis, plasticization, creep, and physical ageing. The Lake--Thomas result
supports a molecular fracture-energy model for a scoped rubber system, not one
universal fatigue law. Lake and Lindley's rubber experiments separately scoped
a mechanical fatigue limit
([doi:10.1002/app.1965.070090405](https://doi.org/10.1002/app.1965.070090405)).
Cavaillé et al. measured dynamic-shear ageing and a memory effect in specified
polymer-glass blends
([doi:10.1016/0032-3861(86)90125-4](https://doi.org/10.1016/0032-3861(86)90125-4)).
Neither result supplies a universal damage state. A current modulus or
validation score can remain acceptable while crack population, physical-ageing
state, remaining repair inventory, or future relaxation differs.

The systems residue is exactly Candidate 003/011/014: estimate hidden health
from complete operation history and bounded probes, distinguish transition
classes, and abstain where no precursor is observable. Survival models,
state-space damage models, changepoint detection, canaries, and scheduled
replacement are the nulls. WS-POLY-07 includes histories with identical current
telemetry and different remaining life.

### Sequence control separates exact order from composition

The sequence-controlled-polymer literature distinguishes degrees of sequence
control from exact sequence definition
([doi:10.1126/science.1238149](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1238149)).
Martens et al. demonstrated encoding and reconstructing text and QR-code data in
collections of sequence-defined oligomers with tandem mass-spectrometric readout
and decoding software
([doi:10.1038/s41467-018-06926-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-06926-3)).
That proof does not establish superior density, latency, energy, error rate,
retention, or cost against electronic or other molecular storage.

The software transfer is not “use polymers as weights.” It is a versioned
sequence, codebook, decoder, error model, addressing scheme, and reconstruction
contract. Error-correcting codes, content-addressed objects, databases, and
ordinary archives are the nulls. WS-POLY-08 tests sequence order under insertion,
deletion, substitution, mixture, and index errors.

### Recycling and degradation require separate ledgers

Laboratory demonstrations show several distinct routes. Christensen et al.
recovered monomers from poly(diketoenamine) networks in scoped mixed formulations
([doi:10.1038/s41557-019-0249-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41557-019-0249-2)).
Tournier et al. engineered an enzyme for PET depolymerization and demonstrated
repolymerized PET in the studied process
([doi:10.1038/s41586-020-2149-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2149-4)).
Häußler et al. introduced cleavable groups into polyethylene-like materials and
demonstrated solvolytic monomer recovery in scoped waste mixtures
([doi:10.1038/s41586-020-03149-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-03149-9)).

These sources prove routes and specimen/process outcomes, not universal closed
loops. Feed preparation, collection, sorting, contamination, additives,
reaction/separation energy, solvent/catalyst/enzyme production, yield, product
quality, emissions, repeated cycles, infrastructure, and displacement of virgin
production remain in the boundary.

The transfer is a typed component/artifact recovery problem with mass, quality,
lineage, and lifecycle accounting. Material-flow analysis, LCA, chain of
custody, and mixed-integer planning are the nulls. WS-POLY-09 rejects any policy
whose gain comes from calling downgraded, exported, stored, or unmeasured mass a
closed loop.

### Processing history and measurement operators cannot be removed

Flow can alter polymer crystallization, morphology, and final properties.
Roozemond et al. reported a specific self-regulating structure-formation regime
under high-rate in-situ measurements
([doi:10.1002/marc.201400505](https://doi.org/10.1002/marc.201400505)). The
result is not a universal claim that deformation history is irrelevant; it is a
bounded observation that itself depends on material, shear layer, temperature,
rate, and measurement support.

SEC estimates molar-mass distributions through a method- and calibration-
specific size separation; dynamic mechanical analysis observes a frequency and
deformation-mode window; DSC reports thermal transitions under a defined sample
and scan; scattering and microscopy observe different spatial averages. The
latent structure is not “the data.” It is inferred through operators.

The admissible transfer is a versioned process/observation contract that retains
history only when it changes prediction or intervention, and abstains outside
support. Process historians, state-space/RNN models, forward simulators,
Tikhonov/NNLS/Bayesian inversion, calibration, and multimodal data fusion are the
nulls. WS-POLY-10 tests inverse support; WS-POLY-12 tests whether endpoint-only
records fail under path-equivalent observations.

## CPU-only workstation falsification package

### Common execution contract

All generators are synthetic and deterministic from committed seeds. They use
dimensionless or explicitly unit-labelled variables and never claim a real
polymer result. Each track runs without a GPU, network service, proprietary
solver, or closed dataset.

- **Pilot:** 8 paired seeds, at most 2 CPU-hours, 8 GiB peak RSS, and 5 GiB
  combined durable writes per track.
- **Confirmation:** 32 fresh paired seeds fixed before pilot inspection, at most
  16 CPU-hours, 16 GiB peak RSS, and 25 GiB durable writes per track.
- **Parallelism:** at most 8 logical CPU threads; record CPU model, core/thread
  count, OS, package lock, compiler/runtime, seed, and configuration hashes.
- **Splits:** development, calibration, hostile validation, and sealed
  confirmation are generated from disjoint mechanism families, not random rows
  from one trajectory.
- **Pairing:** every arm sees the same latent state, disturbances, observation
  operator, and cost schedule for a seed.
- **Tuning:** equal wall/CPU or equal number of evaluated configurations; oracle
  state is an ineligible ceiling.
- **Outputs:** raw per-seed metrics, denominators, failures, confidence intervals,
  and resource vectors; no single composite score may hide a failed safety,
  support, mass-closure, or quality gate.

Every arm records CPU time [s], wall time [s], peak RSS [bytes], bytes read and
written, durable bytes, model/state bytes, p50/p95/p99 latency [s], prescribed
human setup/review [person-s], failed/retried runs, and accepted task outputs.
Whole-system energy [J] may be reported only with an external calibrated meter,
idle baseline, integration window, and uncertainty. Software counters remain
telemetry, not evidence of joules.

For arm $a$, retain

$$
\mathbf C_a=(Q_a,R_a,L_a,T^{\mathrm{cpu}}_a,T^{\mathrm{wall}}_a,
M_a,B^{\mathrm{read}}_a,B^{\mathrm{write}}_a,S_a,H_a,E_a),
$$

where $Q_a$ is the native task-quality vector; $R_a$ is risk/error with counts
and denominators; $L_a$ is latency [s]; $T$ is time [s]; $M$ and $B$ are bytes;
$S_a$ is durable state [bytes and artifact count]; $H_a$ is person-s; and $E_a$
is measured J or `not measured`.

### WS-POLY-01 — distribution-aware reaction and routing state

**Generator.** Simulate 100,000 chains per seed under three development
families: ideal step growth, ideal radical chain growth, and binary terminal-
model copolymerization. Confirmation withholds four mechanism changes:
stoichiometric imbalance, chain transfer, diffusion-limited termination, and a
penultimate composition effect. Create 24 time blocks with conversion,
temperature, two noisy aggregate sensors, and delayed SEC-like histograms.
Inject pairs with equal $M_n$ but different $M_w$, upper 0.1% tail, and sequence
run-length distribution. The control action chooses feed, stop time, or route to
one of three downstream capacity classes.

**Arms.** Conversion-only PID; mean-$M_n$ controller; first-four-moment state;
64-bin population balance with MPC; quantile sketch plus robust control;
particle population filter; Candidate 014 distribution/operator record composed
with the best controller. Give all arms the same delayed observations and action
limits.

**Splits.** Hold out the four mechanism changes, two molar-mass calibration
curves, the highest-viscosity stratum, and multimodal chain populations.

**Metrics.** Conversion error; $M_n$ and $M_w$ error [kg/mol]; $W_1$ distance
[kg/mol]; tail-mass error [kg/kg]; sequence-run error [monomer units]; downstream
overflow count; off-spec mass [kg in a declared synthetic batch]; abstention;
CPU, memory, state, and latency.

**Hostile tests.** Same mean/different tail; censored detector range; shifted
calibration; delayed histogram; bimodal population; mechanism switch; sensor
failure; action saturation.

**Success gate.** No higher overflow or off-spec mass than the best strong null;
at least 20% lower median tail-mass error and 10% lower $W_1$ error on sealed
mechanism families; nominal 95% interval coverage between 92.5% and 97.5%; and a
strict quality/resource Pareto gain after observation and state costs.

**Kill.** Retire the residue if a fixed histogram/population-balance controller
matches it, if the gain exists only in $M_n$, if confirmation mechanism labels
leak, or if unobserved tails receive confident estimates.

### WS-POLY-02 — relaxation-spectrum memory under bandwidth limits

**Generator.** Generate 20,000 strain histories per seed and causal responses

Candidate 002/017 composition with explicit support and retained-kernel contract.

**Metrics.** Stress MAE [Pa in the labelled synthetic scale]; supported and

mode count; state bytes; update latency; CPU; out-of-support abstention; error

**Hostile tests.** Narrow-band aliasing; duplicate modes; missing low frequency;

**Success gate.** Noninferior supported-window error to the best state-space or

**Kill.** Retire if a fixed logarithmic EMA bank or ordinary state-space model

## Confirmatory decision contract

The independent unit is a complete generated process/network/material episode,
not a chain, edge, grid cell, frequency point, damage cycle, or instrument bin.
Seeds sharing a latent mechanism, calibration root, graph family, or lifecycle
inventory are clustered in analysis.

For each track:

1. freeze generator, hashes, seed list, arms, tuning budget, outcomes, thresholds,
   and kill rules before confirmation;
2. select at most one primary mature comparator on pilot data;
3. use paired per-seed contrasts and report percentile/bootstrap intervals over
   independent seed clusters;
4. control the twelve primary superiority tests with Holm family-wise correction
   at 0.05, while safety/support/mass-closure gates remain non-compensable;
5. require the gate order: validity and support, protected risk, task quality,
   then resource superiority; and
6. publish failures, reruns, exclusions, and null results.

No track may promote a principle merely by passing. Passing permits a more
realistic implementation or physical experiment. The material/energy claims of
Candidates 006 and 009 require real devices, calibrated meters, specimen
populations, environmental conditioning, ageing, failure analysis, and complete
lifecycle inventories.

## Cross-fixture failure modes

Reject any claimed gain that depends on:

1. replacing a distribution with a mean after tails change the decision;
2. treating dispersity as epistemic uncertainty;
3. applying chain-, step-, or copolymer kinetics outside their assumptions;
4. inferring a high-resolution relaxation spectrum from an unobserved band;
5. treating WLF coefficients or percolation thresholds as universal constants;
6. calling lower free energy higher task utility;
7. omitting kinetic traps, defects, annealing, reset, or path history;
8. equating connectivity with mechanical or service capability;
9. calling edge-count preservation capability preservation;
10. omitting catalyst, temperature, exchange time, creep, wear, or ageing from
    dynamic-network accounting;
11. scoring crack closure as recovery of every material/system property;
12. assuming infinite repair agent or repeated same-location healing;
13. presenting stimulus response as autonomous decision or free actuation;
14. inferring remaining life from one current performance snapshot;
15. calling composition an exact sequence or omitting codebook/index/decoder;
16. calling disintegration biodegradation, compostability, or environmental
    safety;
17. calling collected, exported, stored, downcycled, or off-spec mass a closed
    loop;
18. reporting recycled content as lifecycle benefit without a functional unit;
19. using one laboratory recovery yield for an untested feed and scale;
20. combining dependent instruments as independent evidence;
21. reconstructing a zero-support latent component without external information;
22. retaining full raw history while charging no storage, invalidation, privacy,
    or review cost;
23. using confirmation truth, mechanism labels, or future failure in
    construction; or
24. converting synthetic CPU counters into physical joules or EU compliance.

## Proposed central claims

The root integrator may assign the next available central IDs. These fifteen
claims are the maximum justified promotion set; the local propositions above do
not all need central entries.

1. **Polymer populations require distribution-qualified claims.** Number-
   average, weight-average, dispersity, tails, architecture, composition, and
   sequence can support different decisions; one average is sufficient only
   after a task-specific sufficiency test. Sources: `Flory1936Distribution`,
   `MayoLewis1944`.
2. **Polymerization equations are mechanism contracts.** Step-growth,
   free-radical, controlled-radical, and copolymer models carry different active
   states, side reactions, and failure regimes; their parameters cannot be
   transferred after a mechanism or transport change without validation.
   Sources: `Flory1936Distribution`, `MayoLewis1944`, `Chiefari1998RAFT`.
3. **Entanglement is a topological mobility constraint.** Reptation theory and
   scoped experiment support constrained chain motion and long renewal scales,
   not a universal scaling law for every polymer system. Sources:
   `DeGennes1971Reptation`, `Klein1978ReptationEvidence`.
4. **Viscoelastic memory is spectrum- and bandwidth-qualified.** A causal
   relaxation kernel can represent physical history, but finite noisy frequency
   support makes detailed spectrum recovery regularization-dependent. Sources:
   `WilliamsLandelFerry1955`, `HonerkampWeese1989`.
5. **Time--temperature superposition is conditional.** Shift factors and WLF
   parameters are fitted within a tested thermo-rheological regime and do not
   license universal extrapolation. Source: `WilliamsLandelFerry1955`.
6. **Polymer phase and self-assembly outcomes are path- and defect-qualified.**
   Equilibrium models constrain possible phases, while boundaries, kinetics,
   annealing, and barriers determine whether and how a low-defect morphology is
   reached. Sources: `Flory1942Solution`, `CahnHilliard1958`,
   `Leibler1980Block`, `MatsenSchick1994`, `MajewskiYager2016`,
   `DoiseEtAl2019`.
7. **Gel criteria must name the criterion and assumptions.** Mean-field
   connectivity, rheological criticality, finite-system spanning, and useful
   service are distinct outcomes affected by loops, correlation, functionality,
   bond lifetime, and observation. Sources: `Flory1941Gel`,
   `Stockmayer1943Gel`, `WinterChambon1986`.
8. **Dynamic covalent exchange is a tradeoff, not free plasticity.** A network
   can exchange topology while retaining a covalent network, but temperature,
   catalyst, relaxation, creep, side reactions, ageing, and reprocessing outcome
   remain in the contract. Source: `MontarnalEtAl2011Vitrimer`.
9. **Healing claims are property-, damage-, inventory-, and recurrence-
   qualified.** Capsule, vascular, and reversible-association systems provide
   different repair access and repeatability; recovered geometry or one
   mechanical metric does not establish complete restored function. Sources:
   `white2001autonomic`, `toohey2007microvascular`,
   `cordier2008rubber`.
10. **Stimulus response is not arbitrary memory.** Shape-memory polymers recover
    a programmed response under a stimulus and declared cycle/protocol; input,
    actuation, latency, drift, and reset remain costs. Sources:
    `Tanaka1978GelCollapse`, `LendleinLanger2002ShapeMemory`.
11. **Current performance need not identify remaining life.** Polymer fracture,
    fatigue, creep, ageing, and repair histories can produce equal current
    observations with different future response, requiring history/probe and
    transition-class qualification. Sources: `LakeThomas1967`,
    `LakeLindley1965Fatigue`, `CavailleEtAl1986Ageing`, plus the explicit
    synthetic test contract.
12. **Sequence-defined storage requires the complete write--address--read--decode
    boundary.** Exact monomer order can encode data, but synthesis/read errors,
    indexing, codebook, mixtures, latency, and lifecycle determine system value.
    Sources: `LutzEtAl2013Sequence`, `MartensEtAl2018Storage`.
13. **Polymer circularity claims require separate mass, quality, lineage, and
    lifecycle ledgers.** Laboratory depolymerization or monomer recovery proves a
    route under tested feed/process conditions, not universal closed-loop impact.
    Sources: `ChristensenEtAl2019PDK`, `TournierEtAl2020PET`,
    `HausslerEtAl2021ClosedLoop`, EU and DIN/EN/ISO records below.
14. **Polymer measurements are operator-qualified.** SEC, DMA, DSC, scattering,
    microscopy, spectroscopy, and mechanical tests observe different supports;
    calibration, specimen, deformation mode, scan/path, and shared roots remain
    inference dependencies. Sources: `ISO16014_1_2019`, `DIN6721_1_2019`,
    `DIN11357_1_2023`, `DIN527_1_2019`, `HonerkampWeese1989`.
15. **The proposed polymer-state contract remains hypothetical.** Carrying only
    decision-relevant distribution, spectrum, topology, path, repair, operator,
    and lifecycle state may reduce invalid interventions beyond mature nulls.
    Evidence test: WS-POLY-01 through WS-POLY-12; no polymer source establishes
    the AI composition.

## Audit decision

1. **Classify DFG 3.16 as dedicated, bounded coverage.** All three current
   constituent fields are directly addressed, while the nine gaps above remain
   visible.
2. **Add no principle and no candidate.** The residue is distribution-, spectrum-,
   topology-, path-, repair-, operator-, and lifecycle-qualified testing.
3. **Route primarily to Candidate 014** for observation/operator/path contracts,
   composed with Candidate 001 for graph change, Candidate 003 for transition
   and recovery dynamics, Candidates 005/011 for repair, Candidate 006 for any
   later physical implementation, Candidate 009 for versioned assurance, and
   Candidates 017/018/019 for compact artifacts, reconstructability, and
   inheritance.
4. **Promote at most the fifteen scoped central claims.** The fifteenth remains
   explicitly hypothetical until the workstation package beats mature nulls.
5. **Use WS-POLY-01 through WS-POLY-12 as the deliverable.** They can falsify bad
   transfers on a workstation. They cannot establish real polymer, energy,
   safety, recycling, biodegradation, or compliance performance.
