# Open questions

Questions stay here until evidence resolves them or a decision record fixes a
project choice.

## Architecture

- **OQ-001:** What unit should routing select: token, modality segment, spatial
  region, temporal event, task, or a hierarchy of these?
- **OQ-002:** How can expert specialization emerge without router collapse or
  permanent modality silos?
- **OQ-003:** Which state belongs in fast weights, an episodic store, external
  factual memory, or the slow model?
- **OQ-004:** Can a shared latent space retain modality-specific uncertainty
  instead of flattening incompatible signals?

## Learning

- **OQ-005:** What observable criterion indicates consolidation rather than
  memorization?
- **OQ-006:** Is explicit offline replay necessary, or can background and
  interleaved consolidation provide the same stability?
- **OQ-007:** Which continual-learning mechanism scales without freezing useful
  capacity or requiring full historical data?
- **OQ-008:** Can pruning be scheduled from causal contribution and routing
  stability rather than magnitude alone?

## Measurement

- **OQ-009:** What task family can compare dense and conditional systems while
  holding capability and quality constant?
- **OQ-010:** Which energy boundary is the first target: accelerator, node,
  cluster, or facility?
- **OQ-011:** How should the project price latency tails, calibration, and rare
  failure alongside average energy?
- **OQ-012:** Is any defensible operation-level comparison between brains and
  digital models possible, or should comparisons remain functional only?
